02 · Agenda question two

Smart efficiency, or customizable private systems?

Answer
Both, in different places. “Private, customizable, Arabic-first” is the headline; efficiency is the number we commit to in every pilot.

Every vendor claims efficiency, so it does not differentiate. Privacy does, and in government it is often the entry ticket: data residency rules, security reviews and the ban on public AI tools stop most offers before the business case is heard. Once we are through that door, the budget owner needs a measured result: days saved, rework removed, cost per request.

Private AI systems for UAE institutions — built around your work, running inside your walls, fluent in Arabic.

Why now

On 23 April 2026 the UAE Cabinet set a goal of running 50% of federal government services and operations on agentic AI within two years, and approved training for 80,000 federal employees. Every entity now needs delivery partners.

Our edge

Emirati-founded, Abu Dhabi-based, Arabic-first, model-agnostic (Qwen, Gemma, Falcon, Jais), human approval built into every action.

Not our fight

We do not sell GPU capacity or cloud. We partner with whoever hosts the infrastructure and own the layer that does the work.

04 · What we will not do

Red lines

  • We do not train our own foundation model. We deploy and adapt open-weight models (Qwen, Gemma, Falcon, Jais). Training from scratch costs millions and no client is asking for it.
  • Client data does not leave the client or the UAE. No public AI APIs on client data, ever.
  • AI never decides about people. Eligibility, hiring, penalties and payments are recommended and evidenced; a named official approves.
  • No accuracy promises before testing on the client's data. We commit to measures after a baseline, and we show the evaluation.
  • No hardware resale, no 24/7 production SLAs, no CCTV or surveillance analytics until we have the team for it.
  • No more than one free pilot, and never without written success criteria and an agreed next phase.
