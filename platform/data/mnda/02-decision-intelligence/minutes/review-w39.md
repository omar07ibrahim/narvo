# Director-General weekly performance review — week 39

**Date:** Monday 28 September 2026, 09:00–10:35 · **Venue:** DG meeting room, Level 21, MNDA Tower
**Chair:** H.E. Eng. Khalid Saif Al Mazrouei, Director General
**Present:** Dr. Amna Rashid Al Ketbi (secretary), Fatima Obaid Al Nuaimi, Eng. Omar Yousef Al Hammadi, Sara Ahmed Al Blooshi, Eng. Hamad Juma Al Kaabi, Vikram Menon, Eng. Tariq Mahmoud Saleh, Maryam Hassan Al Shamsi, Rashed Ali Al Dhaheri, Hessa Abdulla Al Suwaidi
**Next review:** week 40 review scheduled for Monday 5 October (subsequently postponed — DG on official visit; weeks 40 and 41 to be reviewed together on Monday 12 October).

## 1. Housing (Circular 9/2026)

The DG noted that two different backlog figures had been presented in week 38 (Housing and the CHC). Open cases on 27 September stood at 1,033 per HCMS. The surge in applications is not expected to ease before the end of October. Incomplete files remain the main driver of rework.

- **Decision:** fast-track the oldest complete files and report progress weekly → **ACT-2026-049** (Fatima Al Nuaimi, 9 Oct).
- **Decision:** salary-certificate e-service for staff applicants → **ACT-2026-052** (Maryam Al Shamsi, 15 Oct).
- The DG asked the Strategy & Performance Office to reconcile department figures with the scorecard before each review → **ACT-2026-053** (Dr. Amna Al Ketbi, 12 Oct).

## 2. Customer Happiness Centre

Wait times and abandonment are driven by housing status calls. The DG approved Saturday opening for October → **ACT-2026-048** (Sara Al Blooshi, 3 Oct).

## 3. Building permits (Circular 14/2026)

Eng. Omar presented the intake pilot starting 1 October (NOC validity check at intake, automatic NOC request at submission). The DG asked for a measured comparison against the Q3 baseline before the pilot is made standard. Lulu Island Developments PJSC has still not signed the NOC turnaround note (ACT-2026-032 — open).

- **Decision:** consultants' forum on Circular 14/2026 → **ACT-2026-054** (Eng. Omar Al Hammadi, 14 Oct).
- **Decision:** present the Senior Planning Engineer shortlist → **ACT-2026-050** (Maryam Al Shamsi, 8 Oct).

## 4. Facilities — Al Ghaf Community Centre

Repeated comfort complaints in the Multipurpose Hall and Ladies' Fitness. The root-cause report requested from Raseen FM (ACT-2026-043) is overdue. The DG asked that the renewal decision for contract MNDA/CON/2023/019 take the contractor's handling of this issue into account → **ACT-2026-051** (Rashed Al Dhaheri, 20 Oct).

## 5. Finance

Budget execution is below the phased plan, mainly in capex. Re-phasing proposal remains due (ACT-2026-047, 5 Oct).

## 6. Open actions

Of the actions due by this review, fewer than half were closed on time. The DG asked owners to update the register before each review and to propose a realistic date rather than leave items overdue.
