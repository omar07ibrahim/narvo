**Intake pilot — first read.** Since 1 October we received **114 applications**; only **9 were returned to the applicant (7.9%)**, against 27% in Q3. Counter staff report that most NOC problems are now caught before acceptance — typically a 20-minute conversation instead of a full return cycle.

- The average NOC hold for October submissions is **5.9 days** so far, but only a handful of NOCs have been received; we should not read too much into it yet.
- We issued **71 permits** this week (Monday to Friday).
- Lulu Island Developments PJSC has agreed in principle to the 7-working-day NOC turnaround but has not signed the service note (ACT-2026-032 remains open).

**Decision requested.** Approve rolling the intake check and the automatic NOC request into the standard procedure from 1 November, with a formal measurement against the Q3 baseline. A change memo will follow from the process team.
