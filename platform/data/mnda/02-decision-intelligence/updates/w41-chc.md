**This week.** **6,500 contacts** Monday to Friday. Thanks to the extra agents on the Saturday shift, the **phone wait is holding at 5:10**, although abandonment reached **17.2%** for the week, with the worst afternoons on Tuesday and Wednesday when the housing SMS reminders went out.

- Repeat contacts within 7 days rose to **36.9%** — most of them housing applicants calling back after receiving a clarification request.
- Complaints answered within 2 working days remain below target; the housing team has 40 complaints waiting for a technical answer.

**Ask.** Coordinate housing SMS campaigns with the CHC roster (we were not informed of the 6 October campaign), and give agents read access to clarification letters in HCMS.
