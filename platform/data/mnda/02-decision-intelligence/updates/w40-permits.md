**Q3 in numbers.** Q3 closed with **1,047 applications** and **936 permits issued**, i.e. **312 per month**. The **median cycle time improved to 19 days** in the last weeks of September, but it remains above the 15-day target.

**Circular 14/2026.** The process analysis for Q3 confirms what the counter staff told us: gated-community applications (Al Lulu Island, Al Sidr, Marina Quarter) take a median of 27.9 days against 16.8 days elsewhere. **27% of Q3 applications were returned at least once**, and a missing or expired master-developer NOC accounts for 46% of returns. Lulu Island Developments PJSC remains the slowest NOC issuer at 12.4 days on average.

**Intake pilot from 1 October.** Following ACT-2026-045, from Thursday 1 October every application is checked for a valid NOC at intake (validity, plot number, drawing revision) and the NOC request is sent to the master developer automatically at submission, so the planning review can start in parallel.

**Risk.** The Senior Planning Engineer post (REQ-2026-077) is still vacant; two engineers are on leave in the second half of October.
