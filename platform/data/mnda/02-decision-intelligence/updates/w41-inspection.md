**For the record — Q3:** **2,316 inspections** and **398 violations** (see the Q3 safety report circulated on Tuesday).

**This week:** **177 inspections**, **33 violations**. Most frequent: missing edge protection on upper floors in Marina Quarter towers, PPE non-compliance and poor material storage on villa plots in Al Waha Gardens.

Two stop-work notices were issued for open excavations without barriers (MN-08). The contractor forum on 21 October will focus on edge protection and scaffolding.
