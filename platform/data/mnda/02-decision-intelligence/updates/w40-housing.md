**Headline.** September was the first full month under Circular 9/2026 and the volume is well above what the section is staffed for.

- September closed with **612 new applications**, up **38%** on August (443). The surge started in the first week of September and has not eased.
- **41% of September files were incomplete at first review (251 of 612).** The gaps are consistent: salary certificates older than 30 days (39% of incomplete files), the new Certificate of No Property Ownership (31%), spouse Emirates ID (17%) and missing family book pages (13%).
- We decided **298 cases** in September. Average resolution time for September decisions was **19.4 days** against the 12-day SLA; officers are spending most of their time on clarifications rather than decisions.
- Open cases stood at **1,056** at the end of September.

**What we are doing.** Two Saturday overtime sessions were completed (ACT-2026-038). Khalfan Al Ali is piloting a one-page document checklist that officers send with every clarification. Two case-officer posts are still vacant — we have 14 officers in post against an establishment of 16.

**Support needed.** The checklist needs to be enforced at submission in MNDA Smart (ACT-2026-042, Digital Transformation). Without it the incomplete rate will stay around 40%.
