Budget execution reached **65.3%** of the 2026 annual budget at the end of week 40. Operating expenditure is close to plan; the gap sits in capital projects — the FM tender MNDA/PRC/2026/041 award (now expected in November) and the Al Waha Gardens roads package, which slipped by one quarter.

A re-phasing proposal for the delayed capex lines is in preparation (ACT-2026-047) and will come to the week-42 review.
