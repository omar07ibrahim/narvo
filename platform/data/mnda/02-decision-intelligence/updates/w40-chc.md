**September.** The centre handled **28,940 contacts** in September, **+31%** on August (**22,090**). Phone remains the main channel at 61%, followed by WhatsApp 24%, MNDA Smart chat 11% and email 4%. Housing application status is now a third of all enquiries (34%), followed by permit status (18%) and document requirements (14%).

- Average phone wait was **6:40** for the month, abandonment **14.2%**, first-contact resolution **61%**.
- Cost per contact rose to **AED 18.40** because of overtime.

**Actions.** The IVR status message with the self-service link went live on 14 September (ACT-2026-041). Saturday morning opening (09:00–13:00) for housing enquiries starts on 3 October (ACT-2026-048).

**Concern.** Most housing calls are customers asking the same question twice — "what is missing from my file?". The answer sits in HCMS but agents cannot see the clarification letter. We need read access to the clarification history.
