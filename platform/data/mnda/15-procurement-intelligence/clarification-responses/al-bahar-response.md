# Response to Clarification Request MNDA/PRC/2026/041/CLR-02

**Our ref:** ABIS/TND/2026/0388
**Date:** 11 October 2026
**Submitted via:** MNDA Supplier Portal — clarification module

**To:** Mr Rashed Ali Al Dhaheri, Director, Procurement & Contracts, Chair of the Tender Committee, Marsa Al Noor Development Authority, MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, Marsa Al Noor

**Subject:** Tender MNDA/PRC/2026/041 — Integrated Facilities Management Services, MNDA Headquarters & Customer Happiness Centres (36 months) — response to your clarification request ref MNDA/PRC/2026/041/CLR-02

Dear Mr Al Dhaheri,

We thank the Tender Committee for its letter and are pleased to provide our responses to the points raised, in the same order.

## (a) Pest control and vector management — included in the lump sum

We confirm that **pest control and vector management services are included in our submitted lump-sum price, at no additional cost to the Authority**.

The statement in Section 9.3 of our Technical Proposal that these services were "excluded from our scope and assumed to be procured by the Authority separately" was carried over in error from the template of a previous tender, in which the client procured pest control directly. It does not reflect our offer for MNDA/PRC/2026/041, and we kindly request that it be disregarded.

The services will be delivered by our municipality-approved subcontractor, **Gulf Shield Pest Control LLC**, under Al Bahar's management and full responsibility:

- Monthly general pest control treatments at MNDA Tower and the three Customer Happiness Centres
- Quarterly rodent audits, with bait-station mapping and reports in ServiceGrid
- Vector management of the podium, forecourt and landscaped areas, including mosquito breeding-site inspection
- Call-out response to pest sightings within 4 hours during working days

The subcontractor's trade licence and municipal approval are attached (Enclosure 2).

## (b) ISO 41001 certification — renewed certificate FM-41001-3390-R1

We confirm that Al Bahar's ISO 41001:2018 certification has been **maintained without interruption**.

The recertification audit was completed by our certification body on **6 August 2026**, before the expiry of the previous certificate on 14 August 2026. Following the closure of the audit findings, the certification body issued the renewed certificate **FM-41001-3390-R1 on 21 September 2026**, with continuous validity from **15 August 2026 to 14 August 2029**.

The copy of certificate FM-41001-3390 (15 August 2023 – 14 August 2026) included in Appendix F of our proposal was attached in error. A copy of the renewed certificate FM-41001-3390-R1 and the certification body's letter confirming the audit date and continuity of certification are attached (Enclosures 3 and 4).

## (c) ServiceGrid data residency

We confirm that all Authority data in ServiceGrid — asset register, work orders, helpdesk call logs and reports — will be hosted exclusively in the vendor's UAE data centre region in Abu Dhabi, with backups held in the same region and no replication or processing outside the United Arab Emirates. The vendor's data residency confirmation letter is enclosed.

## (d) Tender price unchanged

We confirm that our **tender price remains unchanged at the submitted total of AED 16,718,400**, as stated in our Form of Tender and Commercial Envelope. The clarifications above do not change any price, rate or other term of our submission.
