# Response to Clarification Request MNDA/PRC/2026/041/CLR-01

**Our ref:** RFM/BID/041/C-01
**Date:** 11 October 2026
**Submitted via:** MNDA Supplier Portal — clarification module

**To:** Mr Rashed Ali Al Dhaheri, Director, Procurement & Contracts, Chair of the Tender Committee, Marsa Al Noor Development Authority

**Subject:** Tender MNDA/PRC/2026/041 — Integrated Facilities Management Services, MNDA Headquarters & Customer Happiness Centres (36 months) — response to clarification request ref MNDA/PRC/2026/041/CLR-01

Dear Mr Al Dhaheri,

Thank you for your request. Raseen Facilities Management LLC is pleased to clarify its proposal on the two points raised by the Committee: the energy reduction target in Section 6 and the Emiratisation plan in Section 8 of our Technical Proposal.

## 1. Energy reduction target — raised to 8% with a weather-normalised baseline

Raseen FM revises its commitment from 6% to an **8% reduction in energy consumption over the 36-month term**, measured as follows:

- **Baseline:** consumption data in RFP Annex F for September 2025 – August 2026 — MNDA Tower including the Downtown CHC 8,412,760 kWh, CHC Al Murjan 291,480 kWh and CHC Al Waha 318,950 kWh, a total of 9,023,190 kWh
- **Weather normalisation:** monthly regression of consumption against cooling degree days (base 18 °C) from the nearest meteorological station, so that a hotter or milder year does not distort the result
- **Adjustments:** for material changes in occupancy, operating hours or floor use approved by the Authority
- **Verification:** annual savings statement prepared by Raseen FM and reviewed jointly with the Facilities & Assets Department

## 2. Energy savings trajectory and measures

| Contract year | Cumulative reduction vs. normalised baseline | Indicative annual saving |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 3% | ≈ 270,700 kWh |
| Year 2 | 6% | ≈ 541,390 kWh |
| Year 3 | 8% | ≈ 721,860 kWh |

Main measures, all within our submitted price: optimised chiller sequencing and condenser water temperature reset through the BMS; alignment of AHU and FCU schedules with MNDA working hours; quarterly set-point audits on every floor; sub-metering of the chiller plant, AHUs, lighting and small power per floor; and annual condenser tube cleaning before the summer peak. Any measure requiring capital expenditure will be proposed separately with a payback calculation for the Authority's decision. Progress against the trajectory will be a monthly KPI in our report.

## 3. Emiratisation plan for the MNDA headquarters contract

Raseen FM commits to the following month-by-month plan for UAE nationals within the 92-person on-site team, reaching **8% by month 18** and maintaining at least 8% until the end of the contract:

| Contract months | UAE nationals on site | Share of 92 | Positions added |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | 4 | 4.35% | Helpdesk & Customer Service Lead, two front-of-house coordinators, administration officer |
| 6–8 | 5 | 5.43% | HSE officer |
| 9–11 | 6 | 6.52% | CAFM coordinator |
| 12–17 | 7 | 7.61% | Graduate MEP engineer (Raseen FM graduate programme) |
| 18–36 | 8 | 8.70% | BMS shift supervisor (after 12-month development programme) |

Headcount by nationality will be reported monthly, and any shortfall will be explained with a recovery plan in the quarterly performance review.

## 4. Confirmation of price and other terms

These clarifications do not change our tender price of **AED 17,942,600** or any other part of our Commercial Envelope. All other terms of our submission of 29 September 2026 remain unchanged.
