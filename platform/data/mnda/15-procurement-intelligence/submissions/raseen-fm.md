## 2. Maintenance Methodology (PPM & Reactive)

### 2.1 Planned preventive maintenance programme

PPM schedules are built from the OEM manuals, ASHRAE Standard 180 and Raseen FM's library of 1,940 standard maintenance routines, then adjusted to asset criticality. Every asset in Annex B receives a criticality rating (A/B/C) during mobilisation. Category A assets — chillers, fire pumps, main LV panels, standby generator and lifts — receive the tightest frequencies and are never deferred.

Under MNDA/CON/2023/019 we achieved **96.9% PPM compliance in 2024** against a 95% contractual target. We will apply the same discipline here: PPM work orders are issued automatically by RFM Connect on the first working day of each month, completed jobs require photo evidence and technician sign-off, and any deferral needs written approval from the Authority's representative.

### 2.2 Reactive maintenance priorities and response times

| Priority | Example | Response | Rectification |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Emergency | Lift entrapment, flooding, chiller plant trip | 30 min | Make safe within 2 h |
| P2 Urgent | Loss of cooling to a floor or CHC hall | 1 h | 8 h |
| P3 Routine | Leaking tap, lighting fault, door closer | 4 h | 2 working days |
| P4 Minor | Cosmetic repairs, signage | 1 working day | 5 working days |

These times meet or improve on the KPIs in RFP Annex D. At the CHCs, any fault affecting a customer-facing area during opening hours is automatically raised by one priority level.

### 2.3 Chilled water plant and lifts

The four 600 TR centrifugal chillers are the most critical assets in the portfolio. A chiller plant technician will be on duty on every shift, 24 hours a day, recording hourly readings (approach temperatures, oil pressure, motor amps) on the RFM Connect mobile app. Cooling tower water treatment is checked weekly with quarterly Legionella sampling, condenser tubes are brushed annually before the summer peak, and refrigerant leak checks are carried out quarterly.

Lifts and escalators will be maintained through a subcontract with the original equipment manufacturer's authorised UAE service branch: monthly preventive visits, coordination of annual third-party inspections, and a 30-minute entrapment response, 24/7. Raseen FM remains fully accountable to MNDA for the subcontractor's performance.

## 3. Helpdesk, CAFM & Reporting

### 3.1 RFM Care Centre — 24/7 bilingual helpdesk

All requests are handled by the RFM Care Centre at Raseen Building, Al Quoz, staffed 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, by Arabic- and English-speaking agents. MNDA staff can log requests by phone, email, the RFM Connect app or the client portal. In 2025 the Care Centre handled 84,317 requests across our portfolio and answered 93.8% of calls within 20 seconds.

On site, a helpdesk desk at MNDA Tower will be staffed 07:00–19:00, Monday to Friday, by two coordinators who also support front-of-house at the Downtown CHC. Outside these hours, calls transfer automatically to the Care Centre, which dispatches the on-shift technicians.

### 3.2 RFM Connect CAFM platform

RFM Connect is Raseen FM's own CAFM system, developed in-house since 2017 and hosted in a data centre in the UAE. It covers the asset register, PPM scheduling, work orders, SLA clocks, spare parts inventory, permits-to-work and customer feedback.

- **Mobile app** for technicians, with QR-code asset identification, photo evidence and offline mode
- **Client portal** for MNDA, with live dashboards of open work orders, KPI performance and PPM status
- **Data ownership** remains with MNDA, with full data export in Excel or CSV at any time and at contract end

The Authority's Facilities & Assets team already uses the RFM Connect client portal for the community centres, so no additional user training or licences are needed.

## 5. HSE Management

### 5.1 HSE management system and site controls

Raseen FM operates an HSE management system certified to ISO 45001. On the MNDA sites we will apply:

- Electronic permit-to-work in RFM Connect for hot work, electrical isolation (lock-out/tag-out), work at height, and confined spaces such as water tanks and chiller sumps
- Daily toolbox talks and monthly HSE inspections held jointly with the Authority's representative
- A heat stress plan, including the summer midday work ban (15 June – 15 September, 12:30–15:00) for outdoor tasks such as landscaping and cooling tower works
- COSHH assessments for all cleaning and water treatment chemicals

### 5.2 HSE performance record

In 2025 Raseen FM recorded a Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate of 0.18 per million man-hours across 2.31 million man-hours worked, with no fatalities and no regulatory notices. Any incident on MNDA premises will be reported to the Authority within one hour, followed by a written report within 24 hours and a root-cause investigation within five working days.

## 6. Energy & Sustainability

### 6.1 Energy management approach and reduction target

Energy management will be part of the daily routine of our operations team. We will:

- Install sub-metering on the chiller plant, AHUs, and lighting and small power on each floor of MNDA Tower, connected to the BMS
- Review BMS set-points and operating schedules every quarter
- Provide a monthly energy report comparing consumption with the same month of the previous year
- Recommend energy-saving opportunities to the Authority, with estimated cost and payback

We commit to a **6% reduction in energy consumption over the 36-month contract term**, measured against the consumption data provided in RFP Annex F.

### 6.2 Sustainability practices

We use eco-certified cleaning chemicals and microfibre systems, segregate waste at source (paper, plastics, general and e-waste) with monthly diversion reporting, and use water-efficient drip irrigation for the podium and forecourt landscaping. Our service vehicle fleet is being converted to hybrid vehicles.
