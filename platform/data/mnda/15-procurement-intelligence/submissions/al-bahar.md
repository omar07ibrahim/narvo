## 1. Understanding of Scope & Mobilisation

### 1.1 About Al Bahar and our understanding of the requirement

Al Bahar Integrated Services LLC was established in Abu Dhabi in 2012 and today employs approximately 640 professionals. We built our reputation in cleaning, hospitality and soft services, and since 2020 we have grown a dedicated Technical Services Division delivering MEP maintenance and integrated FM.

We understand that the Authority requires a reliable, customer-focused partner to operate and maintain MNDA Tower on Corniche Road (22 floors, approximately 38,600 m²) and the three Customer Happiness Centres in Downtown, Al Murjan and Al Waha, for a period of 36 months. The Customer Happiness Centres receive a high volume of visitors, and we will give special attention to cleanliness, comfort and the first impression of every customer.

### 1.2 Services we will deliver

Al Bahar will deliver the following services, subject to the assumptions and exclusions in Section 9:

- **Hard services:** HVAC and chilled water systems, electrical, plumbing and drainage, fire and life safety systems, BMS, lifts and escalators (through OEM subcontract)
- **Soft services:** cleaning, waste management, landscaping of the podium and forecourt
- **Front-of-house:** reception support and on-site helpdesk
- **Energy management:** monitoring and reporting of energy consumption

## 2. Maintenance Methodology (PPM & Reactive)

### 2.1 Planned preventive maintenance approach

Our PPM schedules are built from the SFG20 maintenance specification, separating statutory and mandatory tasks from optimal tasks, and supplemented by OEM recommendations for the centrifugal chillers, lifts and fire systems. During mobilisation we will review the asset register provided in the tender documents and prepare annual, quarterly and monthly PPM schedules for all equipment. PPM will be scheduled in ServiceGrid, and technicians will close each job on the mobile app with photographs and readings.

Condition-based maintenance will be applied to critical plant: quarterly vibration and oil analysis on chiller compressors and AHU fan sets, and thermographic surveys of main LV panels twice a year. We will target PPM compliance of 95% or better, reported monthly to the Authority.

### 2.2 Reactive maintenance and response times

| Priority | Description | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | Risk to life, property or critical service | 30 min (MNDA Tower), 60 min (Al Murjan and Al Waha) |
| Urgent | Major disruption to operations | 2 hours |
| Normal | Routine defects | 24 hours |
| Low | Minor or cosmetic works | 3 working days |

Emergency faults will be made safe within 4 hours and urgent faults rectified within 24 hours; other rectification times will be agreed with the Authority during mobilisation in line with the KPIs in the tender documents.

### 2.3 Chilled water plant and lifts

Our technicians will carry out routine operation and maintenance of the four centrifugal chillers, cooling towers and pumps, including daily checks and water treatment. Major chiller overhauls, eddy current testing and compressor repairs will be carried out by a specialist chiller subcontractor approved by the Authority.

Lifts and escalators will be maintained by the original equipment manufacturer under a subcontract with Al Bahar, including monthly maintenance visits and 24/7 breakdown and entrapment response.

## 3. Helpdesk, CAFM & Reporting

### 3.1 Helpdesk arrangements

An on-site helpdesk at MNDA Tower will be staffed by Al Bahar coordinators from 07:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. From 18:00 to 07:00, and on weekends and public holidays, calls will be handled by our call-centre partner, Sawt Contact Solutions FZ-LLC, which provides a 24/7 bilingual (Arabic/English) service. The partner logs every call in ServiceGrid and contacts our duty supervisor, who dispatches the on-call technician.

This arrangement ensures that the Authority has a 24/7 helpdesk service at all times.

### 3.2 ServiceGrid CAFM system

Al Bahar uses ServiceGrid, a licensed cloud-based CAFM platform used by facility management companies in several countries. ServiceGrid is a SaaS solution hosted in the vendor's regional cloud, which provides high availability and regular updates without the need for local servers.

Key features include asset register, PPM scheduling, work order management, mobile app for technicians, SLA tracking and management dashboards. The Authority will receive user accounts to view work orders and reports, and data can be exported on request.

### 3.3 Reporting

We will submit a monthly report to the Authority covering PPM completion, reactive work orders, response times, cleaning audits, HSE statistics and energy consumption. Quarterly review meetings will be held with the Authority's facilities team to discuss performance and improvements.

## 5. HSE Management

### 5.1 HSE system and site practices

Al Bahar operates an occupational health and safety management system certified to ISO 45001. Our site practices include risk assessments and method statements for all activities, permit-to-work forms for high-risk activities (hot work, electrical isolation, working at height and confined spaces) countersigned by the supervisor, daily toolbox talks, PPE for all staff, and a heat stress programme in compliance with the summer midday break.

### 5.2 HSE performance

Al Bahar has recorded zero lost-time incidents across its contracts in the last 24 months (LTIFR 0.00 in 2025 over 1,286,400 man-hours; TRIR 0.62). All incidents and near misses will be reported to the Authority within 24 hours and investigated by our HSE Officer.

## 6. Energy & Sustainability

### 6.1 Energy monitoring and sustainability practices

Our technical team will monitor energy consumption using the existing BMS and include monthly utility consumption by sub-meter in the monthly report. We commit to a **5% reduction in electricity consumption over the 36-month term** against the 2025 consumption in Annex F, through chiller sequencing optimisation, set-point and operating-schedule reviews and LED retrofit proposals submitted to the Authority for approval. Al Bahar supports sustainability through the use of environmentally friendly cleaning products, waste segregation and responsible water use in landscaping.
