## 2. Maintenance Methodology (PPM & Reactive)

### 2.1 Risk-based planned preventive maintenance

Each Annex B asset is given a criticality score (consequence of failure × likelihood) during mobilisation, following ISO 55000 asset management principles. PPM routines are drawn from OEM manuals and ASHRAE Standard 180, then tuned to criticality and condition data from CrescentOne. Critical assets — chillers, fire pumps, main LV switchboards, standby generator, lifts — follow fixed schedules that cannot be deferred without the Authority's written approval.

Across our portfolio, PPM compliance in 2025 was **98.4%** (target 95%), measured automatically from CrescentOne work order closures.

### 2.2 Predictive maintenance for the chiller plant

The chiller plant receives condition monitoring from Day 24:

- Vibration sensors on compressor motors and condenser water pump bearings
- Continuous kW/TR from dedicated power meters on each chiller
- Evaporator and condenser approach temperatures, oil pressure and oil temperature via the BMS
- Refrigerant leak detectors in the plant room

CrescentOne analytics compare each reading with the chiller's own baseline. For example, a condenser approach rising more than 1.5 °C above baseline automatically raises a tube-cleaning work order before efficiency and capacity are lost. Eddy current tube testing is carried out on two chillers per year. At the Al Nawras Investment Corporation headquarters, this approach cut unplanned chiller downtime by 63% in two years.

### 2.3 Reactive maintenance response and resolution

| Priority | Example | Response | Rectification |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Emergency | Entrapment, flooding, plant trip, fire system fault | 20 min Tower · 40 min Al Murjan and Al Waha | Make safe 2 h |
| P2 Urgent | Loss of cooling to a floor or CHC hall | 45 min | 6 h |
| P3 Routine | Lighting, plumbing fixtures, doors | 4 h | 1 working day |
| P4 Minor | Cosmetic, signage | 1 working day | 5 working days |

A dedicated mobile technician van covers the Al Murjan and Al Waha CHCs during opening hours.

### 2.4 Lifts and escalators through OEM subcontract

Lift and escalator maintenance is subcontracted to the original equipment manufacturer's authorised UAE service branch, with monthly preventive visits and 30-minute entrapment response 24/7. The OEM's remote monitoring alarms are integrated into CrescentOne, so every lift fault appears in the Authority's dashboard alongside all other work orders. Crescent audits the subcontractor monthly and is fully accountable for its performance.

## 6. Energy & Sustainability

### 6.1 Energy team and ISO 50001 governance

Energy is managed by Crescent Energy Services under our ISO 50001-certified energy management system. Two CEM-certified engineers, Eng. Nadia Farouk and Eng. Rohit Bhandari, lead the work, supported by an on-site energy analyst. An energy review will be completed within the first 90 days, producing an Energy Management Plan for the Authority's approval.

### 6.2 Committed 12% reduction with measurement and verification

We commit to a **12% reduction in energy consumption over 36 months**, verified under IPMVP Option C against the RFP Annex F baseline (9,023,190 kWh for September 2025 – August 2026), weather-normalised using cooling degree days.

| Contract year | Cumulative reduction |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | 5% |
| Year 2 | 9% |
| Year 3 | 12% (≈1,082,780 kWh per year) |

Main measures: chiller sequencing and condenser water temperature reset (target plant efficiency below 0.70 kW/TR), VSD tuning on pumps and cooling tower fans, AHU schedules aligned to MNDA working hours, demand-controlled ventilation at the CHCs, and set-point governance.

### 6.3 Optional shared-savings mechanism

As an option for the Authority, Crescent offers a shared-savings mechanism: verified savings above the committed 12% are shared 50:50, and any shortfall below 12% at the end of each contract year is credited to the Authority at the prevailing electricity tariff. Low-cost measures are funded by Crescent within the contract price. Terms of the option are set out in Schedule 6 of the Commercial Envelope.

### 6.4 Wider sustainability commitments

Under ISO 14001 we will target 55% waste diversion from landfill by month 24, use eco-certified cleaning products, raise cooling tower cycles of concentration to reduce make-up water, and convert podium irrigation to drip with soil-moisture sensors.
