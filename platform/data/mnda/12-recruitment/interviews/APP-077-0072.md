# Technical interview — APP-077-0072

**Format:** 60-minute structured technical interview, held by video call (candidate at Wadi Sharm), 09:00–10:02. Notes taken by Ahmed Khalifa Al Romaithi and agreed by the panel on 8 Oct 2026.

**Q1 (Eng. Omar): Your CV says "member of the e-permit implementation team". What did you actually do?**
More than the CV indicates. Arvind configured the reviewer workflow for the structural and high-rise module of the municipality's Tasreeh platform and wrote 63 rule-based checks for structural submissions (stamped calculation report present and signed, geotechnical report less than 24 months old, load-assumption form complete, revision consistency across drawing sets). He ran a six-week parallel run comparing system checks against manual intake, found 9 rules producing false returns and corrected them before go-live. Result he quoted: complex files reach technical review about 2 working days sooner, and returns for unstamped calculations in his team fell from roughly 1 in 5 files to fewer than 1 in 20. The business owner for the overall platform was a colleague from the architectural side; he owned the structural module.

**Q2 (Eng. Reem): Your CV lists fire and life-safety review per NFPA 101. Walk us through a smoke-control review.**
On probing, Arvind clarified that fire and life-safety review in Wadi Sharm is carried out by Civil Defence. His role is to check that the Civil Defence preliminary approval is attached and to coordinate structural fire-resistance comments. He could not describe smoke-control design criteria in any depth and acknowledged the CV wording overstated his role.

**Q3 (Eng. Omar): Scenario — transfer girder at podium level, consultant's model shows drift within limits but the wind-tunnel base shear is 18% above code values.**
Strong answer: require the design to use the governing loads, check the combination factors and whether the tunnel results were scaled correctly, and ask for an independent check of the transfer girder. Clear and structured.

**Q4 (Eng. Omar): Circular 14/2026 — master-developer NOCs.**
Limited direct experience; Wadi Sharm has only one gated community and NOCs are checked by the architectural team. Answer reasonable but generic.

**Q5 (Ahmed): Developing reviewers.**
Described a structured six-month plan for national trainees (shadowing, graded files, weekly review of comments). All five trainees since 2019 now review independently.
