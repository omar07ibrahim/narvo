## Experience

### Bayt Al Rimal Development Authority — Head of Structural & Building Plan Review (Jan 2016 – Present)
- Lead a section of 11 reviewers (6 structural, 4 architectural/planning, 1 MEP coordinator); responsible for recruitment, annual objectives, performance reviews and a monthly calibration session in which all reviewers comment on the same file and compare results.
- Section throughput: approx. 210 applications per month (2025 average), of which 15–20 are buildings above 20 storeys.
- Designed and introduced risk-based triage in 2021: low-risk villas and repeat designs routed to a 5-day fast track with checklist review; complex buildings assigned to senior reviewers with a mandatory pre-submission meeting. Median review time fell from 24 to 13 working days in 18 months; first-cycle approval rose from 38% to 57%.
- Introduced independent third-party peer review for all towers above 40 storeys and for transfer structures; wrote the scope and accreditation criteria for peer reviewers (2017).
- Chair the monthly coordination meeting with the authority's three master developers. In 2022 we agreed a joint NOC checklist (19 items) and a rule that the NOC references the drawing revision; NOC-related returns fell from 31% to 12% of all returns in the following year.
- Co-authored the authority's Structural Submission Guidelines (2018, revised 2023): calculation report format, stamping and digital signature requirements, geotechnical minimums.
- Product owner for the structural review module of the authority's e-permit system (2019–2020): defined reviewer checklists, comment codes and the rule that unstamped calculations are rejected at completeness stage.
- Represent the authority on the building code technical committee; reviewed the local adoption of IBC 2021 and ASCE 7-16 wind provisions.
- Escalation point for disputed comments with consultants; personally reviewed 40+ appeals since 2016.
