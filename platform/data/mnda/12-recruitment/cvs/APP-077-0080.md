## Experience

### Ras Sadeem Municipality, Building Permits Department — Plan Review Architect (Apr 2016 – Present)
- Review of architectural drawings for building permits: villas, residential and commercial buildings, fit-out.
- Around 140 files per month (2025), all types; first response to consultant in 4 working days in average.
- Check of setbacks, coverage, heights, parking, accessibility, fire access and egress in coordination with Civil Defence.
- In 2022 the municipality started to require the approval of the master developer for plots in Sadeem Shores community (gated, approx. 900 plots). I coordinate the weekly meeting with the developer's design team since this date.
- I prepared with the developer a common checklist of the community design rules, so the consultant gets the same comments from both sides. In my files, returns for design-guideline reasons went down from 31% (2022) to 17% (2023).
- Agreed with the developer that the NOC must show the drawing revision number; this is now in the municipality checklist.
- Reviewed architectural and fire access part of Sadeem Bay Tower 2 (29 floors, residential) and of the new municipal market.
- Proposed to put the setback and coverage calculation inside the e-submission form (automatic check). It is used since 2024 and the calculation errors in submissions are much less.
- I train the new reviewers in the architecture section (6 since 2021) and I check their comments during first 3 months.
- Prepared the Arabic/English/French comment templates for the section.
