## 1. Executive summary

1. **Housing demand surged after Circular No. 9/2026.** September applications rose to **612 (+38% on August's 443)**
   and **41% (251)** were incomplete at first review. The open backlog reached **1,184 cases** on 9 October, and
   September closures took **19.4 days** on average against the 12-working-day SLA.
2. **Permit cycle times worsened after Circular No. 14/2026.** The Q3 median was **21.6 days** (target 15), driven by
   gated communities (**27.9 days** vs **16.8** elsewhere) and an average **9.2-day** wait for master-developer NOCs.
3. **Customer Happiness Centre demand is up 31%.** September contacts reached **28,940**; phone abandonment is 14.2%
   and first-contact resolution has fallen to 61%. Half of all contacts are status enquiries on housing and permits.
4. **Al Ghaf Community Centre comfort complaints** (23 in August–September) trace to chiller CH-02 and overdue
   condenser cleaning; the renewal decision on contract MNDA/CON/2023/019 is due by **1 November 2026**.
5. Five decisions are requested from the Director General (section 11).

## 3. Housing — demand after Circular No. 9/2026

Circular No. 9/2026 was issued on 18 August 2026 and took effect on **1 September 2026**. It shortened the validity
of salary certificates from 90 to 30 days and added the Certificate of No Property Ownership, the spouse's Emirates
ID and (for loans) a 6-month bank statement.

| Period | Applications received | Change |
|---|---|---|
| July 2026 | 418 | — |
| August 2026 | 443 | +6% |
| September 2026 | **612** | **+38%** |
| 1–9 October 2026 (flash) | **214** | 30.6 per working day vs 27.8 in September |
| Q3 2026 total | 1,473 | |

Part of the September peak reflects applicants who prepared files before the circular took effect, but October intake
shows no sign of easing. The Housing Applications Section has **14 case officers**.

## 4. Housing — incomplete applications and backlog

**41% of September applications were incomplete at first review (251 of 612)**, compared with 22% in August under
the previous requirements. Missing items among incomplete files:

| Missing or invalid item | Share |
|---|---|
| Salary certificate older than 30 days | **39%** |
| Certificate of No Property Ownership not provided | **31%** |
| Spouse's Emirates ID missing | **17%** |
| Family Book pages incomplete (children's pages) | **13%** |

| Indicator (as at 9 October 2026) | Value |
|---|---|
| Open backlog | **1,184 cases** (438 "Incomplete — awaiting applicant"; 746 with MNDA) |
| Open cases per case officer | 84.6 |
| Average resolution time, September closures | **19.4 days** (SLA 12 working days) |
| Repeat handling | **2.3 touches per case** |

Repeat handling is the main capacity drain: officers often request missing items one at a time, although Circular
No. 9/2026 (section 5.4) asks for all items in a single clarification request. Several applicants are MNDA employees
whose salary certificates had expired under the new 30-day rule.

## 5. Building permits — cycle time after Circular No. 14/2026

Circular No. 14/2026 (issued 2 July, effective **15 July 2026**) made a master-developer NOC mandatory for permits in
MN-03 Al Lulu Island, MN-06 Al Sidr Residential and MN-08 Marina Quarter.

| Indicator, Q3 2026 | Value |
|---|---|
| Permit applications received | **1,047** (gated communities 398; other districts 649) |
| Median cycle time, all applications | **21.6 days** (target 15; 2025 baseline 18.9) |
| Median cycle time, gated communities | **27.9 days** |
| Median cycle time, non-gated districts | **16.8 days** |
| Average time in "Awaiting master-developer NOC" | **9.2 days** |
| Applications returned to applicant (rework) | **27%** (283) |
| Throughput | **312 permits issued per month** (936 in the quarter) |

Non-gated permits are close to the service standard; the gap is concentrated almost entirely in the three gated
districts, where the NOC step adds time both directly (waiting) and indirectly (returns for missing or expired NOCs).

## 8. Community facilities — Al Ghaf Community Centre

Al Ghaf Community Centre (MN-07; built 2019; GFA 14,200 m²; two 350 TR air-cooled screw chillers CH-01 and CH-02;
8 AHUs) received **23 comfort complaints** in August and September 2026, mainly from the Multipurpose Hall and Ladies'
Fitness.

- Chiller **CH-02** recorded **11 high-discharge-pressure trips in September**, all between 13:00 and 16:00 with
  ambient temperatures above 44 °C.
- The condenser-coil cleaning planned maintenance has been **overdue since 15 August 2026**.
- September chiller energy consumption was **17% higher** than in September 2025.
- The FM contractor is Raseen Facilities Management LLC under contract **MNDA/CON/2023/019** (community centres FM,
  AED 4,200,000 per year, 1 January 2024 – 31 December 2026, one-year renewal option). The contractor has opened job
  JOB-RFM-26-04471 for the CH-02 trips.
- The renewal or non-renewal notice must be served 60 days before expiry, i.e. **by 1 November 2026**. Tender
  MNDA/PRC/2026/057 (Al Ghaf Community Centre and four satellite facilities, 24 months, estimated AED 5.4M) closes on
  22 October 2026.

## 9. Field inspections and procurement

**Inspections.** Field Operations & Inspection completed **2,316 inspections** in Q3 2026 and recorded **412
violations** (17.8% of inspections). Most frequent: missing edge protection, PPE non-compliance, unsafe scaffolding
(no guardrails or toe boards), unprotected rebar starters, poor housekeeping and material storage, damaged hoarding or
missing signage, open excavations without barriers, and missing fire extinguishers. Inspection rosters were adjusted
for the summer midday work ban (15 June – 15 September, 12:30–15:00) without loss of coverage.

**Procurement.** Tender MNDA/PRC/2026/041 (Integrated Facilities Management — MNDA Headquarters & Customer
Happiness Centres, 36 months, estimated AED 18.6M) closed on 30 September 2026 with three bids. Technical evaluation
is under way (committee chair: Rashed Ali Al Dhaheri); one bidder's ISO 41001 certificate requires clarification.

## 10. People and employee services

- **Emiratisation** reached 62.1% (target 63% for 2026).
- **Recruitment:** requisition MNDA-HR-REQ-2026-077 (Senior Planning Engineer — Building Permits) closed on
  28 September with 86 applications; 6 finalists will be interviewed in October.
- **Salary certificates:** HR Services issued 203 salary certificates in September (August: 88), 61% of them marked
  "for housing application". All were issued within the 1-working-day standard. Many employees are themselves housing
  applicants and were caught by the new 30-day validity rule.
- **Employee requests:** the most frequent HR and IT requests were salary certificates, leave balances, remote-work
  requests under HC-POL-07 and laptop replacements.
- **NARVO programme:** five capability pilots are in controlled evaluation; none is in production until it passes the
  AI-POL-01 threshold (at least 92% accuracy, Arabic evaluated separately).
