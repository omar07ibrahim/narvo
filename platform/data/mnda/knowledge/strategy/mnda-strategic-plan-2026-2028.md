# MNDA Strategic Plan 2026–2028 — Summary

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|

| Period | 1 January 2026 – 31 December 2028 |

This summary sets out the Authority's vision, mission, strategic pillars, key performance indicators and flagship
initiatives for 2026–2028. The full plan, including department operational plans and the risk register, is held by
the Strategy & Performance Office. Baselines are January–October 2025 actuals.

## Context

Marsa Al Noor has grown faster than planned. The population is projected to exceed **186,000 in 2026** (38% UAE
nationals) and to approach 200,000 by 2028, across 412 km² and eight districts. Three of the districts — Al Lulu
Island (MN-03), Al Sidr Residential (MN-06) and Marina Quarter (MN-08) — are gated communities developed by master
developers, where building activity is concentrated.

The 2023–2025 plan delivered the MNDA Smart app, three Customer Happiness Centres and the first phase of Tamkeen CMS.
The main gaps it leaves are:

- building-permit cycle times above the 15-working-day service standard (median 18.9 days in 2025);
- housing decisions missing the 12-working-day SLA in 29% of cases;
- a high share of contacts to the Customer Happiness Centre that are status enquiries, with first-contact resolution
  of 64%;
- manual document checking in housing and permits, which consumes case-officer and permit-officer capacity.

## Vision, mission and values

**Vision.** A liveable, well-planned coastal city where every government service is simple, fast and trusted.
(رؤيتنا: مدينة ساحلية متكاملة التخطيط ونابضة بالحياة، خدماتها الحكومية ميسّرة وسريعة وموثوقة.)

**Mission.** To plan, permit and sustain the development of Marsa Al Noor, and to provide housing and community
services to its residents, through proactive, digital and accountable government.

**Values.**

| Value | What it means at MNDA |
|---|---|
| Customer first | Design services around the customer's journey, not our organisation chart |
| Integrity | Decisions are fair, documented and taken by accountable people |
| Collaboration | One authority: departments share data and solve problems together |
| Innovation | Use technology — including AI — where it measurably improves services |
| Accountability | Every target has an owner, and performance is reported openly |

## Pillar 1 — Liveable communities & housing: initiatives

1. **Tamkeen CMS upgrade (phase 2)** — automated document completeness checks at submission, integration with the
   Marsa Al Noor Land Registry for property-ownership checks, and a single clarification request to applicants.
   Delivery: Q2–Q4 2026. Owner: Housing & Community Services with the DTO.
2. **Housing eligibility review 2026** — update documentary requirements and verification rules for the Housing
   Grant & Loan Programme (MNDA-SVC-201), aligned with the Land Registry integration. Delivery: by Q3 2026.
3. **Al Waha Gardens (MN-04) plot servicing** — complete roads and utilities for the next allocation of citizens'
   housing plots. Delivery: 2026–2027.
4. **Community facilities FM re-procurement** — new integrated facilities management contracts for headquarters,
   Customer Happiness Centres and community centres, with energy and comfort KPIs. Tenders: 2026.

## Pillar 2 — Smart planning & permits: initiatives

1. **Gated-community design compliance** — introduce a mandatory master-developer No Objection Certificate (NOC) for
   building permits in MN-03, MN-06 and MN-08, to prevent post-completion disputes over community guidelines.
   Delivery: 2026.
2. **ePermits gated-community NOC integration** — master developers receive NOC requests and issue NOCs digitally in
   MNDA ePermits, with automatic checks of NOC validity and drawing revision. Delivery: design 2026, live 2027.
3. **e-Plan check** — automated checks of setbacks, plot coverage and parking against Marsa Atlas GIS data.
   Delivery: 2027.
4. **Risk-based inspections** — target site-safety inspections by contractor history and project stage.
   Delivery: 2026–2027. Owner: Field Operations & Inspection.

## Pillar 3 — Customer happiness & digital-first services: initiatives

1. **Proactive status notifications** — automatic WhatsApp and app notifications at every change of status for housing
   cases and permits, to reduce "where is my application?" contacts. Delivery: 2026.
2. **Omnichannel Customer Happiness Centre** — one view of the customer across phone, WhatsApp (+971 50 663 2000), app
   chat and email, with case history from Tamkeen CMS and MNDA ePermits. Delivery: 2026–2027.
3. **Digital-first service redesign** — redesign the ten highest-volume services so that they can be completed fully in
   MNDA Smart. Delivery: 2026–2028.
4. **Customer voice programme** — monthly happiness survey across channels, with results published to every
   department.

## Pillar 4 — Efficient, AI-enabled government: initiatives

1. **NARVO private AI platform programme** — a secure, UAE-hosted AI platform owned by MNDA. 2026 scope: **five
   capability pilots**:
   - housing case preparation (document checks and draft clarification requests in Tamkeen CMS);
   - Customer Happiness assistant for status and requirements enquiries (Arabic and English);
   - permit process intelligence (cycle-time and bottleneck analysis);
   - Employee Services assistant for HR and IT requests;
   - Arabic–English translation with the MNDA glossary.
   Each pilot must pass a documented evaluation under AI-POL-01, in Arabic and English, before production use.
2. **AI governance** — AI Governance Committee, use-case register, monthly monitoring.
3. **Integration layer** — APIs between Tamkeen CMS, MNDA ePermits, MNDA People, MNDA Finance and the Land Registry.

## Pillar 5 — People & capability: initiatives

1. **Emiratisation plan 2026–2028** — targeted recruitment of UAE nationals into planning engineering, inspection and
   digital roles; graduate programme of 24 places per year.
2. **AI and data literacy** — mandatory foundation course for all employees by end of 2027; advanced track for
   case officers, permit officers and analysts.
3. **Leadership pipeline** — succession plans for all director and section-head positions.
4. **Flexible work** — continued remote and flexible work under HC-POL-07 for eligible roles, and review of working-hour
   flexibility in 2026.
5. **Engagement action plans** — every department acts on the results of the annual engagement survey (November).
