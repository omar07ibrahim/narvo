# Employee Handbook — Chapter 3: Working Hours, Leave & Allowances

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reference | MNDA/HC/HB/03 · Version 4.0 |
| Effective | 1 July 2026 (supersedes version 3.2 of 1 January 2025) |
| Owner | Maryam Hassan Al Shamsi, Director, Human Capital |
| Applies to | All MNDA employees on Authority contracts (all grades) |
| Arabic version | HC-HB-03-AR (in case of difference, the Arabic text prevails) |
| Enquiries | hr.services@mnda.gov.ae · ext. 3300 · MNDA People app |

Version 4.0 incorporates Circular No. 11/2026 (flexible start times, ref MNDA/HC/CIR/11/2026) and the summer working
arrangement. Leave and allowance rules follow the federal human resources rules as applied by the Authority.

## 3.1 Working week and official hours

The Authority's working week runs from **Monday to Friday**, with Saturday and Sunday as the weekly rest days.

| Day | Official hours | Hours per day |
|---|---|---|
| Monday – Thursday | 7:30 – 15:30 | 8 |
| Friday | 7:30 – 12:00 | 4.5 |
| Saturday – Sunday | Weekly rest days | — |

The standard working week is 36.5 hours. Customer Happiness Centres (Downtown, Al Murjan, Al Waha) open to the public
during official hours; staff on rostered shifts follow the roster published by the Centre Manager.

Official public holidays are those announced for the government sector; Human Capital confirms the dates by internal
announcement. Attendance is recorded through the MNDA People app (location-verified at MNDA sites) or the entry
terminals at MNDA Tower and the Customer Happiness Centres.

## 3.2 Flexible start (Circular No. 11/2026)

Since **1 July 2026**, employees may start work at any time between **7:00 and 9:00**, provided they complete their
full daily hours (Circular No. 11/2026, ref MNDA/HC/CIR/11/2026).

| Start time | Finish Monday – Thursday | Finish Friday |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00 | 15:00 | 11:30 |
| 7:30 | 15:30 | 12:00 |
| 8:00 | 16:00 | 12:30 |
| 9:00 (latest) | 17:00 | 13:30 |

**Core hours** are **9:00 – 14:00** from Monday to Thursday (9:00 – 11:30 on Friday). All employees must be at work —
or reachable on Microsoft Teams on an approved remote day — during core hours. Meetings involving several teams
should be scheduled within core hours. Flexible start does not apply to rostered shifts at the Customer Happiness
Centres or to field teams whose start time is set by their roster.

## 3.3 Ramadan and summer arrangements

**Ramadan.** During the holy month of Ramadan, official hours are reduced. In Ramadan 2026 the hours were
**9:00 – 14:00 Monday to Thursday** and **9:00 – 12:00 on Friday**; flexible start is suspended during Ramadan. Hours
for each Ramadan are confirmed by Human Capital circular.

**Summer midday work ban.** From **15 June to 15 September**, outdoor work in direct sun is prohibited between
**12:30 and 15:00**. This applies to all field staff, in particular Field Operations & Inspection. Inspection rosters
are rescheduled so that site visits finish by 12:30 or resume after 15:00; time spent indoors (report writing,
office duties) during the ban is normal working time.

**Summer working arrangement (1 July – 30 September).** Under Circular No. 11/2026, office-based employees in
remote-eligible roles work remotely on Fridays during this period. These Fridays do not count against the two-day
weekly remote allowance under HC-POL-07. Each department keeps minimum on-site cover on Fridays.

## 3.4 Annual leave

All employees are entitled to **30 working days of annual leave per year**, regardless of grade.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Accrual | 2.5 working days per completed month of service |
| Request | In MNDA People, at least **5 working days** before the first day of leave |
| Longer leave | At least **10 working days** in advance for leave of more than 10 days |
| Approval | Line manager |
| More than 20 consecutive working days | Line manager and Director |
| Probation period (first 6 months) | Only with Director approval |

Leave is approved subject to operational needs. Managers should respond within 2 working days; a request that has
not been approved does not authorise absence. Public holidays and weekly rest days falling within a period of annual
leave are not deducted from the balance. The current balance is shown in MNDA People and can also be asked of the
NARVO Employee Services assistant.

## 3.5 Carry-over and leave planning

1. Up to **15 days** of unused annual leave may be carried over into the next year.
2. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the following year; days not used by then are forfeited
   unless the Director has deferred the leave in writing for operational reasons.
3. Days above the 15-day limit at 31 December are forfeited.
4. Departments prepare an annual leave plan by 31 January so that peak periods (for example the September–October
   housing application peak) remain covered.
5. Leave cannot be encashed while in service, except as provided in the federal rules on separation.

## 3.6 Sick leave

1. Notify your line manager by 9:00 on the first day of absence (Teams or phone).
2. Any sick leave of **more than 1 day** requires a medical certificate from a **licensed UAE health facility**,
   uploaded to MNDA People within **2 working days** of returning to work.
3. Employees may take **up to 1 uncertified sick day, twice per year**; further single days require a certificate.
4. Sick leave is paid according to the federal human resources rules: **the first 3 months on full pay and the
   next 3 months on half pay**. Longer absences are referred to the medical committee.
5. Sick leave during annual leave is recognised if a certificate is provided; the annual leave days concerned are
   credited back.
6. Repeated short-term sick leave may lead to a confidential discussion with Human Capital and occupational health.

## 3.7 Compassionate leave

Compassionate (bereavement) leave is fully paid and does not reduce the annual leave balance.

| Relationship | Leave |
|---|---|
| Death of a spouse | **5 working days** |
| Death of a first-degree relative (parent, child, sibling) | **3 working days** |
| Death of a second-degree relative (grandparent, grandchild) | **1 working day** |

The leave starts from the date of death or the day after. Request it in MNDA People under "Compassionate leave" and
upload the death notification within 10 working days of returning. A female employee whose husband dies is entitled
to the *iddah* leave provided under the federal rules, in place of the 5 working days above.

## 3.8 Maternity, nursing and paternity leave

| Leave | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| Maternity leave | **90 calendar days on full pay**, which may start up to 30 days before the expected date of delivery |
| Additional maternity leave | Up to **30 days unpaid**, on request, immediately after maternity leave |
| Nursing hours | **2 hours per day** for **1 year** after returning to work, taken at the start or end of the day |
| Paternity leave | **5 working days** on full pay, taken within **6 months** of the birth |

Request maternity leave in MNDA People at least 30 days before it starts, with the medical report showing the
expected date of delivery; upload the birth certificate within 15 working days of the birth. Nursing hours are
recorded in MNDA People and cannot be accumulated. Paternity leave may be taken in one block or split, within the
6-month window.

## 3.9 Hajj leave and exam leave

**Hajj leave.** Muslim employees are entitled to **15 working days** of paid Hajj leave **once during their service**
with the Authority. Requests are submitted in MNDA People by 1 March of the Hajj year with the official Hajj permit;
approval depends on operational cover and is granted in order of first-time pilgrims.

**Exam leave.** Employees enrolled in a study programme **sponsored or approved by the Authority** receive paid exam
leave for each exam day, plus one preparation day for final-year or thesis examinations, up to 10 working days per
academic year. The exam timetable from the institution must be uploaded with the request. Study that is not
sponsored or approved is taken from annual leave.

## 3.10 Allowances

| Allowance | Eligibility | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Housing allowance | All employees | According to grade (MNDA salary scale, Annex 3-B) |
| Transport allowance | All employees | According to grade (Annex 3-B) |
| Social allowance | UAE national employees, for spouse and children | Per the federal social allowance table; updated when the Family Book is updated in MNDA People |
| Field allowance | Inspection staff, Field Operations & Inspection | **AED 1,500 / month** |
| Phone allowance | Field roles and approved on-call roles | **AED 300 / month** |

Allowances are paid monthly with the salary and shown separately on the payslip in MNDA People. The field allowance
stops when an employee moves to an office-based role. Employees who receive a corporate smartphone keep the phone
allowance only if their role remains field-based or on-call.

## 3.11 Overtime

1. Overtime is worked **only with prior written approval** from the Director, requested in MNDA People before the
   work is done. Retrospective requests are not accepted except for documented emergencies (for example a
   P1 IT outage or a site safety incident).
2. Time off in lieu is the preferred form of compensation, taken within 3 months.
3. Paid overtime is capped at 40 hours per month and is calculated according to the federal rules.
4. Directors and employees in grades with an all-inclusive package are not eligible for paid overtime.

## 3.12 Salary certificates and HR letters

Salary certificates and other HR letters are requested through MNDA People or the NARVO Employee Services
assistant. A salary certificate is issued within **1 working day** by HR Services, digitally signed with a QR code,
in Arabic or English. It states basic salary and total allowances.

For a housing application under Circular No. 9/2026, the salary certificate must have been **issued within the last
30 days** on the date the application is submitted; an older certificate is rejected by the Housing programme.
Request a new certificate shortly before you apply. Full details: Procedure HC-PRC-12.
