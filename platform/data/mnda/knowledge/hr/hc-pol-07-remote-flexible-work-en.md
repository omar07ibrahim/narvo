**Amendment note.** This policy is read together with Circular No. 11/2026 (ref MNDA/HC/CIR/11/2026, effective
1 July 2026), which introduced the flexible start window of 7:00–9:00 and the summer working arrangement. Section 3
reflects that circular.

## 1. Purpose

This policy allows eligible employees to work remotely for part of the week where their role permits, so that the
Authority can retain talent, reduce commuting time and keep productivity high, without any reduction in service to
customers or in information security.

Remote work is a **flexible arrangement granted by the Authority, not an entitlement**. It is approved by the line
manager against the needs of the team and can be changed or withdrawn as described in section 11. Working hours,
leave and conduct rules in the Employee Handbook (Chapter 3, HC-HB-03) apply in full on remote days.

## 2. Scope and definitions

The policy applies to all MNDA employees on Authority contracts. It does not apply to contractors or secondees, whose
working location is set by their contract.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Remote day | A full working day performed away from MNDA premises, normally from the employee's home in the UAE |
| Office-based role | A role whose duties are performed mainly at a desk with MNDA systems |
| Remote-eligible role | An office-based role marked "remote-eligible" in MNDA People by Human Capital, after consultation with the Director |
| Core hours | 9:00–14:00, Monday to Thursday, during which employees must be reachable |
| Exceptional remote work | Remote work beyond 2 days per week for medical or family-care reasons (section 7) |

Working remotely from outside the UAE is not permitted under this policy.

## 3. Remote work allowance

1. Employees in remote-eligible roles may work remotely **up to 2 days per week**, with **line manager approval**.
2. Remote days **do not carry over** between weeks: an unused day in one week cannot be added to the next.
3. Remote days are whole days; half days are not recorded as remote days.
4. **Summer arrangement:** under Circular No. 11/2026, office-based employees in remote-eligible roles may work
   remotely on Fridays from **1 July to 30 September**, requested once in MNDA People and approved by the line
   manager. These Fridays **do not count** against the 2-day weekly allowance.
5. Managers may set team days on which everyone is in the office (for example a weekly planning meeting), provided
   the team is informed at least one week ahead.
6. A department may limit remote work during peak periods — for example the September–October housing
   application peak — with notice from the Director.

## 4. Eligibility criteria

An employee may request remote work only when **all** of the following apply:

1. **Probation completed** — at least 6 months of service with the Authority.
2. **Performance** — the most recent performance rating is at least **"Meets expectations"**. Employees with no rating
   yet (first year after probation) are eligible if the line manager confirms satisfactory performance.
3. **Role** — the position is marked **remote-eligible** in MNDA People.
4. **No active improvement plan** or disciplinary sanction in the last 12 months.
5. **Equipment** — the employee has an MNDA-managed laptop and approved VPN access (section 9).

Eligibility is checked automatically in MNDA People when a request is submitted; the employee sees which criterion is
not met. Questions on whether a role is remote-eligible go to the employee's Human Capital business partner.

## 5. Eligibility by role type

| Role type | Examples | Remote work |
|---|---|---|
| Office-based professional and administrative roles | Planning engineers reviewing drawings, case officers, finance, procurement, HR, DTO, strategy | **Eligible** — up to 2 days per week |
| Managers and section heads | Section heads in Housing, Permits, HR | Eligible — up to 2 days per week, staggered with deputies |
| Field inspection roles | Building inspectors, site safety inspectors (Field Operations & Inspection) | **Not eligible** |
| Front-line rostered shifts | Customer Happiness Centre agents at Downtown, Al Murjan and Al Waha | **Not eligible** (remote shifts only if a remote contact-centre roster is approved by the Centre Manager) |
| On-site facilities roles | Facilities & Assets technicians, building supervisors, reception and security liaison | **Not eligible** |
| IT on-site support | Service Desk field engineers | Not eligible on rostered on-site days |

Employees in non-eligible roles benefit from the flexible start window where their roster allows (HC-HB-03, 3.2).

## 6. Request and approval steps

1. **Submit** the request in MNDA People → Flexible Work → Remote day, **at least 1 working day in advance**. A
   recurring pattern (for example every Tuesday and Thursday) is requested once per month, by the 25th of the
   previous month.
2. **Automatic check** — MNDA People confirms eligibility (section 4) and that the weekly limit is not exceeded.
3. **Line manager approval** — the manager approves or declines within 1 working day, considering team cover,
   meetings and service commitments. A declined request shows the reason.
4. **VPN** — on first approval, VPN access is granted automatically by the DTO within 1 working day.
5. **Calendar** — the approved day appears in the employee's Outlook calendar and Teams status ("Working remotely").
6. **Change** — an approved day can be cancelled by the employee or, with at least 1 working day's notice, by the
   manager where business needs require attendance.

## 7. Exceptional remote work

Where an employee needs to work remotely **more than 2 days per week** for a temporary medical reason or family care
(for example recovery after surgery, or caring for a seriously ill child or parent), the following applies:

1. The request is submitted in MNDA People → Flexible Work → Exceptional, with supporting evidence (medical report
   from a licensed UAE health facility, or other documents as applicable).
2. It requires approval by the **Director** of the employee's department **and Human Capital**.
3. The maximum duration is **4 weeks**. A further period requires a new request and fresh evidence.
4. Exceptional remote work may be granted to employees in roles not normally eligible only where meaningful office
   duties can be assigned for the period.
5. Medical information is seen only by Human Capital and is not shared with the line manager.

## 8. Availability and working hours on remote days

1. Normal working hours apply, including the flexible start window of 7:00–9:00 (Circular No. 11/2026).
2. Employees must be **reachable on Microsoft Teams during core hours 9:00–14:00** (9:00–11:30 on Friday), with Teams
   status kept up to date, and must answer calls and messages within 15 minutes during core hours.
3. Cameras are switched on for team meetings unless the organiser agrees otherwise.
4. Attendance is recorded through the MNDA People app check-in, which accepts remote check-in only on approved
   remote days.
5. An employee who becomes sick on a remote day records sick leave in the usual way (HC-HB-03, 3.6).
6. The employee must be able to come to the office within 2 hours if an urgent operational need arises.

## 9. Equipment and information security

1. Remote work is performed only on an **MNDA-managed laptop**, connected through the **MNDA VPN** with
   **multi-factor authentication**. Personal computers may not be used to access MNDA systems.
2. **No Confidential paper documents** may be taken home or printed at home (IS-POL-01, section 7).
3. Calls involving personal data of customers or employees are taken where they cannot be overheard.
4. Public Wi-Fi may be used only through the VPN; screens must not be visible to others.
5. Lost or stolen devices are reported immediately to the IT Service Desk (ext. 4357).
6. The Authority does not pay home internet or utility costs for remote days. A second monitor for home use is not
   provided; employees may take home their office headset.

## 10. Responsibilities

| Party | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee | Requests remote days in advance; meets availability, security and output expectations; keeps Teams status current |
| Line manager | Approves fairly and consistently; agrees objectives and output; maintains service cover |
| Director | Approves exceptional remote work; may set department-wide limits during peak periods |
| Human Capital | Maintains the remote-eligible role list; approves exceptional requests; reports usage quarterly |
| Digital Transformation Office | Provides VPN, MFA and managed laptops; monitors security events |

## 11. Suspension and withdrawal

1. A line manager may suspend an employee's remote work, with written reasons, where availability or output
   expectations are not met, or where the employee's latest performance rating falls below "Meets expectations".
2. A breach of information security rules (section 9) leads to immediate suspension and may lead to disciplinary
   action under the Authority's procedures.
3. Suspension is reviewed after 3 months. The employee may raise a concern with Human Capital if they believe a
   decision was unfair.
4. Directors may suspend remote work for a whole department for a defined period for operational reasons, with
   notice to Human Capital.

## 12. FAQ — eligibility and requests

**I joined MNDA four months ago. Can I work remotely?**
Not yet. Remote work is available after completing the 6-month probation period. You can use the flexible start
window (7:00–9:00) from your first day.

**I did not use my remote day this week. Can I take three days next week?**
No. Remote days do not carry over between weeks; the limit is 2 days in each week.

**Do the summer Fridays count towards my 2 days?**
No. Fridays between 1 July and 30 September worked remotely under Circular No. 11/2026 do not count.

**How far in advance must I ask?**
At least 1 working day before the remote day. Recurring patterns are approved monthly, by the 25th of the previous
month.

## 13. FAQ — working remotely

**I am a building inspector. Can I work remotely on days when I only write reports?**
No. Field inspection roles are not remote-eligible. Report writing is done at the office or the field base.

**My mother is in hospital and I need to work from home for three weeks. What can I do?**
Submit an exceptional remote work request with supporting evidence. It requires approval by your Director and Human
Capital and can be granted for up to 4 weeks.

**Can I take case files home on a remote day?**
Only electronically, through the VPN and MNDA systems. Confidential paper documents may never be taken home.

**Do I need VPN even if I only use Microsoft Teams?**
Yes. All remote work uses the MNDA-managed laptop and the VPN with MFA. VPN is granted automatically once your first
remote day is approved.
