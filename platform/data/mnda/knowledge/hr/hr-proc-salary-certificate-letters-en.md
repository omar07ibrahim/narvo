# Procedure — Salary Certificates & HR Letters (HC-PRC-12)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reference | MNDA/HC/PRC/12 · Version 1.3 |
| Effective | 1 September 2026 (supersedes version 1.2 of 1 February 2026) |
| Owner | Maryam Hassan Al Shamsi, Director, Human Capital |
| Process lead | Latifa Khamis Al Romaithi, Senior HR Services Officer |
| Contact | hr.services@mnda.gov.ae · ext. 3300 |

Version 1.3 was issued after Circular No. 9/2026 (Housing Grant & Loan Programme): salary certificates used for a
housing application must now be **issued within the last 30 days** (previously 90 days) and must state basic salary
and total allowances. Section 8 explains what this means for employees who are housing applicants.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how MNDA employees request, and how HR Services issues, salary certificates and other HR
letters, and the service level for each letter type.

It applies to all current employees on Authority contracts. Former employees request experience certificates by
email to hr.services@mnda.gov.ae. Letters for contractors and secondees are issued by their own employer.

All letters are generated from the data held in MNDA People (salary, grade, position, join date). Employees should
check that their personal details — name as on the Emirates ID, position title and bank account — are correct in
MNDA People before requesting a letter, because HR Services cannot change data while issuing a letter.

## 2. Letter types and service levels

| Letter | Typical use | Service level | Approver / issuer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salary certificate** (شهادة راتب) | Housing applications, banks, tenancy, schools | **1 working day** | Latifa Khamis Al Romaithi, Senior HR Services Officer |
| Salary transfer letter | Opening a loan or credit card; addressed to a named bank | 2 working days | Senior HR Services Officer, with Finance (Payroll) confirmation |
| Experience certificate | Professional bodies, further study, future employers | 3 working days | Director, Human Capital |
| No-objection (NOC) letter | Driving licence, visa for a family member, embassy visa | 2 working days | Senior HR Services Officer |
| Employment verification | Requested by a government entity | 1 working day | Senior HR Services Officer |

Service levels count from submission of a complete request in MNDA People, excluding weekends and public holidays.
Requests submitted after 14:00 are counted from the next working day.

## 3. How to request a letter

**Option A — MNDA People (web or app)**

1. Open MNDA People → Requests → HR Letters.
2. Select the letter type, language (Arabic or English) and addressee (section 5).
3. For a salary certificate, state the purpose — select "Housing application" if it is for the Housing Grant &
   Loan Programme.
4. Submit. You receive a reference in the format HRL-2026-NNNNN and a notification when the letter is ready.

**Option B — NARVO Employee Services assistant**

Ask the assistant, for example "I need a salary certificate in Arabic for my housing application". The assistant
prepares the request in MNDA People and shows it to you for confirmation; nothing is submitted until you confirm.
The certificate itself is always issued and signed by HR Services, not by the assistant (AI-POL-01, section 3).

## 4. Contents of the salary certificate

Every salary certificate states:

1. Employee's full name as on the Emirates ID, and Emirates ID number.
2. Employee number, position title, grade and department.
3. Date of joining the Authority and employment status (permanent / fixed-term).
4. **Basic salary** (monthly, AED).
5. **Total allowances** (monthly, AED), and, where requested, the breakdown: housing, transport, social, field and
   phone allowances.
6. **Total monthly salary** (AED).
7. Date of issue, letter reference (HRL-2026-NNNNN) and the QR verification code.

The certificate does not show deductions, loan instalments or personal bank details. A salary certificate for a
housing application includes basic salary and total allowances as required by Circular No. 9/2026, section 4.4.

## 5. Language and addressee options

**Language.** Certificates are available in **Arabic or English**. A bilingual version is not issued; request two
letters if both are needed.

**Addressee.** Choose one of:

| Option | When to use |
|---|---|
| "To whom it may concern" (إلى من يهمه الأمر) | General use, including the MNDA Housing Grant & Loan Programme |
| A named entity, e.g. a bank, school or landlord | When the receiving organisation requires its own name on the letter |

Salary transfer letters are always addressed to a named bank and branch. Embassy NOC letters name the embassy and
the country and dates of travel. Letters cannot be addressed to individuals other than an official of a named entity.

## 6. Digital signature and QR verification

1. All letters are issued as PDF documents, **digitally signed** by the approver in MNDA People.
2. Each letter carries a **QR code** that links to the MNDA verification page, which confirms the reference, issue
   date, employee name and letter type. Receiving entities can verify a letter without contacting HR Services.
3. A letter that has been edited after issue fails verification. Never alter a PDF letter.
4. Stamped paper originals are provided only where the receiving entity does not accept digital letters, on request,
   within 1 additional working day.
5. Letters are valid until the date stated by the receiving entity; the Authority does not set an expiry, but see
   section 8 for housing applications.

## 7. Collection and delivery

1. The signed PDF is available in MNDA People → My Documents as soon as it is issued, and is emailed to the
   employee's MNDA address.
2. Paper originals are collected from HR Services, Floor 6, MNDA Tower, Monday to Thursday 7:30–15:30 and Friday
   7:30–12:00, on presentation of the staff card.
3. Letters are never sent to personal email addresses or to third parties by HR Services, except an employment
   verification requested officially by a government entity.
4. Copies of issued letters are retained for **2 years** under RM-POL-02 and then deleted automatically.

## 8. Validity for housing applications (Circular No. 9/2026)

Many MNDA employees are themselves applicants to the Housing Grant & Loan Programme. Since **1 September 2026**:

1. A salary certificate must be **issued within the last 30 days** on the date the housing application is submitted.
   **A certificate older than 30 days is rejected** by the Housing programme and the application is marked
   "Incomplete — awaiting applicant".
2. If both spouses are employed, a certificate is needed for each spouse.
3. Request your certificate **no more than a few days before you submit** the housing application. If you are asked to
   resubmit documents later, check the date again and request a new certificate if needed — it is issued within
   1 working day.
4. Choose "To whom it may concern" or address it to the "Housing Applications Section, Marsa Al Noor Development
   Authority".

## 9. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Consequence | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Using a certificate older than 30 days for a housing application | Application marked incomplete; processing clock stops | Request a new certificate just before applying |
| Wrong addressee (e.g. a bank name on a certificate used for housing or tenancy) | Receiving entity rejects the letter | Use "To whom it may concern" unless a named entity is required |
| Name spelt differently from the Emirates ID | Bank or embassy rejects the letter | Correct your name in MNDA People first |
| Editing or merging PDF letters | QR verification fails | Submit the PDF exactly as issued |
| Requesting an English letter when Arabic is required | Re-issue needed | Check the receiving entity's language requirement |

## 10. HR Services processing steps

1. The request arrives in the HR Services queue in MNDA People, with the system-generated draft letter.
2. The HR Services officer checks the data against the employee's record (position, salary, allowances, join date)
   and the requested language and addressee.
3. The approver listed in section 2 reviews and digitally signs the letter. Salary certificates are signed by
   **Latifa Khamis Al Romaithi**, Senior HR Services Officer, or by an authorised HR Services officer in her absence.
4. MNDA People publishes the letter to the employee and records the issue in the letter register.
5. Requests not completed within the service level are escalated automatically to the Senior HR Services Officer.

Monitoring: HR Services reports weekly volumes and on-time issue rates to the Director of Human Capital.
