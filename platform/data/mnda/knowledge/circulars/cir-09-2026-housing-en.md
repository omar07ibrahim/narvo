# Circular No. 9/2026

**To:** All Customer Happiness Centres, Housing Applications Section, applicants and their representatives
**Subject:** Updated requirements for applications under the Housing Grant & Loan Programme (MNDA-SVC-201)
**Issued:** 18 August 2026 · **Effective:** 1 September 2026
**Issued by:** Fatima Obaid Al Nuaimi, Director, Housing & Community Services

## 1. Purpose

This circular updates the documentary requirements and eligibility checks for the Housing Grant & Loan
Programme, following the Authority's 2026 review of eligibility verification and the integration of the
Marsa Al Noor Land Registry with the Authority's case management system.

## 2. Scope

The requirements apply to every new application submitted on or after 1 September 2026 through any channel
(MNDA Smart app, website, Customer Happiness Centres). Applications submitted before that date are assessed
under the previous requirements unless the applicant changes the requested product (grant or loan).

## 3. Eligibility

1. The applicant must be a UAE national, aged 21 or above, and the head of the household.
2. **Housing Grant:** total monthly household income must not exceed **AED 30,000**. Maximum grant amount:
   **AED 600,000**, non-repayable, for construction or purchase of a first home in Marsa Al Noor.
3. **Housing Loan:** total monthly household income must not exceed **AED 60,000**. Maximum loan: **AED 900,000**,
   interest-free, repayable over up to **25 years**, with instalments not exceeding 25% of household income.
4. Neither the applicant nor the spouse may own residential property in the UAE, except property inherited
   jointly and shared with three or more heirs.

## 4. Required documents

| # | Document | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Emirates ID — applicant | Valid, front and back |
| 4.2 | Emirates ID — spouse | **New:** valid, front and back (married applicants) |
| 4.3 | Family Book (Khulasat Al Qaid) | **Complete copy, all pages including children** |
| 4.4 | Salary certificate | **Issued within the last 30 days** (previously 90 days), stating basic salary and total allowances; for both spouses if both are employed |
| 4.5 | Certificate of No Property Ownership | **New:** issued by the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry within the last 60 days |
| 4.6 | Bank statement | **New — loan track only:** last 6 months, stamped by the bank |
| 4.7 | Plot allocation letter or purchase contract | Where a plot or unit has been identified |
| 4.8 | Marriage certificate | Only where the family book has not yet been updated |

## 5. Incomplete applications

1. An application missing any document in section 4 is marked **"Incomplete — awaiting applicant"** and the
   applicant is notified through the channel used to apply, listing every missing or invalid item.
2. The applicant has **15 working days** to provide the missing documents. The case is suspended during this
   period and the service-level clock (12 working days) stops.
3. If documents are not received within 15 working days, the application is closed and may be re-submitted.
4. Case officers must request **all** missing items in a single clarification request wherever possible to avoid
   repeated handling.

## 6. Verification

1. Salary certificates older than 30 days on the date of submission are treated as missing.
2. Income figures are verified against the salary certificate; any difference above 5% between declared and
   certified income requires a clarification.
3. The Certificate of No Property Ownership may be retrieved directly from the Land Registry by the case officer
   **with the applicant's consent**; in that case it does not need to be uploaded.

## 7. Decisions

Eligibility decisions are taken by the Head of the Housing Applications Section or an authorised deputy. Automated
tools may prepare checks and drafts but **may not approve or reject an application** (see the MNDA Responsible AI
Policy, AI-POL-01).
