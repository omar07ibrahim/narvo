# Circular No. 14/2026

**To:** Licensed engineering consultants, contractors, property owners, master developers, permit officers
**Subject:** Master-developer No Objection Certificate (NOC) for building permits in gated communities
**Issued:** 2 July 2026 · **Effective:** 15 July 2026
**Issued by:** Eng. Omar Yousef Al Hammadi, Director, Planning & Building Permits

## 1. Background

Several completed buildings in gated communities were found to deviate from the master developers' community
design guidelines (façade materials, roof equipment screening and boundary walls), creating disputes after
completion. To prevent this, design compliance with community guidelines must be confirmed before a permit is issued.

## 2. Applicable districts

The NOC is mandatory for all plots located in:

- **MN-03 Al Lulu Island** — master developer: Lulu Island Developments PJSC
- **MN-06 Al Sidr Residential** — master developer: Sidr Communities LLC
- **MN-08 Marina Quarter** — master developer: Marina Quarter Properties LLC

## 3. Applicable services

- MNDA-SVC-101 Building Permit — New Construction
- MNDA-SVC-102 Fit-out Permit, **only** where façade, roof or external works are affected
- Modification permits for external alterations

## 4. Requirements

1. The NOC must be issued by the master developer on its letterhead, reference the plot number
   (format MN-DD-SSS-PPPP) and the drawing revision it approves.
2. The NOC is valid for **90 days** from issue. An expired NOC is treated as missing.
3. The drawing revision referenced in the NOC must match the revision submitted to the Authority.
   A mismatch requires a new NOC.
4. Master developers are requested to issue NOCs within **7 working days** of a complete request.

## 5. Processing

1. Applications without a valid NOC are returned to the applicant with the status "Returned — NOC required".
2. Where the applicant has requested the NOC but not yet received it, the application may be held in the status
   **"Awaiting master-developer NOC"** for up to 20 working days; the service-level clock is suspended.
3. Permit officers record the NOC reference and validity date in the permit file.

## 6. Transitional provision

Applications submitted before 15 July 2026 and already under technical review are exempt.
