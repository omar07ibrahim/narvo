# Circular No. 11/2026

**To:** All MNDA employees, line managers, department directors and Customer Happiness Centre supervisors
**Subject:** Flexible start times and summer working arrangements
**Issued:** 2 June 2026 · **Effective:** 1 July 2026 (section 6 applies from 15 June 2026)
**Issued by:** Maryam Hassan Al Shamsi, Director, Human Capital

## 1. Purpose

This circular introduces a flexible start window for the Authority's working day and sets out the working
arrangements for the summer of 2026. Its aims are to support employee wellbeing during the hottest months, to
reduce peak-hour commuting on Corniche Road and the main access roads to MNDA Tower, and to protect field staff
from heat stress, while keeping every public-facing service fully available during the Authority's official hours.

The circular should be read together with the MNDA Remote Work Policy (HC-POL-07). Where this circular is
silent, HC-POL-07 and the Authority's existing leave and attendance rules continue to apply.

## 2. Scope

1. The circular applies to all MNDA employees on permanent and fixed-term contracts, in all departments and
   locations (MNDA Tower, the three Customer Happiness Centres, the MN-05 field depot and community facilities).
2. Section 4 (flexible start) applies to office-based staff. Staff on rostered shifts — Customer Happiness Centre
   counter and contact-centre staff, and field teams on departmental rosters — follow sections 6 and 7.
3. Staff of contractors and outsourced service providers working on MNDA premises follow their own employer's
   working arrangements and the conditions of their contract with the Authority.

## 3. Official working hours (unchanged)

The Authority's official working hours, which are also the public service hours, remain as follows:

| Day | Official hours | Working hours |
|---|---|---|
| Monday – Thursday | 7:30 – 15:30 | 8 |
| Friday | 7:30 – 12:00 | 4.5 |
| Saturday – Sunday | Weekly rest | — |

The daily working hours include the rest break. Nothing in this circular changes the hours during which the
public can reach the Authority by telephone (800 6632), WhatsApp or in person.

## 4. Flexible start times (from 1 July 2026)

From 1 July 2026, office-based employees may start work at any time within the flexible start window, provided
they complete the required daily hours and are present during core hours.

| Day | Flexible start window | Required hours | Core hours | Finish time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday – Thursday | 7:00 – 9:00 | 8 | 9:00 – 14:00 | 15:00 – 17:00 |
| Friday | 7:00 – 9:00 | 4.5 | 9:00 – 11:30 | 11:30 – 13:30 |

1. No prior registration of the chosen start time is needed; the start time is taken from the check-in record.
2. Absence during core hours requires approved leave or personal permission.
3. A line manager may require a fixed start time for a specific role or day (for example committee meetings,
   site visits or service coverage), giving at least 2 working days' notice.

## 5. Summer arrangement for office-based staff (1 July – 30 September 2026)

1. From 1 July to 30 September 2026, office-based staff who are eligible for remote work under HC-POL-07 may
   work **Fridays remotely**.
2. Remote Fridays **do not count** toward the maximum of 2 remote days per week permitted under HC-POL-07.
3. The employee requests the summer remote-Friday arrangement once in MNDA People; it takes effect when the line
   manager approves it. The manager may suspend it for a given Friday for operational reasons with 2 working
   days' notice.
4. On a remote Friday the employee completes 4.5 working hours within the flexible start window, checks in through
   the MNDA People app and remains reachable by telephone and the Authority's collaboration tools throughout.

## 6. Field staff — midday work ban (15 June – 15 September 2026)

1. This section applies from **15 June 2026** to staff whose duties are performed outdoors, including inspectors
   and municipal maintenance crews of the Field Operations & Inspection Department, Housing & Community Services
   engineers carrying out site technical assessments, and Facilities & Assets technicians.
2. From 15 June to 15 September, **all outdoor work stops between 12:30 and 15:00**. This period is paid working
   time and is used for indoor duties such as reports, inspection records and planning.
3. Field inspections are scheduled **between 07:00 and 12:30 or after 15:00**. Departments may roster early, split
   or late shifts so that inspections can take place after 15:00; such shifts are recorded in MNDA People.
4. Emergency make-safe work (exposed cables, flooding, open manholes) may continue during the ban period only with
   heat-stress controls: shade, drinking water, work in pairs and supervisor approval.
5. Inspectors continue to verify during site inspections that construction sites observe the midday break.

## 7. Customer Happiness Centres

1. Public hours at the Downtown, Al Murjan and Al Waha Customer Happiness Centres remain **Monday – Thursday
   7:30 – 15:30 and Friday 7:30 – 12:00**. The 800 6632 hotline and WhatsApp channel follow the same hours for
   non-emergency enquiries.
2. Counter and contact-centre staff work **rostered shifts** and are not covered by the flexible start window or the
   remote-Friday arrangement.
3. Standard rosters: early shift 7:00 – 15:00 (opening and preparation), late shift 7:45 – 15:45 (closing and
   end-of-day reconciliation); Friday 7:30 – 12:00.
4. Rosters are prepared by the Manager, Customer Happiness Centre (Sara Ahmed Al Blooshi) and published in MNDA
   People at least two weeks in advance.

## 8. Recording working time in MNDA People

1. All working time is recorded in the **MNDA People** HR system through the **app check-in** (check-in and
   check-out). On MNDA premises the check-in is location-verified; remote check-in is available only on approved
   remote days.
2. A missed check-in or check-out is corrected through an attendance correction request in MNDA People within 3
   working days, approved by the line manager.
3. Where the required daily hours are not completed, the shortfall is recorded as short hours and offset against
   the employee's personal permission balance. Three or more unexplained shortfalls in a calendar month are
   referred to the line manager.
4. Technical problems with the app check-in are reported to the IT Service Desk.

## 9. Responsibilities of managers

Line managers are responsible for applying this circular in their teams. In particular they must:

1. Ensure that each team providing a service is covered for the full official hours, 7:30 – 15:30 Monday to
   Thursday and 7:30 – 12:00 on Friday.
2. Schedule internal meetings within core hours (9:00 – 14:00 Monday to Thursday) wherever possible.
3. Approve or decline remote-Friday requests within 2 working days.
4. Plan field inspection schedules in line with section 6 and keep a record of any emergency work during the
   midday ban period.
5. Review attendance exceptions in MNDA People every week.

## 10. Ramadan working hours

This circular does not affect Ramadan working hours. Ramadan hours are announced separately each year by circular;
in 2026 they were **9:00 – 14:00 Monday to Thursday and 9:00 – 12:00 on Friday**. During Ramadan the flexible start
window in section 4 does not apply unless the Ramadan circular for that year provides otherwise.

## 11. Enquiries

Questions about this circular should be sent to the Human Capital Department at **hr.services@mnda.gov.ae** or
raised as an HR request in the MNDA People app. Problems with the MNDA People app check-in should be reported to
the IT Service Desk. Department directors are asked to brief their teams on this circular before 1 July 2026.
