# MNDA Procurement Manual — Version 3.2

## Part 2 — Procurement Methods, Thresholds and Approval Authorities

**Document ref:** MNDA/PRC/MAN/3.2 · **Approved by:** H.E. Eng. Khalid Saif Al Mazrouei, Director General, on 2 March 2025 · **Owner:** Procurement & Contracts Department · **Supersedes:** Version 3.1

This Part applies to all purchases of goods, services and works by the Authority, regardless of funding source. Definitions in Part 1 (Articles 1–7) apply. All values are exclusive of VAT.

### Article 8 — Determination of the estimated value

Before a procurement method is selected, the requesting department, with the Procurement & Contracts Department, shall determine the estimated value of the requirement. The estimated value:

1. covers the full contract term, including any optional extension or renewal periods;
2. is calculated exclusive of VAT;
3. is based on a documented source, such as previous contract prices, a market survey or a cost estimate prepared by the requesting department;
4. is recorded in the purchase requisition before the procurement is initiated.

For contracts of more than twelve months, the total value over the full term, and not the annual value, determines the procurement method and the approval authority.

### Article 9 — Prohibition of splitting requirements

1. It is prohibited to divide a requirement into smaller purchases, or to spread its value across several requisitions, purchase orders or financial periods, with the intent or effect of avoiding a procurement method or an approval authority.
2. Requirements of the same nature requested by the same department within a twelve-month period shall be aggregated for the purpose of determining the method.
3. The Procurement & Contracts Department shall review purchasing data each quarter to detect possible splitting and shall refer suspected cases to Internal Audit.
4. Requesting or approving a split requirement constitutes a disciplinary violation for both the requesting officer and the approver.

### Article 10 — Summary of procurement methods and thresholds

| Method | Estimated value (excl. VAT) | Minimum competition | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct purchase — single quotation | Up to AED 20,000 | 1 written quotation | ERP purchase order |
| Direct purchase — three quotations | AED 20,001 – 100,000 | 3 written quotations | ERP purchase order |
| Limited tender | AED 100,001 – 1,000,000 | At least 3 invited registered suppliers | RFQ via MNDA Supplier Portal |
| Public tender | Above AED 1,000,000 | Open to all eligible registered suppliers | MNDA Supplier Portal, minimum 21 days |
| Framework call-off | As per framework | As per Article 15 | MNDA Supplier Portal |
| Emergency procurement | Any value | As per Article 16 | Documented after the event |

### Article 11 — Direct purchase

1. Direct purchase may be used where the estimated value does not exceed **AED 100,000**.
2. Up to **AED 20,000**, one written quotation from a registered supplier is sufficient, provided the buyer records that the price is reasonable compared with market prices or previous purchases.
3. From **AED 20,001 to AED 100,000**, at least **three written quotations** from registered suppliers shall be obtained. Where fewer than three are received, the reasons shall be documented and approved by the Section Head.
4. Quotations shall be compared on a like-for-like basis. The purchase shall be awarded to the lowest compliant quotation unless a documented technical justification is approved.

### Article 12 — Limited tender

1. A limited tender shall be used where the estimated value exceeds AED 100,000 and does not exceed **AED 1,000,000**.
2. At least **three suppliers registered** in the relevant category of the MNDA Supplier Portal shall be invited through a Request for Quotation (RFQ). Invited suppliers shall be selected on the basis of category, past performance and rotation, and the selection shall be recorded.
3. The minimum response period is ten calendar days from the date of the RFQ.
4. Where fewer than three compliant responses are received, the Director, Procurement & Contracts may approve the continuation of the process, or may require the RFQ to be reissued, with written reasons.

### Article 13 — Public tender

1. A public tender is mandatory where the estimated value exceeds **AED 1,000,000**.
2. Public tenders shall be advertised on the **MNDA Supplier Portal** and are open to all registered suppliers meeting the eligibility requirements.
3. The period between publication and closing shall be at least **21 calendar days**. A period of at least 30 days is recommended for complex services and works.
4. Where an addendum materially changes the requirements less than seven days before closing, the closing date shall be extended by at least seven days.
5. Services and works tenders shall use the two-envelope procedure described in Part 3.

### Article 14 — Minimum content of RFQs and tender documents

Every RFQ and tender document shall contain, as a minimum:

1. the scope of work and technical specifications or service levels;
2. the evaluation criteria and their weights, including any mandatory pass/fail requirements;
3. the delivery or completion period and the place of delivery or performance;
4. the payment terms, being **payment within 30 days** of receipt of a valid invoice supported by approved delivery or completion documents;
5. the contract conditions, the offer validity period (normally 90 days) and the closing date and method of submission;
6. In-Country Value requirements, where applicable;
7. the procedure and deadline for clarification questions.

Criteria not stated in the RFQ or tender documents shall not be used in the evaluation.

### Article 15 — Framework agreements

1. Framework agreements may be established for recurring requirements through a public tender, for a maximum term of three years.
2. Where all terms are fixed in the framework, call-offs may be placed directly with the framework supplier. Where more than one supplier is appointed and terms are not fully fixed, a mini-competition shall be held among them.
3. The value of each call-off is subject to the approval authorities in Article 17. The cumulative value of call-offs shall not exceed the framework ceiling approved at award.

### Article 16 — Emergency procurement

1. Emergency procurement may be used only where an unforeseeable event threatens life, public safety or health, or the continuity of a critical service of the Authority, and the delay of a normal procedure would cause serious harm.
2. Emergency purchases shall be approved by the director of the requesting department and the Director, Procurement & Contracts. Where prior written approval is not possible, verbal approval shall be documented within three working days.
3. The purchase shall be limited to what is necessary to address the emergency and to a period not exceeding 30 days.
4. All emergency purchases shall be reported to the Tender Committee at its next meeting. Poor planning shall not constitute an emergency.

### Article 17 — Approval authorities

| Contract value (excl. VAT) | Approval of award |
|---|---|
| Up to AED 100,000 | Section Head, Procurement |
| Up to AED 1,000,000 | Director, Procurement & Contracts |
| Up to AED 10,000,000 | Tender Committee recommendation and approval by the Director General |
| Above AED 10,000,000 | Tender Committee recommendation, approval by the Director General, and notification to the Board of Directors |

No officer may approve a purchase in which he or she was the requesting officer. Approval authority may be delegated only in writing by the Director General.

### Article 18 — Supplier registration and eligibility

1. Only suppliers registered on the MNDA Supplier Portal may be invited to an RFQ or awarded a contract, except in emergency procurement under Article 16.
2. Registration requires a valid trade licence, a VAT registration (TRN) where applicable, and the documents specified for the category.
3. For contracts above AED 1,000,000, a valid In-Country Value certificate is mandatory, as set out in Part 3.
4. Suppliers suspended or debarred by the Authority shall not be invited or awarded for the period of the suspension.
