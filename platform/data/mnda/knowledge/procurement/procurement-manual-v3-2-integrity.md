# MNDA Procurement Manual — Version 3.2

## Part 4 — Integrity, Supplier Performance and Records

**Document ref:** MNDA/PRC/MAN/3.2 · **Approved by:** H.E. Eng. Khalid Saif Al Mazrouei, Director General, on 2 March 2025 · **Owner:** Procurement & Contracts Department

This Part applies to every employee of the Authority who takes part in any stage of a procurement, including members of tender, opening and evaluation committees, technical advisers and approvers.

### Article 33 — Conflict of interest

1. A conflict of interest exists where an employee, or a relative up to the fourth degree, has a financial, employment, family or other personal interest in a bidder, supplier or subcontractor, or in the outcome of a procurement, that could affect, or appear to affect, the employee's impartiality.
2. Examples include: shareholding or partnership in a bidder; current or recent employment (within the previous two years) with a bidder; a close relative employed by a bidder in a management or bid role; and any promise of future employment.
3. An actual, potential or perceived conflict shall be treated with the same seriousness.

### Article 34 — Declaration of interest by committee members

1. **Every member** of a tender, opening or evaluation committee, and every technical adviser, shall sign a declaration of interest and confidentiality on form **PRC-F-11** **before the bids are opened**.
2. The declaration is made after the names of the bidders are known and covers each bidder and its declared subcontractors.
3. A member who becomes aware of a conflict after signing shall immediately notify the committee chair in writing and update the declaration.
4. No member may take part in opening or evaluation until a signed declaration is on record. Declarations are filed with the tender record in the MNDA Supplier Portal.

### Article 35 — Recusal and replacement

1. A member who declares a conflict shall **recuse** himself or herself from all deliberations, scoring and decisions on the tender concerned and shall not have access to the bids.
2. The committee chair shall decide on the replacement of the member and record the decision. Where the chair has a conflict, the decision is taken by the Director General.
3. Scores given by a member who should have recused shall be disregarded, and the affected part of the evaluation shall be repeated.
4. Recusals and replacements are recorded in the evaluation report.

### Article 36 — Gifts, hospitality and benefits

1. Employees involved in procurement shall not request or accept any gift, hospitality or benefit from a bidder or supplier **during an active tender** in which that bidder or supplier participates, regardless of value.
2. Outside an active tender, gifts of nominal value not exceeding **AED 500** may be accepted only where refusal would cause offence, and shall be recorded in the **Gifts and Hospitality Register** within three working days.
3. Any gift with a value above AED 500 shall be politely refused or returned, and the offer recorded in the register.
4. Cash, vouchers and gifts of any value offered in connection with an evaluation or award shall be refused and reported to the Head of Legal Affairs.

### Article 37 — Confidentiality of procurement information

1. Bids, evaluation scores, committee deliberations, clarification correspondence and draft reports are confidential until the award is approved, and commercially sensitive bid information remains confidential after award.
2. Information shall be shared only with those who need it for their role in the procurement, through the Supplier Portal or the Authority's approved systems. Bid documents shall not be sent to personal email accounts or external storage.
3. AI-assisted tools may process bid documents only within systems approved by the Digital Transformation Office that keep the data within the Authority's environment.
4. Breach of confidentiality is a disciplinary violation and may lead to the cancellation of the procurement.

### Article 38 — Communication with bidders during a tender

1. From publication until the award is notified, all communication with bidders about the tender shall take place in writing through the Supplier Portal, through the procurement officer named in the tender documents.
2. Committee members and staff of the requesting department shall not meet or communicate with any bidder about the tender, including at social or industry events. Any approach by a bidder shall be reported to the committee chair on the same day.
3. Existing contract communications with an incumbent supplier continue normally but shall not refer to the tender.

### Article 39 — Supplier performance records

1. The contract owner department shall rate each supplier with a contract above AED 1,000,000 **every quarter**, and other suppliers at contract completion, on the following scale:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Excellent — consistently exceeds contract requirements |
| 4 | Good — meets all requirements, minor issues resolved promptly |
| 3 | Satisfactory — meets most requirements; some issues needing follow-up |
| 2 | Below expectations — repeated failures against KPIs or obligations |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory — serious or persistent failures |

2. Each rating shall be supported by evidence such as KPI reports, complaints, audit findings and correspondence.
3. A rating of 2 or below requires a performance improvement plan agreed with the supplier, and is notified to the Procurement & Contracts Department.

### Article 40 — Use of past performance in evaluation and contract decisions

1. Past performance records of the Authority **shall be considered** in the evaluation of bids where the tender documents include experience, references or past performance among the criteria, for incumbent and previous suppliers alike.
2. An average rating below 3 over the previous twelve months, or an open performance improvement plan, shall be brought to the attention of the evaluation committee and recorded in the evaluation report.
3. Performance records shall also be considered in decisions on contract renewal, extension or non-renewal.
4. A supplier shall be given the opportunity to comment on a rating before it is used against it in an evaluation.

### Article 41 — Records retention and audit trail

1. All procurement records shall be retained for **seven years** after contract completion or cancellation of the procurement, whichever is later. These include requisitions, tender documents, bids, declarations, scoring and moderation sheets, clarifications, override logs, reports, approvals and contracts.
2. Records shall be kept in electronic form in the Supplier Portal and the Authority's records system, with an **audit trail** showing every access, change, approval and override, with user identity and time.
3. Records shall not be deleted or altered before the end of the retention period. Disposal after the retention period requires approval of the Head of Legal Affairs.

### Article 42 — Reporting of breaches

Any employee who becomes aware of a breach of this Manual, including undeclared conflicts, splitting of requirements or improper contact with bidders, shall report it to the Director, Procurement & Contracts or directly to Internal Audit. Reports made in good faith are protected, and the identity of the reporting employee is kept confidential.

### ملخص باللغة العربية — حدود الشراء وتضارب المصالح

يُلخّص هذا القسم أبرز أحكام دليل المشتريات (الإصدار 3.2) المعتمد من سعادة المدير العام بتاريخ 2 مارس 2025، ولا يُغني عن الرجوع إلى نصوص المواد.

**أولاً: حدود الشراء وصلاحيات الاعتماد** (القيم غير شاملة لضريبة القيمة المضافة)
- **الشراء المباشر:** حتى 100,000 درهم؛ يُكتفى بعرض سعر واحد حتى 20,000 درهم، وتُشترط ثلاثة عروض أسعار مكتوبة من 20,001 إلى 100,000 درهم.
- **المناقصة المحدودة:** حتى 1,000,000 درهم، بدعوة ثلاثة موردين مسجلين على الأقل بموجب طلب عروض أسعار.
- **المناقصة العامة:** لما يزيد على 1,000,000 درهم، تُطرح عبر بوابة الموردين لمدة لا تقل عن 21 يوماً.
- يُحظر تجزئة المتطلبات بقصد التحايل على طريقة الشراء أو صلاحية الاعتماد.
- **الاعتماد:** رئيس القسم حتى 100,000 درهم؛ مدير إدارة المشتريات والعقود حتى 1,000,000 درهم؛ لجنة المناقصات وسعادة المدير العام حتى 10,000,000 درهم؛ وما زاد على ذلك يُخطَر به مجلس الإدارة.

**ثانياً: تضارب المصالح**
- يلتزم كل عضو في لجان المناقصات والتقييم بتوقيع إقرار المصالح والسرية قبل فتح العطاءات.
- يتنحى العضو عن أي إجراء تكون له أو لأحد أقاربه حتى الدرجة الرابعة مصلحة فيه، ويقرر رئيس اللجنة استبداله.
- لا تُقبل أي هدية من مقدم عطاء خلال فترة المناقصة، وتُقيَّد الهدايا الرمزية التي لا تتجاوز 500 درهم في سجل الهدايا، ويُعاد ما زاد عليها.
- تُحفظ سجلات المشتريات ومسار التدقيق مدة سبع سنوات.
