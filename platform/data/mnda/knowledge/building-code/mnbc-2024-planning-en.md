# Marsa Al Noor Building Code 2024 (MNBC-2024) — Planning & External Appearance Excerpts

**Issued by:** Marsa Al Noor Development Authority — Planning & Building Permits Department
**Legal basis:** Law No. 4 of 2011 establishing the Marsa Al Noor Development Authority
**Issued:** 1 March 2024 · **Effective:** 1 June 2024
**Edition:** Consolidated text incorporating Amendment No. 1/2026 (effective 1 September 2026)

## About this excerpt

This document reproduces the provisions of MNBC-2024 that are applied in drawing review for Building Permits
(MNDA-SVC-101) and Fit-out Permits (MNDA-SVC-102), and at the final inspection for the Building Completion
Certificate (MNDA-SVC-103): Part 3 (Plot Development), Part 5 (External Appearance) and Part 7 (Compliance), with
key definitions from Part 1. Part 4 (Industrial plots in MN-05), Part 6 (Structural and MEP) and the annexes are not
reproduced.

Where both this code and a master developer's community design guidelines apply, **the stricter requirement
governs**. Cross-references to circulars were updated editorially in the consolidated edition of September 2026.
Terminology follows the MNDA glossary. In case of any discrepancy between the Arabic and English texts, the Arabic
text prevails.

## Article 1.3 — Key definitions (extract)

- **Plot:** a land parcel registered with the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry, identified by a plot number in the format
  MN-DD-SSS-PPPP (district-sector-plot).
- **Setback:** the minimum horizontal distance between the plot boundary and the outermost face of the building,
  excluding permitted projections.
- **Plot coverage:** the area of the building footprint, including all roofed areas, as a percentage of the plot area.
- **Gross floor area (GFA):** the sum of all floor areas measured to the external face of external walls, excluding
  basements used for parking or plant and open roof terraces.
- **Podium:** the base of a high-rise building, not exceeding 5 floors above ground, typically containing parking,
  retail and amenities. **Tower:** the part of the building above the podium.
- **Height to parapet:** measured from the crown of the adjacent road at the mid-point of the plot frontage to the top
  of the roof parapet.
- **Gated community:** a district developed and managed by a master developer under a master development agreement with
  the Authority — MN-03 Al Lulu Island, MN-06 Al Sidr Residential and MN-08 Marina Quarter.
- **Community design guidelines:** the design rules issued by a master developer and registered with the Authority.

## Article 3.2 — Setbacks: villa plots

**Part 3 — Plot Development.** 3.2.1 Minimum setbacks for villa plots are:

| Zone | Districts | Front | Side | Rear |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Villa plots | MN-02 Al Murjan, MN-04 Al Waha Gardens, MN-07 Al Ghaf Community | 4.5 m | 1.5 m | 3.0 m |
| Gated villa communities | MN-03 Al Lulu Island, MN-06 Al Sidr Residential | 6.0 m | 2.0 m | 3.0 m |

3.2.2 In gated villa communities, the setbacks of the community design guidelines apply where they are stricter.

3.2.3 Permitted projections into setbacks: sunshades, cornices and architectural fins up to 0.6 m. Habitable rooms,
external staircases and balconies are not permitted within side setbacks.

3.2.4 On corner villa plots, the setback on the secondary street frontage may be reduced to 3.0 m.

3.2.5 Open-sided car shades not exceeding 3.0 m in height are permitted within the front setback of villa plots in
MN-02, MN-04 and MN-07; in gated communities only where the community design guidelines allow them.

## Article 3.2 — Setbacks: high-rise and mixed-use plots

3.2.6 On plots in **MN-08 Marina Quarter** and **MN-01 Downtown**, the following minimum setbacks and separations apply:

| Element | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Podium — from every plot boundary | 3.0 m |
| Tower above podium — from every plot boundary | 6.0 m |
| Separation between towers (on the same plot or on adjoining plots) | 12.0 m |

3.2.7 The tower setback is measured from the plot boundary to the outermost face of the tower floor plate, including
balconies and enclosed winter gardens.

3.2.8 In MN-08, the community design guidelines of Marina Quarter Properties LLC apply in addition, where stricter.

3.2.9 Basements may extend to the plot boundary provided that no part of the basement structure, ramps or ventilation
openings projects above finished ground level within the setback.

## Article 3.3 — Plot coverage

3.3.1 The building footprint must not exceed the following percentage of the plot area:

| Building type | Districts | Maximum plot coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Villas | MN-02, MN-04, MN-07 | 60% |
| Villas in gated communities | MN-03, MN-06 | 50% |
| Commercial and mixed-use — podium | MN-01, MN-08 | 70% |
| Towers — floor plate above podium | MN-01, MN-08 | 45% |

3.3.2 Plot coverage includes every roofed structure on the plot — the main building, annexes, majlis, service blocks
and covered terraces.

3.3.3 Excluded from plot coverage: open-sided car shades up to 30 m² in total, pergolas with at least 50% of the roof
open, and uncovered swimming pools.

3.3.4 Example: on a 720 m² villa plot in MN-02, the maximum footprint is 432 m²; on a 720 m² plot in MN-06 it is 360 m².

## Article 3.4 — Building heights

3.4.1 Maximum heights are:

| Zone | Maximum height |
|---|---|
| Villas — MN-02, MN-03, MN-04, MN-06, MN-07 | G+1, and 12.0 m to the top of the parapet |
| Villas — MN-04 corner plots of 600 m² or more | G+2 permitted, 15.0 m to the top of the parapet |
| Towers — MN-08 Marina Quarter | As designated by the height zones of the Marina Quarter master plan; absolute maximum **45 floors** |
| Towers — MN-01 Downtown | As designated by the Downtown Height Plan |

3.4.2 G+2 villas are not permitted in any district other than MN-04, and only on corner plots of at least 600 m².

3.4.3 Marina Quarter master plan height zones: Zone A (waterfront) up to 45 floors; Zone B up to 30 floors; Zone C
(inland edge) up to 16 floors.

3.4.4 A roof stair enclosure or lift overrun is excluded from the height limit where it does not exceed 3.0 m in
height and 15% of the roof area, and is set back at least 2.0 m from the front façade.

## Article 3.5 — Parking

3.5.1 Minimum parking provision:

| Use | Minimum spaces |
|---|---|
| Villa | 2 per unit |
| Apartment — studio or 1 bedroom | 1 per unit |
| Apartment — 2 bedrooms | 1.5 per unit |
| Apartment — 3 bedrooms or more | 2 per unit |
| Offices | 1 per 45 m² GFA |
| Retail | 1 per 30 m² GFA |
| Visitor parking (apartment and office buildings) | Additional 10% of the required spaces |

3.5.2 **Accessible bays:** 2% of all spaces (minimum one), each 3.6 m × 5.5 m, located nearest to the accessible entrance.

3.5.3 **EV-ready bays:** in new buildings whose building permit application is submitted on or after 1 January 2025,
10% of spaces must be EV-ready — dedicated conduit, cable route and reserved electrical capacity for a 7 kW charger
per space.

3.5.4 Standard bays are 2.5 m × 5.5 m. Fractions are rounded up. Example: 40 two-bedroom apartments require 60 spaces
plus 6 visitor spaces = 66, of which at least 2 accessible bays and 7 EV-ready bays.

## Article 5.4 — Roof equipment screening

**Part 5 — External Appearance.**

5.4.1 All rooftop plant — including chillers, AC condensing units, water tanks, pumps and satellite dishes — must be
**fully screened from street level and from adjacent plots**.

5.4.2 Screens must be **permanent** and match the façade materials and colours of the building.

5.4.3 The minimum screen height is the **height of the tallest screened equipment + 0.3 m**.

5.4.4 Louvred screens must have an opacity of at least **50%** when viewed perpendicular to the screen. Screens must
not use solar-reflective or polished surfaces that cause glare.

5.4.5 Screens are excluded from the height limit of Article 3.4 where set back at least 1.5 m from the roof edge.

5.4.6 *(Added by Amendment No. 1/2026, effective 1 September 2026.)* In gated communities (MN-03, MN-06, MN-08),
screens must be **integrated into the architectural design** of the building and **shown on the permit drawings**
(roof plan and elevations). **Temporary or fabric screens are not accepted.**

## Article 5.5 — Façades in gated communities

5.5.1 In MN-03 Al Lulu Island, MN-06 Al Sidr Residential and MN-08 Marina Quarter, façades must comply with the master
developer's **community design guidelines**, including:

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Material palette | Only materials listed in the community design guidelines |
| Colour range | Within the colour range approved in the guidelines |
| Glazing reflectivity | External visible light reflectance **not exceeding 15%** |
| Boundary walls | **Maximum 2.4 m** high, measured from finished ground level outside the plot; finish as per the guidelines |

5.5.2 Design compliance with the community design guidelines is confirmed before permit issue through the
master-developer NOC (Article 7.2 and Circular No. 14/2026).

5.5.3 Any later change to façade colours, cladding or boundary walls requires a new master-developer NOC and a
modification permit for external alterations.

## Article 5.6 — Signage

5.6.1 Every building must display the plot number (MN-DD-SSS-PPPP) and the building number on a plaque near the main
entrance, legible from the street.

5.6.2 Commercial signs on façades are limited to one sign per tenant per street frontage, placed within the sign zone
shown on the approved permit or fit-out permit drawings.

5.6.3 Signs are not permitted on roofs or above the parapet. Flashing, moving or animated illuminated signs are not
permitted. In residential districts, illuminated signs must be switched off between 23:00 and 06:00.

5.6.4 In villa districts, commercial signs are not permitted; a licensed home business may display a plaque not
exceeding 0.3 m².

5.6.5 In gated communities, the master developer's signage guidelines apply in addition.

## Article 7.2 — Master-developer design compliance

**Part 7 — Compliance.**

7.2.1 For plots in MN-03, MN-06 and MN-08, the following applications must include a master-developer NOC confirming
compliance with the community design guidelines, in accordance with **Circular No. 14/2026**: building permits for new
construction; fit-out permits where façade, roof or external works are affected; and modification permits for
external alterations.

7.2.2 The NOC must be issued on the master developer's letterhead, quote the plot number and the drawing revision it
approves, and be valid at submission (**90 days** from issue). A drawing revision mismatch requires a new NOC.

7.2.3 Master developers are requested to issue NOCs within 7 working days of a complete request. Applications may be
held as "Awaiting master-developer NOC" for up to 20 working days, with the service-level clock suspended.

7.2.4 At completion, the master developer issues a final compliance letter, submitted with the application for the
Building Completion Certificate (Article 7.4).

## Article 7.4 — Completion inspection

7.4.1 No building may be occupied before a Building Completion Certificate (MNDA-SVC-103) is issued.

7.4.2 The Field Operations & Inspection Department carries out a final inspection verifying compliance with the
approved drawings and with Articles 3.2 to 3.5, 5.4 to 5.6 and, in gated communities, the community design guidelines.

7.4.3 Where snags are found, a snag list is issued. The re-inspection is carried out within **5 working days** of the
consultant's request.

7.4.4 Works that deviate materially from the approved drawings must be rectified, or regularised through a
modification permit, before the certificate can be issued.

7.4.5 Certificates are issued digitally with a QR code and a reference in the format MNDA-BCC-YYYY-NNNNN.

## Amendments

| Amendment | Issued | Effective | Articles | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No. 1/2026 (ref. MNDA/PBP/MNBC/AMD/01/2026) | 4 August 2026 | 1 September 2026 | 5.4 (new clause 5.4.6) | Roof equipment screening tightened for gated communities MN-03, MN-06 and MN-08 |

Under Amendment No. 1/2026, roof equipment screens in gated communities must be integrated into the architectural design
and shown on the permit drawings; temporary or fabric screens are not accepted. The amendment applies to building
permit and fit-out permit applications submitted on or after 1 September 2026. Applications submitted earlier are
assessed under clauses 5.4.1–5.4.5, which already require permanent screens. Amendment No. 1/2026 is the first
amendment to MNBC-2024.
