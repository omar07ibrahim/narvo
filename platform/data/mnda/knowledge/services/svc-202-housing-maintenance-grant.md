# Housing Maintenance Grant (MNDA-SVC-202)

A non-repayable grant of up to **AED 150,000** for UAE national families to carry out essential repairs to the home
they own and live in in Marsa Al Noor. Arabic service name: منحة صيانة المسكن. Service level: **15 working days** from
a complete application to decision. No fees.

## Who can apply

1. The applicant is a UAE national, aged 21 or above, and the head of the household.
2. The applicant owns a **single home**, located in Marsa Al Noor and used as the family's main residence. Neither the
   applicant nor the spouse owns any other residential property in the UAE.
3. The home is **15 years old or more** (from the date of its completion certificate or the Land Registry building
   record), **or** it needs essential repairs confirmed by the Authority's site technical assessment, whatever its age.
4. Total monthly household income does not exceed **AED 30,000**.
5. No Housing Maintenance Grant has been paid for the same home in the previous 10 years.

Accessibility adaptations for a family member of determination are eligible regardless of the age of the home.

## Eligible works and grant amount

| Category | Examples of eligible works |
|---|---|
| Structural repairs | Spalling concrete, corroded reinforcement, cracked slabs, beams or columns, settlement damage |
| Waterproofing | Roof membrane replacement, wet-area waterproofing, water tank leaks |
| Electrical | Rewiring, distribution board replacement, earthing to current standards |
| AC replacement | Replacement of failed or end-of-life split or package units |
| Accessibility adaptations for people of determination | Ramps, grab rails, accessible bathroom, door widening, stair lift |

**Not eligible:** extensions or new rooms, majlis or annex construction, cosmetic works on their own (painting,
decorative finishes), landscaping, swimming pools, furniture and appliances (other than AC units), and any works
started before the site technical assessment.

**Grant amount:** up to AED 150,000 per home, set by the approved scope in the technical assessment report. If the
owner's chosen quotation is higher than the approved amount, the owner pays the difference directly to the
contractor before mobilisation.

## Required documents

| # | Document | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emirates ID — applicant and spouse | Valid, front and back |
| 2 | Family Book | Complete copy, all pages including children |
| 3 | Title deed | Marsa Al Noor Land Registry; if the home is co-owned, NOC from every co-owner |
| 4 | Salary certificate | **Issued within the last 30 days**, for both spouses if both are employed (same rule as Circular No. 9/2026); pension statement for retirees |
| 5 | Two contractor quotations | From **MNDA-registered contractors**, itemised, on letterhead with the trade licence number, valid at least 60 days |
| 6 | Photographs | Clear, dated photos of each item to be repaired |
| 7 | People of Determination card or medical report | Accessibility adaptations only; medical report issued within the last 6 months |

## Process

1. **Submission** through MNDA Smart → Housing → Maintenance Grant, or at any Customer Happiness Centre. A case number
   is issued immediately.
2. **Completeness check and assignment** to a case officer — within 1 working day.
3. **Eligibility verification** — household income, property ownership (Marsa Al Noor Land Registry) and age of the home.
4. **Site technical assessment** by Housing & Community Services engineers, normally within 5 working days. The
   assessment report confirms the eligible scope, its priority and the approved amount, and checks both quotations
   against that scope.
5. **Decision** by the Head of the Housing Applications Section or an authorised deputy. Automated tools may prepare
   checks and drafts but may not approve or reject an application (MNDA Responsible AI Policy, AI-POL-01).
6. **Tripartite agreement** signed by the owner, the selected contractor and the Authority. Works must start within
   30 days of signing.
7. **Works and payment** — works are supervised by an MNDA engineer; payments are made in instalments directly to the
   contractor (see below).

## Payment schedule

The grant is paid **directly to the contractor**, never to the owner, in three instalments:

| Instalment | Share | Released when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 40% | Tripartite agreement signed and contractor mobilised on site (start-of-works visit by the MNDA engineer) |
| 2 | 40% | 60% of the approved works completed, verified on site by the MNDA engineer |
| 3 | 20% | Final handover inspection passed and owner's acceptance signed |

Example: for an approved amount of AED 118,400, the contractor receives AED 47,360, AED 47,360 and AED 23,680.
The contractor remains responsible for defects in the works for 12 months after handover; the owner reports defects
through MNDA Smart or 800 6632 quoting the case number.

## Service level and fees

- **Service level:** 15 working days from a **complete** application to decision, including the site technical
  assessment. The duration of the works is not counted.
- **Incomplete applications:** the case is marked "Incomplete — awaiting applicant" and the clock stops. The applicant
  has 15 working days to provide the missing items; otherwise the case is closed and may be re-submitted.
- **Fees:** none.

## Tracking and status meanings

Case numbers use the same format as other housing cases, e.g. **MNDA-HG-2026-005233**. Check status in MNDA Smart →
My Applications, by WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000 (send the case number) or by calling 800 6632.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Received; completeness check in progress |
| Under review | Assigned to a case officer |
| Incomplete — awaiting applicant | Documents missing or invalid; 15 working days to respond |
| Verification | Income, Land Registry ownership and home age checks |
| Site assessment scheduled | Engineer's visit booked; applicant receives the date by SMS |
| Decision pending | With the Section Head for decision |
| Approved — agreement pending | Tripartite agreement to be signed |
| Works in progress | Contractor on site; instalments released per schedule |
| Completed / Not approved | Case closed; the applicant receives a letter |

## Most common reasons for delay

1. Salary certificate older than 30 days on the date of submission.
2. Quotation from a contractor that is not registered with MNDA, or quotations that are not itemised.
3. Co-owned title deed without the NOC of every co-owner.
4. Photographs that do not clearly show the defect.
5. Applicant not available at the booked site assessment; the visit is rebooked once.
