# Municipal Maintenance Request (MNDA-SVC-502)

Residents, businesses and visitors can report faults in public streets, parks and public spaces maintained by the
Authority. Arabic service name: طلب صيانة بلدية. Requests are handled by the Field Operations & Inspection Department.
Service level: **3 working days**; emergencies made safe **within 4 hours**. No fees.

## What you can report

| Category | Examples | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Street lighting | Lights out or flickering, a whole street dark, damaged pole | 3 working days |
| Potholes and road surface | Potholes, broken kerbs, sunken road surface | 3 working days (made safe or temporarily repaired) |
| Park irrigation leaks | Burst irrigation pipes, sprinklers running at midday, flooded paths | 3 working days |
| Damaged signage | Road signs, street name signs, direction signs damaged or missing | 3 working days |
| Stray shopping trolleys | Abandoned trolleys in streets, parks or car parks | 3 working days |
| Drainage blockages | Blocked gullies or stormwater drains, standing water | 3 working days |

## Emergencies — call 800 6632

The following are **emergencies** and must be reported by phone on **800 6632**, which takes emergency calls 24 hours
a day:

- **exposed or damaged electrical cables** — for example an open street-light pole hatch or a cable cut during works;
- **flooding** of roads, underpasses or property from the public drainage network;
- **open or missing manhole covers**;
- a fallen light pole, sign or tree blocking a road or footpath.

A field crew attends and makes the location safe **within 4 hours** — barriers and warning signs, isolation of power
with the utility provider, temporary covers or pumping. Keep a safe distance and do not touch exposed cables. Where
there is an immediate danger to life, call the emergency services first.

## How to report

**MNDA Smart (recommended):** Services → Report an Issue → choose the category, take or attach a **photo**, drop a
**location pin** (or allow GPS) and add a short description.

**WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000:** send a photo, share your location and describe the problem.

**Other channels:** hotline 800 6632, chc@mnda.gov.ae, or any Customer Happiness Centre (Downtown, Al Murjan, Al Waha).

Tips that speed up the repair: give the nearest landmark or plot number (e.g. MN-04-117-0031); for street lighting,
quote the pole number painted on the pole (e.g. SL-04-1182); for a pothole, say which lane is affected.

## What happens after you report

1. **Ticket issued** immediately, in the format **CHC-2026-NNNNNN** (e.g. CHC-2026-119637).
2. **Triage** by the Field Operations control room on the same working day. Duplicate reports of the same fault are
   merged; you are linked to the existing ticket and receive its updates.
3. **Dispatch** to a Field Operations & Inspection crew or to the term maintenance contractor.
4. **Make safe or repair**, with before-and-after photos uploaded to the ticket.
5. **Closure notification** with the "after" photo, followed by a short satisfaction survey.

Where a permanent repair needs larger works — road resurfacing, cable replacement, drainage re-laying — the location is
made safe within the service level and the permanent repair is scheduled in the planned maintenance programme; the
ticket shows the planned date.

Stray shopping trolleys: the retailer is notified to collect them within 24 hours; trolleys not collected are removed
by the Authority and repeated cases are referred for inspection.

## Service levels and summer working

| Type | Target |
|---|---|
| Emergency (exposed cables, flooding, open manholes) | Made safe within **4 hours**, 24 hours a day, via 800 6632 |
| Standard request | Repaired or made safe within **3 working days** |
| Permanent repair needing planned works | Date communicated on the ticket |

**Summer midday work ban (15 June – 15 September):** non-emergency outdoor work is not carried out between **12:30 and
15:00**; crews work from 07:00 to 12:30 and after 15:00. Emergency make-safe work continues during the ban period
with heat-stress controls (Circular No. 11/2026, section 6). The 3-working-day target is unchanged.

## Areas outside MNDA maintenance

- **Gated communities:** internal roads, street lighting, landscaping and irrigation in MN-03 Al Lulu Island, MN-06
  Al Sidr Residential and MN-08 Marina Quarter are maintained by the master developers — Lulu Island Developments PJSC,
  Sidr Communities LLC and Marina Quarter Properties LLC. The Authority forwards such reports within 1 working day and
  closes the ticket with the master developer's reference.
- **Electricity or water supply to a property:** forwarded to the utility provider.
- **Private property and building common areas:** the responsibility of the owner or building management.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Received | Ticket created; awaiting triage |
| Assigned | Allocated to a field crew or the maintenance contractor |
| Crew dispatched | Crew on the way or on site |
| Made safe — permanent repair scheduled | Hazard removed; planned repair date shown |
| Resolved | Repair complete; "after" photo attached |
| Forwarded to master developer | Location inside MN-03, MN-06 or MN-08 |
| Forwarded to utility provider | Supply fault outside MNDA responsibility |
| Closed | Ticket closed; can be reopened within 7 days |
| Reopened | Fault recurred or was not fixed; reassigned with priority |

## Channels and contacts

- **Hotline** 800 6632 (800 MNDA) — 24 hours for emergencies; Monday – Thursday 7:30 – 15:30, Friday 7:30 – 12:00 for other requests.
- **WhatsApp** +971 50 663 2000 · **MNDA Smart app** → Report an Issue · **Email** chc@mnda.gov.ae
- **Customer Happiness Centres** — Downtown, Al Murjan and Al Waha; Monday – Thursday 7:30 – 15:30, Friday 7:30 – 12:00.

Complaints about the handling of a maintenance request are submitted as a Complaint (MNDA-SVC-501), quoting the ticket number.
