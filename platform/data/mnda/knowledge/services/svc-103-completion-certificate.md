# Building Completion Certificate (MNDA-SVC-103)

Issued when construction under a building permit (MNDA-SVC-101) is complete and the building has been built in
accordance with the approved drawings and the Marsa Al Noor Building Code 2024 (MNBC-2024). Arabic service name:
شهادة إنجاز مبنى. A building may not be occupied before the certificate is issued, and the certificate is required to
register the completed building with the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry. Service level: **10 working days**.
Fee: **AED 1,000**.

## Who applies

The owner, through the **licensed engineering consultant who supervised the works** (consultant of record for
supervision). The contractor cannot apply directly. The building permit must have been valid when the works were
completed; if the permit has expired, the consultant must first apply for an extension.

For homes built under the Housing Grant & Loan Programme (MNDA-SVC-201), the completion certificate is the
document that allows the final disbursement to be released.

## Readiness checklist before applying

Apply only when all of the following are in place, otherwise the final inspection will fail:

- all works complete, including external works, boundary walls, landscaping and parking;
- parking bays marked, including accessible bays (2%) and, for permits applied for from 1 January 2025, EV-ready
  bays (10%) under MNBC-2024 Article 3.5;
- rooftop plant, chillers, water tanks and satellite dishes permanently screened (MNBC-2024 Article 5.4); in
  gated communities, for permits applied for from 1 September 2026, screens integrated into the architecture as
  shown on the permit drawings (Amendment No. 1/2026);
- temporary utility connections replaced by permanent connections;
- Civil Defence completion inspection passed;
- site cleared, hoarding and site offices removed, pavements, kerbs and driveways reinstated.

## Required documents

| # | Document | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Building permit reference (e.g. MNDA-BP-2026-01932) | Linked automatically to the application |
| 2 | As-built drawings — architectural, structural, MEP | Stamped by the supervising consultant, showing all approved revisions |
| 3 | Final site survey by a licensed land surveyor | As-built footprint, setbacks and heights |
| 4 | Consultant's completion report | Signed statement that the works follow the approved drawings, list of approved variations, summary of material test results |
| 5 | Civil Defence completion certificate | Always |
| 6 | Utility connection certificates | Electricity, water, sewerage; district cooling where applicable |
| 7 | Lift and escalator test certificates | Where installed, from an accredited third-party inspection body |
| 8 | Master-developer final compliance letter | Plots in MN-03, MN-06 and MN-08 only |

## Master-developer final compliance letter (gated communities)

For plots in **MN-03 Al Lulu Island** (Lulu Island Developments PJSC), **MN-06 Al Sidr Residential** (Sidr
Communities LLC) and **MN-08 Marina Quarter** (Marina Quarter Properties LLC), the application must include a final
compliance letter from the master developer. It complements the design-stage NOC required by Circular No. 14/2026
and confirms that the completed building matches the approved drawing revision and the community design
guidelines, in particular:

- façade materials and colours within the approved palette, and glazing reflectivity of 15% or less;
- roof equipment screening — permanent, integrated screens, no fabric or temporary screens;
- boundary walls — maximum 2.4 m high, finish as per the guidelines.

The letter must be on the master developer's letterhead and quote the plot number (MN-DD-SSS-PPPP) and the
building permit reference. Consultants are advised to request it at least 10 working days before applying.

## Process

1. **Submission** through MNDA Smart or the consultant portal on mnda.gov.ae, with all documents and the AED 1,000 fee.
2. **Completeness check** — within 1 working day.
3. **Document review** by the Planning & Building Permits Department.
4. **Final inspection** by the Field Operations & Inspection Department, scheduled within 3 working days of the
   document review. The supervising consultant and the contractor's site representative must attend.
5. **Result:**
   - *Pass* — the certificate is issued.
   - *Snags* — a snag list is issued in MNDA Smart. Once the snags are closed, the consultant requests a
     re-inspection, which is carried out **within 5 working days** of the request.
   - *Major deviation* — works that differ materially from the approved drawings require rectification or a
     modification permit before the application can continue.
6. **Certificate issue** — digital certificate with QR code, reference **MNDA-BCC-2026-NNNNN**, shared
   electronically with the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry.

## What the final inspection checks

| Area | What inspectors verify |
|---|---|
| Plot development | Setbacks (MNBC-2024 Art. 3.2), plot coverage (Art. 3.3), number of floors and height to parapet (Art. 3.4) |
| Parking | Number of spaces, visitor spaces, 2% accessible bays, 10% EV-ready bays where applicable (Art. 3.5) |
| External appearance | Façade materials as approved, roof equipment screening (Art. 5.4), gated-community façade rules (Art. 5.5), signage and plot number plaque (Art. 5.6) |
| Safety and access | Balustrades and handrails, accessible routes and ramps, fire exits clear and signed |
| Public realm | Pavements, kerbs and driveways reinstated; no damage to street lighting, signage or drainage; site fully cleared |

## Most common snags

1. Rooftop equipment not screened, screened on one side only, or screened with fabric or temporary panels.
2. Accessible parking bays missing, undersized or not marked; EV-ready conduits not installed.
3. Boundary wall higher than 2.4 m in a gated community, or finished in an unapproved colour.
4. Façade colour or cladding different from the approved drawings or the community guidelines.
5. Missing balustrades, handrails or plot number plaque.
6. Site debris, hoarding or damaged pavement left at the plot frontage.

## Service level and fees

- **Service level:** 10 working days from a complete application to certificate issue.
- The clock stops while the application is "Returned — documents incomplete" or "Snags — awaiting rectification".
- **Re-inspection:** within 5 working days of the consultant's re-inspection request.
- **Fee:** AED 1,000, paid on submission. The fee covers the final inspection and the re-inspection of the snag list.

## Tracking and status meanings

Certificate applications use the reference **MNDA-BCC-2026-00871** (example). Check status in MNDA Smart → My
Applications, by WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000 or by calling 800 6632.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Received; completeness check in progress |
| Returned — documents incomplete | A document is missing or invalid; consultant notified |
| Document review | As-built drawings and certificates under review |
| Inspection scheduled | Final inspection date confirmed in MNDA Smart |
| Snags — awaiting rectification | Snag list issued; clock stopped |
| Re-inspection scheduled | Re-inspection within 5 working days of request |
| Certificate issued | Building may be occupied and registered |
| On hold — major deviation | Rectification or modification permit required |
