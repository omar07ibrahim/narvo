# Building Permit — New Construction (MNDA-SVC-101)

Required before any new building works start on a plot in Marsa Al Noor.

## Who applies

The plot owner through a consultant licensed by the Authority (engineering consultancy registered with MNDA).

## Required documents

1. Owner Emirates ID / trade licence (companies).
2. Title deed from the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry.
3. Approved affection (site) plan — latest revision.
4. Architectural drawings stamped by the licensed consultant.
5. Structural design and calculations **stamped and signed** by a registered structural engineer.
6. Civil Defence preliminary approval (fire & life safety).
7. Contractor trade licence and MNDA contractor classification.
8. **Master-developer NOC** for plots in MN-03, MN-06 and MN-08 (Circular No. 14/2026), valid 90 days and matching
   the submitted drawing revision.

## Process

1. Submission and fee payment.
2. Completeness check (1 working day).
3. Planning review — setbacks, plot coverage, height, parking.
4. Structural review.
5. Civil Defence coordination.
6. Permit issue (digital permit with QR code).

## Service level and fees

15 working days for a complete application. Fee: **AED 1,500 + AED 3 per m² of gross floor area**.

## Frequent reasons applications are returned (Q3 2026)

Master-developer NOC missing or expired (46% of returns); structural calculations not stamped (18%); site plan
revision mismatch (14%).

## Tracking

Application numbers look like **MNDA-BP-2026-01932**. Status via MNDA Smart, permits@mnda.gov.ae or 800 6632.
