# Fit-out Permit (MNDA-SVC-102)

Required before interior fit-out works start in an existing building or unit in Marsa Al Noor — villas, apartments,
offices, shops, restaurants, clinics and community facilities. Arabic service name: رخصة تشطيبات داخلية.
Service level: **5 working days**. Fee: **AED 800**.

## Works that need a fit-out permit

A fit-out permit is required for:

- new, removed or relocated partitions, false ceilings and raised floors;
- relocation of kitchens, washrooms or other wet areas;
- changes to HVAC ductwork, lighting or power layouts, or any increase in electrical load;
- any change to fire alarm, sprinkler, smoke control or emergency lighting systems;
- shop, restaurant and clinic fit-outs, including kitchen exhaust systems;
- fit-out works that affect the façade, roof or external areas — for example new rooftop AC condensing units,
  façade louvres or exhaust outlets, external shopfronts and shade structures on terraces.

**No permit is needed** for painting, like-for-like replacement of floor and wall finishes, sanitary fittings or light
fittings, loose furniture, or minor repairs that do not touch MEP or fire systems.

**A different permit is needed** for structural alterations (new openings in slabs or load-bearing walls, mezzanines,
extensions, additional floors) and for external alterations without interior fit-out (modification permit).

## Who applies

The owner of the unit, or the tenant with the owner's written NOC. The application is submitted on their behalf by:

- a **licensed engineering consultant** registered with MNDA — required for all works that modify fire systems or
  affect the façade, roof or external areas; or
- an **MNDA-approved fit-out contractor** — for interior-only works with no change to fire and life-safety systems.

The contractor carrying out the works must hold a valid trade licence and MNDA contractor registration, whoever
submits the application.

## Required documents

| # | Document | When required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emirates ID (individuals) or trade licence, e.g. CN-2194407 (companies) | Always |
| 2 | Title deed from the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry, or registered tenancy contract | Always |
| 3 | Owner / landlord NOC for the proposed works | Applicant is not the owner |
| 4 | Building management NOC (owners' association or building manager) | Units in towers and multi-tenant buildings |
| 5 | Fit-out drawings — layout, reflected ceiling plan, MEP layouts, sections — stamped by the licensed consultant or approved fit-out contractor | Always |
| 6 | Civil Defence approval of the fire system changes | Fire alarm, sprinkler, smoke control or emergency lighting modified |
| 7 | Master-developer NOC — valid 90 days, matching the submitted drawing revision | **Only** façade, roof or external works in MN-03, MN-06, MN-08 |
| 8 | Contractor trade licence and MNDA contractor registration | Always |

## Master-developer NOC — when it applies (Circular No. 14/2026 §3)

Under Circular No. 14/2026, a fit-out permit needs a master-developer NOC **only** where the works affect the
façade, roof or external areas of a plot in a gated community. Interior-only fit-outs do not need it, even in gated
districts; the building management NOC (document 4) is still required for towers.

| Example | District | Master-developer NOC? |
|---|---|---|
| Apartment interior fit-out in a Marina Quarter tower, no external change | MN-08 | No |
| Villa kitchen relocation, no new external openings | MN-06 | No |
| New rooftop AC condensing units on a villa | MN-03 | **Yes** — Lulu Island Developments PJSC |
| Restaurant with kitchen exhaust outlet through the façade | MN-08 | **Yes** — Marina Quarter Properties LLC |
| Office fit-out in a Downtown tower | MN-01 | No — district not covered |

The NOC must be on the master developer's letterhead, quote the plot number (format MN-DD-SSS-PPPP) and the drawing
revision it approves, and be valid (90 days from issue). A revision mismatch requires a new NOC.

## Process

1. **Submission** through MNDA Smart or the consultant portal on mnda.gov.ae, with all documents and the AED 800 fee.
2. **Completeness check** — within 1 working day.
3. **Technical review** — layout, means of escape, accessibility, MEP loads and, where rooftop equipment is added,
   roof equipment screening under MNBC-2024 Article 5.4.
4. **Civil Defence coordination** where fire systems are modified.
5. **Permit issue** — digital permit with QR code, to be displayed at the unit entrance for the duration of the works.
6. **Works** — Field Operations & Inspection may carry out unannounced site checks.
7. **Pre-occupancy inspection** — requested in MNDA Smart when works are complete and carried out within 3 working
   days. On a pass, a Fit-out Completion Notice is issued and the unit may be occupied or open for business. If the
   inspection fails, a snag list is issued and a re-inspection is requested once the snags are closed.

## Service level, fees and validity

- **Service level:** 5 working days from a complete application to permit issue or return.
- **Fee:** AED 800 per permit, paid on submission.
- **Clock stops** while the application is "Returned — NOC required", "Returned to applicant" or "Awaiting
  master-developer NOC" (maximum 20 working days, per Circular No. 14/2026).
- **Validity:** 6 months from issue; one extension of 3 months may be requested in MNDA Smart before expiry.
- **Changes during works:** revised drawings are submitted as a permit amendment. Where façade, roof or external
  works in MN-03, MN-06 or MN-08 are affected, the new drawing revision needs a new master-developer NOC.

## Tracking and status meanings

Fit-out permit references look like **MNDA-FO-2026-02764**. Check status in MNDA Smart → My Applications, by
WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000 (send the reference number) or by calling 800 6632.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Received; completeness check in progress |
| Returned — NOC required | Façade, roof or external works in MN-03/MN-06/MN-08 without a valid master-developer NOC |
| Awaiting master-developer NOC | NOC requested but not yet received; held up to 20 working days, clock stopped |
| Returned to applicant | Other documents missing or drawings need correction |
| Under technical review | With a permit officer |
| Civil Defence coordination | Fire system changes under review |
| Permit issued | Works may start |
| Inspection requested / Snags open | Pre-occupancy inspection booked, or snags to be closed |
| Completed | Fit-out Completion Notice issued |

## Frequent reasons applications are returned

1. Owner or landlord NOC missing, unsigned, or signed by someone other than the registered owner.
2. Building management NOC missing for a unit in a tower or multi-tenant building.
3. Drawings not stamped by the licensed consultant or approved fit-out contractor.
4. Fire alarm or sprinkler changes shown on drawings without Civil Defence approval.
5. Master-developer NOC missing or expired for rooftop or façade works in MN-03, MN-06 or MN-08.
6. Drawing revision on the master-developer NOC does not match the revision submitted to the Authority.
7. Contractor not registered with MNDA, or trade licence expired.
