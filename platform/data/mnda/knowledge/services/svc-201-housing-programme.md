# Housing Grant & Loan Programme (MNDA-SVC-201)

Support for UAE national families to build or buy their first home in Marsa Al Noor.

## Who can apply

UAE nationals aged 21+, head of household, who (with their spouse) do not own residential property in the UAE.
Grant track: household income up to AED 30,000 per month (grant up to AED 600,000).
Loan track: household income up to AED 60,000 per month (interest-free loan up to AED 900,000 over up to 25 years).
Full rules: Circular No. 9/2026.

## Channels

MNDA Smart app (recommended), mnda.gov.ae, or any Customer Happiness Centre (Downtown, Al Murjan, Al Waha),
Monday–Thursday 7:30–15:30, Friday 7:30–12:00.

## Steps

1. Submit the application with all required documents (see Circular 9/2026, section 4).
2. Automatic completeness check and assignment to a case officer (within 1 working day).
3. Eligibility verification — income, property ownership (Land Registry), family composition.
4. Site or plot verification where applicable.
5. Decision by the Head of the Housing Applications Section.
6. Signing of the grant/loan agreement and disbursement schedule.

## Service level

12 working days from a **complete** application to decision. The clock stops while the application is
"Incomplete — awaiting applicant".

## Fees

No fees.

## Tracking

Applicants can check status in MNDA Smart → My Applications, by WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000 (send the case number),
or by calling 800 6632. Case numbers look like **MNDA-HG-2026-004817**.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Received, completeness check in progress |
| Under review | Assigned to a case officer |
| Incomplete — awaiting applicant | Documents missing or invalid; applicant notified; 15 working days to respond |
| Verification | Land Registry / income verification in progress |
| Decision pending | With the Section Head for decision |
| Approved / Not approved | Decision issued; the applicant receives a letter |

## Most common reasons for delay (September 2026)

Salary certificate older than 30 days; Certificate of No Property Ownership not attached; spouse Emirates ID
missing; incomplete family book copy.
