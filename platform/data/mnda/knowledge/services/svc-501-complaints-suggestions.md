# Complaint / Suggestion (MNDA-SVC-501)

Customers can submit complaints, suggestions and compliments about any MNDA service, facility or employee. Arabic
service name: شكوى / مقترح. First response within **2 working days**; resolution target **5 working days** (10 working
days for complex cases, with notice). No fees.

## What you can submit

- **Complaint** — dissatisfaction with a service, a delay beyond the published service level, the conduct of an
  employee, the condition of an MNDA facility, or the quality of a municipal service already reported.
- **Suggestion** — an idea to improve a service, a facility, a procedure or the Authority's digital channels.
- **Compliment** — recorded and passed to the employee or team concerned.

**Handled through other routes:**

- Objections to a formal decision (for example a housing "Not approved" decision or a permit refusal) follow the
  grievance procedure stated in the decision letter.
- New maintenance faults — street lighting, potholes, drainage — are reported as a Municipal Maintenance Request
  (MNDA-SVC-502).
- Emergencies such as exposed cables, flooding or open manholes: call **800 6632** immediately.

## What to include

To allow a complaint to be resolved at the first contact, please include:

1. Name and mobile number (for example +971 50 418 2276), and Emirates ID where the complaint relates to an application.
2. The related reference number, if any — housing case (MNDA-HG-2026-…), building permit (MNDA-BP-2026-…), fit-out
   permit (MNDA-FO-2026-…), event permit (MNDA-EV-2026-…) or an earlier ticket (CHC-2026-…).
3. What happened, with date, time and location.
4. What outcome you are asking for.
5. Supporting photos or documents (up to 5 attachments in MNDA Smart).

## Service levels

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement and ticket number | Immediately (digital channels) or at the counter |
| First response — an officer contacts the customer | Within **2 working days** |
| Resolution — standard complaints | Within **5 working days** |
| Resolution — complex complaints (several departments, site visit or legal review) | Within **10 working days**; the customer is notified of the extension before the 5th working day |
| Suggestions | Acknowledged within 2 working days; outcome communicated after the quarterly review |

The complaint is investigated by the department concerned; the Customer Happiness Centre tracks the ticket and keeps
the customer informed until closure.

## Escalation levels

If a ticket receives no response within 2 working days, misses its resolution target, or the customer is not
satisfied with the outcome, the customer may escalate it — through the "Escalate" button on the ticket in MNDA Smart,
by WhatsApp quoting the ticket number, or by calling 800 6632.

| Level | Escalated to | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manager, Customer Happiness Centre — Sara Ahmed Al Blooshi | 2 working days |
| 2 | Director of the department concerned (e.g. Planning & Building Permits, Housing & Community Services, Field Operations & Inspection) | 3 working days |
| 3 | Director General's Office | 5 working days; this is the Authority's final response |

Each level reviews the full ticket history; the customer does not need to repeat the complaint.

## Reopening a ticket and the satisfaction survey

**Reopening:** if the issue recurs or was not actually resolved, the customer can reopen the ticket **within 7 days of
closure** with the same ticket number, through MNDA Smart, WhatsApp or 800 6632. Reopened tickets are assigned to a
supervisor rather than the original officer. After 7 days a new ticket is opened and linked to the earlier one.

**Satisfaction survey:** after closure the customer receives a short survey by SMS or WhatsApp (rating 1 to 5 and an
optional comment). A rating of 1 or 2 triggers a call-back from a supervisor within 2 working days. Survey results
are reported monthly to the Strategy & Performance Office.

## Anonymous suggestions and the Suggestions Reward Scheme

**Anonymous suggestions** are accepted through MNDA Smart ("Submit anonymously") and the suggestion boxes at the
Customer Happiness Centres. A ticket number is still issued, so the outcome can be tracked. Anonymous complaints are
also accepted and investigated where the details allow, but the Authority cannot contact the customer about them.

**Suggestions Reward Scheme:** suggestions are reviewed every quarter by the Suggestions Committee of the Strategy &
Performance Office. Each suggester is told whether the idea was implemented, accepted for study or not adopted, with
the reason. Implemented suggestions receive a certificate of appreciation and a reward of up to AED 5,000 depending on
impact. Anonymous suggesters may claim recognition later by quoting their ticket number. MNDA employees submit ideas
through the internal staff scheme, not this service.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Received | Ticket created; acknowledgement sent |
| Assigned | Allocated to the responsible department and officer |
| In progress | Under investigation; the officer may call the customer |
| Awaiting customer information | Clarification requested; clock paused until the customer replies |
| Extended — complex case | Target moved to 10 working days; customer notified |
| Escalated (Level 1 / 2 / 3) | Under review at the escalation level shown |
| Resolved | Outcome communicated to the customer |
| Closed | Ticket closed; can be reopened within 7 days |
| Reopened | Reopened by the customer; assigned to a supervisor |
