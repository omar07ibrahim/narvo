# Community Event Permit (MNDA-SVC-301)

Required to hold a community event — family and cultural gatherings, national day celebrations, charity events,
sports activities, bazaars and school events — in MNDA community venues and public spaces in Marsa Al Noor.
Arabic service name: تصريح فعالية مجتمعية. Service level: **3 working days**. Fee: **AED 300**, waived for registered
non-profit organisations and school events.

## Venues covered

| Venue | District | Capacity / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Al Ghaf Community Centre — Multipurpose Hall | MN-07 | Up to 420 seated / 600 standing |
| Al Ghaf Community Centre — Majlis Hall | MN-07 | Up to 120 seated |
| Al Ghaf Community Centre — Activity Rooms 1–3 | MN-07 | 30–40 people each |
| Corniche public spaces — Corniche Plaza, promenade, beach lawns | MN-01 | Outdoor; any road use needs police coordination |
| Neighbourhood parks — Al Murjan Park, Al Waha Gardens Central Park, Al Ghaf Park | MN-02, MN-04, MN-07 | Outdoor |

Events in the common areas of gated communities (MN-03, MN-06, MN-08) also need the written consent of the master
developer. Private gatherings inside a private home do not need a permit.

## Who can apply and when

**Who:** individuals (UAE residents aged 21 or above), companies with a valid trade licence, registered non-profit
organisations, schools, and community groups through a named organiser who is responsible on the day.

**When:** at least **10 working days** before the event date. Events above 300 attendees or using public roads need
Civil Defence and police approvals, which take additional time; for these, applying 20 working days ahead is strongly
recommended. A hall at Al Ghaf Community Centre can be held provisionally for 5 working days while the permit
application is processed.

## Requirements

| # | Requirement | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Organiser identification | Emirates ID (individuals), trade licence (companies), registration certificate (non-profits) or letter from the school principal |
| 2 | Event plan | Description, programme, timings including set-up and dismantling, activities, name and mobile of the person in charge on site |
| 3 | Site layout | Stage, seating, entrances and exits, first-aid point, toilets, waste points, vendor stalls, parking |
| 4 | Crowd estimate | Expected peak attendance and registration or ticketing method |
| 5 | Civil Defence approval | Required for events with **more than 300 attendees** |
| 6 | Police coordination letter | Required for any use of public roads — closures, processions, runs, cycling events |
| 7 | Waste management plan | Number of bins, recycling, clean-up responsibility and timing |
| 8 | Public liability insurance | Events above 300 attendees or with temporary structures (stages, tents) |

Food vendors must hold valid food-handling permits; sellers at bazaars must hold their own trade or home-business licences.

## Noise, timing and summer restrictions

- **Amplified sound:** not permitted after **22:00** in residential districts — MN-02 Al Murjan, MN-03 Al Lulu Island,
  MN-04 Al Waha Gardens, MN-06 Al Sidr Residential and MN-07 Al Ghaf Community. Sound levels must not exceed
  65 dB(A) at the nearest residential façade at any time.
- **Outdoor events in residential districts** must end by 23:00, with the site cleared the same night or by 08:00 the
  following morning.
- **Summer restriction (15 June – 15 September):** no outdoor event activity — including set-up and dismantling by
  workers — between **12:30 and 15:00**. Outdoor events held in summer must provide shade, drinking water and a staffed
  first-aid point.
- Indoor events at Al Ghaf Community Centre are not affected by the summer restriction.

## Process

1. **Application** through MNDA Smart → Community → Event Permit, or at any Customer Happiness Centre. The AED 300 fee
   is paid on submission (unless waived) and refunded in full if the permit is not approved.
2. **Completeness check** — within 1 working day.
3. **Review** by the Community Services team of the Housing & Community Services Department — venue availability,
   crowd safety, noise, waste and summer restrictions. Outdoor sites are coordinated with the Field Operations &
   Inspection Department.
4. **Permit issue** — digital permit with QR code; it must be available on site throughout the event.
5. **On the day** — Field Operations & Inspection may inspect the event and may stop activities that breach the
   permit conditions.
6. **After the event** — the venue is handed back clean; outdoor sites are cleared within the times stated above.

## Service level and fees

- **Service level:** 3 working days from a complete application to decision.
- **Fee:** AED 300 per event; one permit covers up to 3 consecutive days at the same venue.
- **Waiver:** registered non-profit organisations (registration certificate attached) and school events (letter from
  the school principal).
- **Venue hire** at Al Ghaf Community Centre is charged separately under the published Community Facilities tariff.
- **Changes** to the date, venue or crowd size after approval must be requested at least 5 working days before the event.

## Tracking and status meanings

Permit references look like **MNDA-EV-2026-00684**. Check status in MNDA Smart → My Applications, by WhatsApp
+971 50 663 2000 or by calling 800 6632.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Received; completeness check in progress |
| Returned — information required | A requirement is missing or incomplete; organiser notified |
| Under review | With the Community Services team |
| Approved — permit issued | Permit available in MNDA Smart with QR code |
| Not approved | Reasons stated; fee refunded |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn by the organiser |

## Common reasons applications are returned

1. Application submitted less than 10 working days before the event.
2. Expected attendance above 300 without Civil Defence approval.
3. Site layout without marked exits, first-aid point or waste points.
4. Road closure or procession planned without a police coordination letter.
5. Outdoor programme or set-up scheduled between 12:30 and 15:00 during 15 June – 15 September.
6. Amplified music planned after 22:00 in a residential district.
