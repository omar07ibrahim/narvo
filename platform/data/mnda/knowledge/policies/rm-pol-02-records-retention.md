# Records Management & Retention Policy (RM-POL-02)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reference | MNDA/DGO/POL/RM-02 · Version 2.0 |
| Effective | 1 June 2025 (supersedes RM-POL-01 of 2019) |
| Approved by | H.E. Eng. Khalid Saif Al Mazrouei, Director General |
| Policy owner | Records Management Unit, Director General's Office |
| Legal holds | Adv. Noura Khalifa Al Qubaisi, Head of Legal Affairs |
| Contact | records@mnda.gov.ae |

Version 2.0 adds retention rules for Microsoft Teams chats, CCTV footage and AI audit logs, and makes MNDA Records
(EDRMS) the single repository for official records.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy ensures that the Authority creates, keeps and disposes of its records in a way that supports service
delivery, accountability, audit and legal obligations, and that no record is kept longer than necessary.

A **record** is any information, in any format, created or received in the course of MNDA business and kept as
evidence of a decision, transaction or activity. The policy covers paper and electronic records in every system:
MNDA Records (EDRMS), Tamkeen CMS, MNDA ePermits, MNDA People, MNDA Finance, Marsa Atlas, email, Microsoft Teams,
CCTV systems and the logs of AI systems such as the NARVO platform.

It applies to all employees, contractors and vendors who create or hold MNDA records. Records remain the property of
the Authority regardless of where they are stored.

## 2. Principles and the EDRMS

1. **MNDA Records (EDRMS)** is the official repository for records not held in a line-of-business system. Records
   held in Tamkeen CMS, MNDA ePermits, MNDA People or MNDA Finance are managed in place, with the retention rule
   configured in that system.
2. Each record is filed against a **business classification** (function → activity → transaction) that determines
   its retention period automatically.
3. Records are classified for security under IS-POL-01 (Public, Internal, Confidential) in addition to their
   retention class.
4. Records are captured at the time of the transaction, not at the end of a project.
5. Personal folders, USB drives and personal email are never used to hold MNDA records.
6. Retention periods start from the trigger event stated in the schedule (closure, expiry, separation), not from
   the date the record was created.

## 3. Retention schedule

### 3.1 Retention schedule — operational records

| Record class | Retention | Trigger | Final action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building permit files (MNDA-SVC-101 to 103, incl. drawings and NOCs) | **Permanent** | Permit issue | Transfer to the MNDA archive after 10 years |
| Housing case files (MNDA-SVC-201, 202) | **15 years** | Case closure | Secure destruction |
| Complaints and suggestions (MNDA-SVC-501) | **5 years** | Complaint closure | Secure destruction |
| Inspection reports and violation notices | 10 years | Violation closed | Secure destruction |
| CCTV footage (MNDA buildings and CHCs) | **90 days** | Recording | Automatic overwrite, unless on legal hold |
| AI audit logs (runs, retrievals, tool calls, approvals) | **5 years** | Log entry | Secure deletion |

### 3.2 Retention schedule — corporate records

| Record class | Retention | Trigger | Final action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procurement and tender files (incl. bids and evaluation) | **10 years** | Contract end | Secure destruction |
| Contracts and amendments | **10 years** | Contract expiry | Secure destruction |
| HR personnel files | **50 years** | Separation | Secure destruction |
| Salary certificates and HR letters (copies) | **2 years** | Issue date | Automatic deletion |
| Financial transactions and payment vouchers | 10 years | End of financial year | Secure destruction |
| Policies, circulars and Board decisions | **Permanent** | Supersession | Archive |

## 4. Email and Microsoft Teams

1. **Email** is retained according to the business classification of its content. An email that is a record —
   for example an applicant's submission, an approval or a contractual instruction — must be filed to EDRMS or the
   relevant case or permit file, where it takes that record's retention period.
2. Email not filed as a record is deleted automatically from mailboxes **3 years** after receipt or sending.
3. **Microsoft Teams chats** (one-to-one and group) are retained for **1 year** and then deleted automatically.
   Decisions made in chat must be confirmed by email or recorded in the relevant system.
4. Files shared in Teams channels follow the retention of the department site's records class.
5. Mailboxes of leavers are retained for 90 days, then reviewed by the line manager before deletion.

## 5. Disposal approval

1. Each quarter, EDRMS and the line-of-business systems generate a **disposal list** of records that have reached
   the end of their retention period.
2. The Records Management Unit sends each list to the owning department director, who confirms or extends
   (with reasons) within 20 working days.
3. Legal Affairs checks the list against active legal holds before anything is destroyed.
4. Disposal is carried out by the Records Management Unit: secure deletion for electronic records, cross-cut
   shredding by an approved contractor for paper, with a **certificate of destruction** filed in EDRMS.
5. A disposal register (record class, quantity, date, approver) is kept permanently.
6. Records may not be destroyed outside this process, even if their retention period has passed.

## 6. Legal hold

1. The **Head of Legal Affairs, Adv. Noura Khalifa Al Qubaisi**, may place a legal hold on any records relevant to
   actual or reasonably expected litigation, an investigation, an audit or a regulatory enquiry.
2. A legal hold suspends every retention rule for the records concerned, including automatic deletion of email,
   Teams chats, CCTV footage and AI audit logs.
3. Legal Affairs notifies the Records Management Unit and the record owners in writing; the DTO applies the hold in
   the systems within 1 working day.
4. Employees who receive a hold notice must not delete, alter or move the records concerned.
5. Only the Head of Legal Affairs can lift a hold; normal retention then resumes from the original trigger date.

## 7. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Director General's Office — Records Management Unit | Owns this policy, the retention schedule and the disposal process; trains records coordinators |
| Department directors | Information owners; approve disposal lists; nominate a records coordinator |
| Records coordinators (one per department) | File records correctly; maintain the department file plan |
| Legal Affairs | Legal holds; advice on retention for litigation and regulatory purposes |
| Digital Transformation Office | Configures retention in EDRMS, Microsoft 365 and line-of-business systems; secure deletion |
| All employees | Capture records in the right place; never destroy records outside the process |

Questions about retention or filing: records@mnda.gov.ae.
