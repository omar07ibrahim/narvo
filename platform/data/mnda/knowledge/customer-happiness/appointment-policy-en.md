# Appointment Policy — Customer Happiness Centres

This policy applies to appointments at MNDA's three Customer Happiness Centres. It complements the MNDA Customer
Happiness Charter (ref MNDA/CHC/CHARTER/2026). Centre ids used in MNDA systems are shown in brackets.

## 1. Centres, addresses and opening hours

| Centre | Address | Service counters | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Downtown (downtown) | MNDA Tower, ground floor, Corniche Road, MN-01 | 9 | Mon–Thu 7:30–15:30, Fri 7:30–12:00 |
| Al Murjan (al-murjan) | Al Murjan Community Services Building, Street 9, MN-02 | 6 | Mon–Thu 7:30–15:30, Fri 7:30–12:00 |
| Al Waha (al-waha) | Al Waha Gardens Service Centre, Plot MN-04-100-0002 | 5 | Mon–Thu 7:30–15:30, Fri 7:30–12:00 |

All centres are closed on Saturdays, Sundays and official public holidays. All three centres handle every service
listed in section 11; documents handed in at any centre are added to the same digital file.

### 1.1 Temporary notice — Al Waha centre capacity (5–15 October 2026)

While the customer hall is refurbished, the Al Waha Gardens Service Centre operates with **3 of its 5 counters until
Thursday 15 October 2026**. Appointment slots at Al Waha are therefore limited and are often fully booked several days
ahead. When no Al Waha slot is available, customers are offered the next available Al Waha slot and the earliest slots
at Al Murjan and Downtown, and reminded that most documents can be uploaded in MNDA Smart without an appointment.

## 2. Appointment slots

- Appointments are in **30-minute slots** starting on the hour or half hour.
- First slot 7:30. Last slot 15:00 Monday–Thursday and 11:30 on Friday.
- One slot covers one service for one customer. Customers with two unrelated matters should book two slots.

## 3. How to book

| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| Hotline 800 6632 | The virtual assistant books 24/7; agents during centre hours |
| WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000 | Send "appointment" and follow the steps |
| MNDA Smart | Appointments → Book |
| Centre reception | In person during opening hours |

Bookings require identity verification as set out in the Charter (section 4). Bookings are not taken by email. Each
booking receives a reference in the format **APT-26-NNNNN** and an SMS confirmation.

## 4. Booking rules

1. Appointments can be booked **up to 14 calendar days ahead**.
2. Same-day bookings are possible until 2 hours before the slot, if a slot is free.
3. A customer may hold **no more than 2 active bookings** at any time across all centres.
4. A booking is for the named customer. A representative must bring a valid power of attorney (or, for companies, an
   authorisation letter on the company's letterhead together with the trade licence).

## 5. Rescheduling and cancelling

- Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled **up to 2 hours before** the slot, through any booking channel.
- A cancellation made less than 2 hours before the slot, or not made at all, is recorded as a no-show.
- Cancelled slots are released immediately for other customers.

## 6. Arrival and grace period

- Please arrive 10 minutes early and check in at the kiosk with your APT reference or Emirates ID.
- A **10-minute grace period** applies. After 10 minutes the slot is released and the customer is served as a walk-in.

## 7. No-shows

Two no-shows within **60 days** suspend online booking (MNDA Smart, WhatsApp and the virtual assistant) for **30 days**.
During a suspension, customers can still be served as walk-ins or book through a Customer Happiness agent or at a centre
reception. Customers are informed by SMS when a suspension starts and ends.

## 8. Priority service

**Senior citizens and People of Determination** are served at the priority counter on arrival, with or without an
appointment, and do not count against the 2-booking limit. Accompanying family members may complete the transaction on
their behalf at the same counter. Customers who need special assistance can mention it when booking.

## 9. Walk-in customers

- Walk-ins take a ticket at the kiosk and are served **after customers with appointments**, in ticket order.
- The last walk-in ticket is issued 30 minutes before closing (15:00 Monday–Thursday, 11:30 Friday).
- When the expected wait exceeds the remaining opening time, reception offers the next available appointment.

## 10. Confirmations and reminders

- SMS confirmation on booking, with centre address and what to bring.
- Reminder SMS one working day before the appointment (on Friday for Monday appointments).
- Rescheduling and cancellation are confirmed by SMS.

## 11. What to bring

| Service | Typical purpose | Bring |
|---|---|---|
| Housing documents (housing_documents) | Submit or complete a Housing Grant & Loan application | Original Emirates ID of applicant and spouse; complete Family Book; salary certificate(s) issued within the last 30 days; no-property certificate (or give Land Registry consent); 6-month stamped bank statement for the loan track; case number if already applied |
| Housing consultation (housing_consultation) | Discuss a case, a decision, or sign a grant/loan agreement | Original Emirates ID; case number (MNDA-HG-2026-NNNNNN); decision or approval letter if applicable |
| Permit consultation (permit_consultation) | Discuss a building or fit-out permit with the permits desk | Emirates ID or trade licence; permit number (MNDA-BP-2026-NNNNN); correspondence with the master developer; consultant contact details |
| Completion certificate (completion_certificate) | Documents for a Building Completion Certificate (MNDA-SVC-103) | Emirates ID or trade licence; building permit number; consultant's completion report; Civil Defence final approval; as-built drawings |
| General (general) | Any other service, complaint or enquiry | Emirates ID and any reference numbers (CHC ticket, case or permit) |
