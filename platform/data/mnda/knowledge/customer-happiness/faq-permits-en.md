# FAQ — Building, Fit-out and Completion Permits

Covers MNDA-SVC-101, MNDA-SVC-102 and MNDA-SVC-103, and the master-developer NOC requirement of **Circular No. 14/2026**
(ref MNDA/PBP/CIR/14/2026, effective 15 July 2026).

## Which permits does MNDA issue, and what do they cost?

| Service | Code | Service level | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Building Permit — New Construction | MNDA-SVC-101 | 15 working days | AED 1,500 + AED 3 per m² of gross floor area (GFA) |
| Fit-out Permit | MNDA-SVC-102 | 5 working days | AED 800 |
| Building Completion Certificate | MNDA-SVC-103 | 10 working days | AED 1,000 |

Example: a villa with a GFA of 742.5 m² pays AED 1,500 + (742.5 × 3) = **AED 3,727.50**. Fees are paid when the
application is submitted. Service levels count from a complete application and are suspended while an application is
returned to the applicant or held awaiting a master-developer NOC.

## Who can apply for a building permit?

The plot owner applies through an engineering consultancy registered with MNDA. The consultant prepares and stamps the
drawings and is the technical contact for the application. Owners can follow the status themselves in MNDA Smart.

## What documents are needed for a new building permit (MNDA-SVC-101)?

1. Owner's Emirates ID, or trade licence for companies.
2. Title deed from the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry.
3. Approved affection (site) plan — **latest revision**.
4. Architectural drawings stamped by the licensed consultant.
5. Structural design and calculations **stamped and signed on every page** by a registered structural engineer.
6. Civil Defence preliminary approval (fire & life safety).
7. Contractor trade licence and MNDA contractor classification.
8. Master-developer NOC for plots in MN-03, MN-06 and MN-08 (see below).

## What is the master-developer NOC, and where is it required?

Since 15 July 2026, a No Objection Certificate from the community's master developer is mandatory before MNDA can issue
a permit in the three gated districts:

| District | Master developer |
|---|---|
| MN-03 Al Lulu Island | Lulu Island Developments PJSC |
| MN-06 Al Sidr Residential | Sidr Communities LLC |
| MN-08 Marina Quarter | Marina Quarter Properties LLC |

The NOC confirms that the design complies with the community's guidelines (façade materials, roof equipment screening,
boundary walls). It is requested by the owner or consultant directly from the master developer — MNDA does not issue it
and cannot request it on the applicant's behalf. Applications submitted before 15 July 2026 and already under technical
review are exempt.

## Does a fit-out permit need a NOC?

Only when the fit-out affects the **façade, roof or external works** (Circular 14/2026, section 3) — for example a new
shopfront, external signage, roof-mounted equipment or changes to balconies. Purely internal fit-outs (partitions,
ceilings, internal MEP, finishes) in MN-03, MN-06 and MN-08 do not need a NOC.

## What must the NOC show, and how long is it valid?

- Issued on the master developer's letterhead.
- States the plot number in the format **MN-DD-SSS-PPPP**.
- States the **drawing revision** it approves. This revision must match the revision submitted to MNDA; a mismatch
  requires a new NOC (section 4.3).
- Valid for **90 days from issue**. An expired NOC is treated as missing (section 4.2) — even if the design has not
  changed. Check the expiry date before you submit.

## What does "Awaiting master-developer NOC" mean?

If the NOC has been requested but not yet received, the application can be held in the status **"Awaiting
master-developer NOC"** for **up to 20 working days**, and the service-level clock is suspended (section 5.2). If no
valid NOC is uploaded within 20 working days, the application is returned with the status "Returned — NOC required"
and can be resubmitted, without a new fee, once the NOC is issued. Master developers are asked to issue NOCs within
**7 working days** of a complete request (section 4.4).

## My NOC is late. What can I do?

1. Ask your consultant to confirm with the master developer's NOC desk that the request is complete and references the
   correct plot number and drawing revision.
2. Contact the Customer Happiness Centre (800 6632, WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000 or MNDA Smart chat). We can add a
   follow-up note to your permit file and confirm the date your 20-working-day hold ends.
3. If the NOC is still outstanding after 10 working days in the status, or the hold limit is less than 5 working days
   away, ask for an **escalation to Planning & Building Permits**. Escalations are handled by a Customer Happiness agent,
   who will pass the full context to the permit officer and call you back.

MNDA cannot issue the NOC or waive it, and the virtual assistant cannot change the hold limit.

## Why was my application returned?

Most common return reasons in Q3 2026 (27% of applications were returned at least once):

| Reason | Share of returns |
|---|---|
| Master-developer NOC missing or expired | 46% |
| Structural calculations not stamped | 18% |
| Site-plan revision mismatch | 14% |
| Other (e.g. contractor classification, title deed, Civil Defence approval) | 22% |

The return notice lists every item to correct.

## What does "structural calculations not stamped" mean?

The structural calculation report must carry the stamp and signature of a registered structural engineer **on every
page**, not only on the cover. Reports stamped on the cover page only are returned. Your consultant uploads the fully
stamped report and resubmits.

## What does "site-plan revision mismatch" mean?

The architectural drawings must be based on the **latest revision** of the affection (site) plan held by the Land
Registry. If the Land Registry has issued a newer revision — for example with a changed setback — the drawings must be
revised to it before resubmission. Consultants can download the current revision from the Land Registry.

## Do I pay again when I resubmit a returned application?

No. A returned application is resubmitted under the same application number without a new fee.

## I paid the fee twice. How do I get a refund?

Contact the Customer Happiness Centre with the application number and, if possible, the payment references or a
screenshot of the card transactions. Duplicate payments are refunded to the **original payment method within 10
working days after approval by the CHC Manager** (Customer Happiness Charter, section 9). Neither agents nor the virtual
assistant can approve a refund themselves.

## How do I track my application?

Application numbers look like **MNDA-BP-2026-01932**. Check the status in **MNDA Smart → My Permits**, by email at
permits@mnda.gov.ae, on WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000 or on 800 6632. Status is given to the owner or the registered
consultant after identity verification. Issued permits can be downloaded with their QR code from MNDA Smart → My
Permits → View permit → Download PDF.
