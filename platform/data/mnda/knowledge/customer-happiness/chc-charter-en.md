# MNDA Customer Happiness Charter

**Issued by:** Sara Ahmed Al Blooshi, Manager, Customer Happiness Centre
**Approved by:** H.E. Eng. Khalid Saif Al Mazrouei, Director General
**Issued:** 1 June 2026 · **Revision 2:** 7 September 2026 (virtual assistant extended to WhatsApp and the hotline)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Charter sets out the service standards customers can expect from the Marsa Al Noor Development Authority (MNDA)
on every channel, the rules our staff and our virtual assistant follow, and how to escalate when we fall short.
It applies to all customer contacts about MNDA services, including housing (MNDA-SVC-201, 202), building permits
(MNDA-SVC-101, 102, 103), community events (MNDA-SVC-301), complaints and suggestions (MNDA-SVC-501) and municipal
maintenance requests (MNDA-SVC-502).

## 4. Identity verification

We protect your information. **No case, permit, ticket, payment or appointment details are disclosed until two
verification factors have been matched.**

### 4.1 Verification factors

| Factor | Counts as | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Registered mobile number (caller ID on the hotline, or the WhatsApp number) | 1 factor | Must match the mobile number held on the customer record |
| Emirates ID number | 1 factor | On a registered mobile, the last five digits are enough; on any other number the full number is required |
| Case or permit number **plus** date of birth | 1 factor | Both must match the same record |
| Signed-in MNDA Smart session (password and one-time code) | 2 factors | App chat inside a signed-in session needs no further checks |

### 4.2 Rules

1. A contact from a registered mobile needs **one** further factor: the Emirates ID number, or the case/permit number
   with date of birth.
2. A contact from an **unknown or withheld number** must give **two** factors other than the mobile number: the full
   Emirates ID number **and** the case/permit number with date of birth. If the customer cannot, we give general
   information only, and may offer to send a status link by SMS to the registered mobile.
3. Staff and the virtual assistant **never read out a full Emirates ID number** and never disclose financial data such
   as salary amounts, bank statement contents or card numbers, even to verified customers. Customers may confirm such
   data; we do not repeat it.
4. We do not confirm to a third party whether a person has an application with MNDA. A relative or representative needs
   the customer's presence or a valid power of attorney.
5. For business customers, the authorised signatory is verified as an individual, and the trade licence number must
   match the licence held on file.
6. Emails are answered with case-specific details only when they come from the email address on the customer record.

## 5. The MNDA Virtual Assistant

### 5.1 Disclosure

The virtual assistant always introduces itself as MNDA's virtual assistant. Emails it prepares say so in the signature.
You may ask for a member of staff at any time and the assistant will hand you over (section 6). The assistant operates
under the MNDA Responsible AI Policy (AI-POL-01).

### 5.2 What the assistant may do

- Answer questions from MNDA's published knowledge: circulars, service guides, FAQs and this Charter, quoting the
  reference (for example "Circular 9/2026, section 4.4").
- Give the status, next step and outstanding items of an application to a **verified** customer.
- Book, reschedule and cancel centre appointments.
- Log complaints, suggestions and municipal maintenance requests, and give the ticket reference.
- Add notes to a case or permit file (for example, an expected upload date or a follow-up request).
- Send self-service instructions, links and document checklists.
- Prepare a summary and hand the conversation to a human agent.

### 5.3 What the assistant may not do

- Approve, reject or recommend a decision on any application.
- Waive, reduce or refund any fee.
- Extend a deadline, grant an exception or accept a document that does not meet the published requirements.
- Change or reverse a decision.
- Disclose internal notes, officer recommendations or the identity of staff beyond their role.
- Promise an outcome or a date that is not recorded in the case or permit system.

These actions require the CHC Manager or the service-owning department (for example, the Head of the Housing
Applications Section for housing decisions).

### 5.4 Accuracy

If the assistant is not certain that an answer is supported by published knowledge, it says so and offers a handover.
A sample of assistant conversations is reviewed every week (section 10).

## 6. Handover to a human agent

### 6.1 When we hand over

The assistant hands the conversation to a human agent when:

1. the customer asks to speak to a person;
2. it has failed twice to understand or resolve the request;
3. the customer's sentiment is clearly negative (frustration, distress, or a sentiment score of -0.5 or below);
4. the customer appears vulnerable — for example a senior citizen, a Person of Determination, or someone in
   distress or bereavement;
5. the contact is a complaint about the conduct of a member of staff;
6. the request needs an action the assistant may not take (section 5.3), such as a refund, waiver, exception or
   change of decision;
7. the customer mentions legal action, a media enquiry, or a safety emergency.

### 6.2 Full context — you never repeat yourself

Every handover carries the verification status, the full transcript, a summary written for the agent, what has
already been tried, the references involved (case, permit, ticket, appointment, payment), the customer's sentiment and
a suggested next step. **Agents must not ask the customer to repeat information already given.** The customer is told
the handover reference (format HO-YYMM-NNNN) and how they will be contacted.

### 6.3 Priority

Handoffs are marked **high priority** when the customer has contacted us about the same matter more than once within
7 days, when a deadline falls within 5 working days, when the customer is vulnerable, or when sentiment is strongly
negative. All other handoffs are normal priority.

## 7. Complaints, suggestions and escalation

### 7.1 Logging

Complaints and suggestions (MNDA-SVC-501) and maintenance requests (MNDA-SVC-502) can be made on any channel. Each
receives a reference in the format **CHC-2026-NNNNNN** and is assigned to the responsible department. Complaints are
answered within 2 working days; maintenance requests are completed or scheduled within 3 working days.

### 7.2 Escalation path

| Level | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Happiness agent | First point of contact for any handover |
| 2 | Team Leader, Customer Happiness Centre | Customer not satisfied with the agent's answer; same working day |
| 3 | **Sara Ahmed Al Blooshi**, Manager, Customer Happiness Centre | Unresolved at level 2, refunds, service failures; response within 2 working days |
| 4 | Director of the service-owning department | Unresolved at level 3, or a decision only the department can take; response within 5 working days |

## 8. Appointments

Appointments can be booked through the hotline, WhatsApp, MNDA Smart or at a centre, in 30-minute slots up to 14 days
ahead. Senior citizens and People of Determination receive priority service at every centre. Full rules are in the
MNDA Appointment Policy for Customer Happiness Centres.

## 9. Refunds of duplicate or erroneous payments

1. A fee paid twice for the same application, or paid in error (for example, the wrong service or the wrong amount),
   is refunded.
2. A request can be made on any channel, quoting the application number and, where available, the payment references
   or a screenshot of the card transactions.
3. The Customer Happiness Centre verifies the payments against the application record and prepares the refund request.
4. **Every refund requires the approval of the CHC Manager.** Neither agents nor the virtual assistant can approve a
   refund.
5. After approval, the amount is refunded to the **original payment method within 10 working days**, and the customer
   is informed on the channel they used.
6. Fees for applications that were processed are not refunded when an application is returned or not approved.
   Resubmitting a returned application does not carry a new fee.

## 10. Feedback and customer satisfaction

- After each contact we invite you to rate the service from 1 (very unhappy) to 5 (very happy).
- Every rating of 1 or 2 is reviewed by a team leader within 2 working days and may lead to a call-back.
- The CHC Manager reviews a sample of 40 virtual-assistant conversations every week for accuracy, tone and correct
  verification, and corrects knowledge articles where needed.
- Monthly results (volumes, waiting times, first-contact resolution and satisfaction) are reported to the Director
  General's office.

## 11. Privacy and data protection

- Calls and chats are recorded for quality and training. Recordings and transcripts are kept for 24 months.
- Your data is used only to deliver and improve MNDA services and is accessed only by authorised staff.
- We never ask for passwords or one-time codes, and we never place personal data in links we send.
- You may ask for a copy of the data we hold about you through happiness@mnda.gov.ae.

## 12. What we ask of you

- Keep your mobile number and email up to date in MNDA Smart.
- Provide accurate information and complete documents.
- Treat our staff with courtesy; abusive contacts may be ended after a warning.
