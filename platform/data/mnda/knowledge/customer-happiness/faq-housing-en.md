# FAQ — Housing Grant & Loan Programme (MNDA-SVC-201)

Answers reflect **Circular No. 9/2026** (ref MNDA/HCS/CIR/09/2026), effective 1 September 2026. Applications submitted
before 1 September 2026 are assessed under the previous requirements unless the applicant changes the requested product
(grant or loan).

## Who can apply, and how much support is available?

UAE nationals aged 21 or above who are the head of the household, where neither the applicant nor the spouse owns
residential property in the UAE (property inherited jointly with three or more heirs is excepted).

| Track | Household income limit (monthly) | Maximum amount | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Housing Grant | AED 30,000 | AED 600,000 | Non-repayable; to build or buy a first home in Marsa Al Noor |
| Housing Loan | AED 60,000 | AED 900,000 | Interest-free; repaid over up to 25 years; instalment not above 25% of household income |

The service is free of charge.

## What documents do I need?

| # | Document | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Emirates ID — applicant | Valid, front and back |
| 4.2 | Emirates ID — spouse | Valid, front and back (married applicants) — **new** |
| 4.3 | Family Book (Khulasat Al Qaid) | Complete copy, **all pages including children** |
| 4.4 | Salary certificate | Issued **within the last 30 days**, showing basic salary and total allowances; for both spouses if both work |
| 4.5 | Certificate of No Property Ownership | From the Marsa Al Noor Land Registry, issued within the last 60 days — **new** (or give consent, see below) |
| 4.6 | Bank statement | **Loan track only:** last 6 months, stamped by the bank — **new** |
| 4.7 | Plot allocation letter or purchase contract | Where a plot or unit has been identified |
| 4.8 | Marriage certificate | Only where the family book has not yet been updated |

In September 2026, 41% of applications were incomplete at first review. The most common gaps were: salary certificate
older than 30 days (39% of incomplete files), no-property certificate missing (31%), spouse Emirates ID missing (17%)
and family book pages missing (13%).

## Why must my salary certificate be no older than 30 days?

Eligibility depends on household income limits (AED 30,000 for the grant, AED 60,000 for the loan), so the declared
income must reflect the family's current situation. Circular 9/2026 (section 4.4) therefore reduced the validity of
salary certificates from **90 days to 30 days** for all applications submitted from 1 September 2026. A certificate
older than 30 days on the date it is submitted is treated as a missing document (section 6.1). The rule applies even if
your salary has not changed, and no exceptions can be made by the Customer Happiness Centre or the virtual assistant.

## My salary certificate is a few weeks old. Will it be accepted?

What counts is the certificate's age **on the day you submit it**:

| Certificate date | Submitted on | Age | Accepted? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 September 2026 | 1 October 2026 | 22 days | Yes |
| 19 August 2026 | 26 September 2026 | 38 days | No — treated as missing |
| 12 July 2026 | 21 September 2026 | 71 days | No — treated as missing |

Once accepted, a certificate does not "expire" while your application is being processed — you will not be asked for a
new one because of processing time. Ask your employer early: some HR departments need up to 5 working days.

## Does my spouse need a salary certificate and Emirates ID?

If your spouse is employed, their salary certificate is also required, issued within the last 30 days. The spouse's
Emirates ID (front and back) is required for all married applicants (section 4.2). If the spouse's card is being
renewed, upload the new card as soon as it is received, within your response deadline.

## What is the Certificate of No Property Ownership and where do I get it?

It is a certificate from the **Marsa Al Noor Land Registry** confirming that you do not own residential property. It
must be issued within the last 60 days (section 4.5). You can request it from the Land Registry yourself — or you can
avoid this step entirely by giving consent (next question).

## Can MNDA obtain the no-property certificate for me?

Yes. Under section 6.3 of the circular, the case officer can retrieve it directly from the Land Registry **with your
consent**, and you then do not need to upload it. The check covers both the applicant and the spouse.

To give consent in MNDA Smart: **My Applications → select your case → Missing documents → Certificate of No Property
Ownership → "I consent to retrieval from the Land Registry" → confirm with the one-time code**. The item then shows
"Retrieval in progress". You can also tick the consent box when you first apply, or give consent at any Customer
Happiness Centre.

## Do I need a bank statement?

Only for the **loan track**. You need a statement for the **last 6 months, stamped by the bank** (section 4.6).
E-statements from a banking app are accepted when they carry the bank's stamp (including a digital stamp); a QR
verification code on its own is not a stamp. Statements covering fewer than 6 months are returned as incomplete.
Grant applicants do not need a bank statement.

## What does "complete Family Book copy" mean?

All pages of the Family Book, including the pages showing every child — not only the pages of the head of household
and spouse. If the Family Book has not yet been updated after your marriage, attach the marriage certificate as well
(section 4.8).

## My application is "Incomplete — awaiting applicant". What happens now?

1. You receive one notification listing **every** missing or invalid item (section 5.1 and 5.4).
2. You have **15 working days** from the notification to provide them (section 5.2).
3. While the application is incomplete, it is suspended and the **12-working-day service clock stops**. It restarts
   when the file is complete.
4. If the documents are not received within 15 working days, the application is closed. You may submit a new
   application at any time.

## How do I upload missing documents?

In MNDA Smart: **My Applications → select your case → Upload missing documents**, then upload each item against the
listed requirement. You can also hand documents in at any Customer Happiness Centre (Downtown, Al Murjan, Al Waha).

**Documents sent by email or WhatsApp are not attached to your case automatically.** Always upload through MNDA Smart or
at a centre so the case officer receives them.

## How long does it take to get a decision?

**12 working days from a complete application** to a decision. The clock does not run while the application is
"Incomplete — awaiting applicant".

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Received; completeness check in progress |
| Under review | Assigned to a case officer |
| Incomplete — awaiting applicant | Documents missing or invalid; 15 working days to respond |
| Verification | Land Registry and income verification in progress |
| Decision pending | With the Head of the Housing Applications Section |
| Approved / Not approved | Decision issued; a letter is sent |

## How can I check my application status?

- **MNDA Smart** → My Applications (recommended).
- **WhatsApp +971 50 663 2000** — send your case number (format MNDA-HG-2026-NNNNNN).
- **Hotline 800 6632** — the virtual assistant answers 24/7.

For your privacy, status is given only after verification: from your registered mobile, the last five digits of your
Emirates ID or your case number with date of birth; from any other number, your full Emirates ID number and your case
number with date of birth.

## Who decides on my application? Can the virtual assistant approve it?

Eligibility decisions are taken by the **Head of the Housing Applications Section** or an authorised deputy (section 7).
The virtual assistant, Customer Happiness agents and automated tools **cannot approve or reject an application, waive a
requirement or extend a deadline** (MNDA Responsible AI Policy, AI-POL-01). They can explain requirements, give status,
log follow-ups and book appointments.

## My application was not approved. What can I do?

The decision letter states the reason and the circular section it relies on. If you believe the decision was based on
information that is no longer correct — for example, a property that was transferred but still appears in the Land
Registry — submit a reconsideration request with supporting documents (such as the transfer deed) at any Customer
Happiness Centre or through 800 6632. The request is reviewed by the Housing Applications Section.
