# Site Safety Inspection Checklist & Photo Evidence Guide

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reference | MNDA/FOI/CHK/07/2026 · Version 2.0 (replaces CHK/07/2025 v1.3) |
| Issued | 15 February 2026 |
| Approved by | Eng. Hamad Juma Al Kaabi, Director, Field Operations & Inspection |
| Applies to | Authorised Inspectors and reviewers using the inspection module of MNDA ePermits |
| Legal basis | CSSR-2025 (MNDA/FOI/REG/03/2025), Art. 30–32 and Annex B |

## 1. Purpose and how to use this guide

This guide tells inspectors what to photograph, how to decide each defect, and what evidence closes it. It has one
section per defect code **D01–D13**; each code maps to one CSSR-2025 article and one severity class.

AI-assisted image review (CSSR-2025 Art. 30.5) screens uploaded photographs and proposes candidate defects using the
same codes. **AI flags are advisory only.** No Notice of Violation may be issued until an Authorised Inspector has
accepted each finding. Inspection findings are a Tier 3 AI use case under the Authority's Responsible AI Policy
(AI-POL-01): the inspector is the decision-maker and is accountable for every finding.

## 2. Photo standards

- **Device.** Use the Authority-issued tablet or phone with the ePermits inspection app; location services on and
  clock set to network time.
- **Quality.** At least 12 MP, no filters, no digital zoom beyond 2×. Re-take any blurred or back-lit image.
- **Viewpoint.** For each finding take one **wide context shot** (shows level, grid line or a landmark) and one
  **close-up** of the defect. For edges, scaffolds and excavations take a **second angle** to rule out protection
  outside the frame. Record the camera position in the caption (e.g. "Level 2, grid 4/B, facing east") so the
  contractor can repeat it for closure.
- **Scale reference.** Include a folding rule (2 m), a ranging pole (0.5 m red/white bands), a person (about 1.7 m), a
  standard block (400 × 200 mm) or a hoarding panel (2.4 m) wherever a dimension matters.
- **Timestamp.** The app burns date, time and inspection reference into a visible overlay and keeps the original
  EXIF data. Device time must be within ±2 minutes of network time.
- **Geotag.** GPS accuracy 10 m or better. Photos geotagged more than 30 m outside the plot boundary are flagged for
  checking (GPS drift near high-rise buildings in MN-08 is common).
- **Privacy.** Photograph the hazard, not the person. Faces are not needed for a PPE finding; do not photograph worker
  ID cards. Blur faces before any image is shared outside the Authority.
- **Contractor photos.** Verification photos must be uploaded through the contractor's e-services account with EXIF
  intact. Screenshots and images forwarded through messaging apps (metadata stripped) are rejected.
- **Retention.** Inspection photos are kept for 10 years after the violation is closed (RM-POL-02).

## 3. Reviewer decisions — accept / reject / add missed defect

Each AI flag shows the defect code, a box on the image and a confidence score. The reviewer must take one decision
per flag; nothing is accepted automatically, whatever the confidence.

- **Accept** — the defect is clearly visible, meets the article's threshold (e.g. a drop of 2 m or more for D01, depth
  over 1.2 m for D07) and the evidence standard in the code section below is met.
- **Reject** — select a reason code:
  - R1 protection or control present outside the frame (confirmed by second angle or on site);
  - R2 below the article threshold;
  - R3 not a defect (e.g. a cap worn under a helmet);
  - R4 image quality insufficient — re-take;
  - R5 duplicate of another finding;
  - R6 outside the site boundary or not the contractor's work.
- **Change code** — the hazard is real but mis-classified (e.g. flagged D01 on a scaffold platform → D03).
- **Add missed defect** — draw a box, choose the code and add a caption. Missed defects are fed back to DTO for model
  monitoring.
- **One finding per defect per location.** Several photos of the same open edge are one finding; open edges on two
  different levels are two findings.
- **Repeat check.** The system shows any finding under the same article on the same site and contractor in the last
  90 days; if present, the class escalates under Art. 31.6 and the reviewer confirms it.

During the pilot (October–December 2025) reviewers rejected 23% of AI flags; the most common rejections were D01 with
protection out of frame and D02 with a cap worn under a helmet.

## D01 — Missing or inadequate edge protection (CSSR-2025 Art. 12.3, Critical)

**Requirement:** guardrails at every open edge, slab perimeter, floor opening or stair void with a fall of 2 m or
more: top rail 950–1,100 mm, intermediate rail, toe board at least 150 mm, or an equivalent barrier.

- **Look for:** slab edges, balconies, roofs, lift shafts and stair voids with no rail; missing mid-rail or toe board;
  tape or rope used instead of rails; uncovered floor openings; workers close to an unprotected drop.
- **Typical false flags:** edge protected by netting, a scaffold or a parapet just out of frame; ground-floor slab
  less than 2 m above grade; protection removed under a permit while a harnessed worker is clipped on; a completed
  masonry wall at 950 mm or more.
- **Evidence for a finding:** wide shot showing the level (fall height at least 2 m) and the unprotected length;
  second angle confirming no protection; estimated length of open edge in metres.
- **Closure evidence:** guardrail at 950–1,100 mm with mid-rail and 150 mm toe board visible **along the full length of
  the previously open edge**, same viewpoint as the original photo, plus a close-up with a folding rule showing
  top-rail height. Floor openings: fixed cover marked "OPENING — DO NOT REMOVE", or rails on all sides.

## D02 — PPE non-compliance (CSSR-2025 Art. 8.1, Major)

**Requirement:** helmet, high-visibility clothing and safety footwear at all times; task PPE; full-body harness
clipped to a rated anchor at height without collective protection.

- **Look for:** no helmet, no hi-vis, sandals or trainers, a worker at an open height not clipped on, grinding without
  eye protection.
- **Typical false flags:** a cloth cap or neck shade worn **under** a helmet; hi-vis hidden by harness straps; a white
  helmet blending into a white wall; persons inside the site office or welfare cabin; an escorted visitor in the
  office compound.
- **Evidence for a finding:** count of persons in frame and of non-compliant persons; location; for harness findings,
  the height and absence of collective protection.
- **Closure evidence:** photo of the same work area and crew showing every person with helmet, hi-vis and safety boots
  (and clipped on where required), plus the contractor's PPE issue record or toolbox-talk attendance sheet dated
  after the notice.

## D03 — Unsafe scaffolding (CSSR-2025 Art. 15.2, Critical)

**Requirement:** erected by competent persons, fully boarded at working lifts, guardrails and toe boards, braced and
tied, base plates and sole boards, valid green tag renewed every 7 days.

- **Look for:** working lifts without guardrails or toe boards, part-boarded platforms, missing bracing, standards on
  blocks, ladders tied to rails, no tag, tag older than 7 days.
- **Typical false flags:** scaffold under erection with a red tag and access blocked; unused lifts not boarded but not
  accessible; toe boards hidden behind sheeting; the green tag fitted at another access point.
- **Evidence for a finding:** full elevation; close-up of each defect; close-up of the tag with date legible, or
  photo of the access point with no tag.
- **Closure evidence:** the same elevation from the same viewpoint showing every working lift fully boarded with
  guardrails, mid-rails and toe boards; base plates on sole boards; close-up of a green tag dated after the notice with
  the inspector's name.

## D04 — Unprotected rebar starters (CSSR-2025 Art. 13.6, Major)

**Requirement:** protruding starter bars and dowels presenting an impalement or laceration hazard capped with
approved mushroom caps or covered by protective troughs.

- **Look for:** vertical bar ends in columns, walls or slabs without orange or yellow caps, near walkways, ladders or
  working positions.
- **Typical false flags:** **rebar inside formwork that will be poured the same day** in an area closed to other
  trades; bars fully enclosed by shutters; caps in place but in shadow; bars bent over and tied under an approved
  detail.
- **Evidence for a finding:** close-up showing uncapped ends and a wide shot showing the walkway or work position next
  to them; number of uncapped bars (approximate).
- **Closure evidence:** wide shot of the same area showing **every** bar end capped or the row covered by a trough;
  or a dated photo showing the bars cast into concrete.

## D05 — Poor housekeeping (CSSR-2025 Art. 21.1, Minor)

**Requirement:** work areas, walkways and stairs free of debris, offcuts, packaging and waste; waste removed to
designated skips at least daily.

- **Look for:** scattered debris, timber offcuts with nails, packaging, waste piles, trip hazards in walkways and
  stairs, overflowing skips.
- **Typical false flags:** materials staged at the work face for same-day use; waste at the designated collection
  point awaiting the daily skip lift; formwork being struck in an active area.
- **Evidence for a finding:** wide shot of the area showing the extent; location.
- **Closure evidence:** same viewpoint showing walkways and stairs clear and waste in skips; skip removal docket dated
  after the notice.

## D06 — Damaged hoarding or missing site signage (CSSR-2025 Art. 6.2, Major)

**Requirement:** continuous, undamaged hoarding at least 2.4 m high next to public areas; bilingual site board
(permit number, contractor, consultant, emergency contact); mandatory safety signs at every gate.

- **Look for:** gaps, fallen or bent panels, open perimeter to a road or footpath, no site board, no signs at a gate.
- **Typical false flags:** a vehicle gate open with a gateman and barrier; panels removed for a delivery under
  supervision; the site board fitted at the other gate.
- **Evidence for a finding:** photo from the public side showing the gap or missing board, with the panel height for
  scale; gate name.
- **Closure evidence:** same viewpoint showing continuous hoarding at 2.4 m or more; legible site board showing the
  permit number; signs at each gate.

## D07 — Open excavation without barriers (CSSR-2025 Art. 17.4, Critical)

**Requirement:** excavations over 1.2 m deep shored, battered or benched; rigid barriers at least 1 m back from the
edge; spoil and materials at least 1 m from the edge; ladder or ramp access.

- **Look for:** open trench or pit with no barrier, tape only, spoil heaped at the edge, vertical unsupported sides,
  no ladder.
- **Typical false flags:** excavation 1.2 m deep or less; a batter that looks vertical because of perspective; barrier
  behind the photographer; a backfilled trench.
- **Evidence for a finding:** shot across the excavation with a person, ladder or ranging pole for depth; edges
  without barriers; spoil position.
- **Closure evidence:** same viewpoint showing rigid barriers at least 1 m back on all sides, spoil moved 1 m clear,
  shoring or batter visible and a ladder in place; or a dated photo of the backfilled excavation.

## D08 — Missing or non-compliant fire extinguisher (CSSR-2025 Art. 19.2, Major)

**Requirement:** charged, inspected extinguisher of suitable type within 10 m of every hot-work location, fuel or gas
store, generator and site office, mounted or on a stand and signed.

- **Look for:** welding, cutting or a generator with no extinguisher nearby; empty stand; gauge in the red; gas
  cylinders without an extinguisher.
- **Typical false flags:** extinguisher present but out of frame; welding set stored, not in use; a CO₂ unit beside a
  generator counted as missing because it is not red-labelled powder.
- **Evidence for a finding:** wide shot covering the hazard and the area within 10 m; close-up of an empty stand or
  discharged gauge.
- **Closure evidence:** wide shot showing the extinguisher within 10 m of the hazard, gauge in the green, current
  inspection tag and sign visible.

## D09 — Unsafe ladder use (CSSR-2025 Art. 16.3, Major)

**Requirement:** industrial grade, undamaged, about 75° (1:4), secured top or bottom, extending 1 m above the landing,
access or short-duration light work only, three points of contact.

- **Look for:** makeshift or timber ladders, damaged stiles, unsecured ladders, wrong angle, standing on top rungs,
  ladder used as a working platform.
- **Typical false flags:** a stepladder used correctly for a short task; a ladder secured by a stabiliser out of
  frame; an internal ladder inside a scaffold access bay.
- **Evidence for a finding:** side view showing angle and securing; close-up of damage.
- **Closure evidence:** compliant industrial ladder at about 75°, tied and extending 1 m above the landing, or the
  replacement access (stair tower or mobile platform); damaged ladder shown removed or destroyed.

## D10 — Exposed or unsafe electrical installation (CSSR-2025 Art. 18.2, Critical)

**Requirement:** installed by a licensed electrician; 30 mA RCDs; boards closed and locked; armoured or protected
cables routed overhead or along edges, out of water and walkways.

- **Look for:** open boards, cables across walkways or in water, damaged insulation, taped joints, improvised plugs.
- **Typical false flags:** armoured cable along a wall edge (permitted); board open while an electrician works on it
  under lockout; cable crossing a route under a cable ramp.
- **Evidence for a finding:** close-up of the defect; wide shot showing the walkway or water.
- **Closure evidence:** board closed and locked with RCD test label; cables on hangers or protected along edges; the
  electrician's test record dated after the notice.

## D11 — Unsafe material stacking (CSSR-2025 Art. 21.4, Major)

**Requirement:** firm level ground, stable and interlocked, no higher than 2 m (or 3 times the base width), at least
1 m from slab edges and excavations, secured against wind.

- **Look for:** leaning or tall stacks of blocks, pipes, formwork or rebar; materials at slab or excavation edges;
  loose sheets.
- **Typical false flags:** stacks that look tall due to a low camera angle; material beside a fully built wall rather
  than an open edge.
- **Evidence for a finding:** photo with a person or pallet for height; distance to the edge.
- **Closure evidence:** same viewpoint showing stacks at 2 m or less, interlocked, at least 1 m from edges, and sheets
  strapped or weighted.

## D12 — Blocked access or emergency egress (CSSR-2025 Art. 20.3, Major)

**Requirement:** pedestrian routes, stair cores, emergency exits and assembly-point routes kept clear, at least 1.2 m
wide, separated from vehicle routes.

- **Look for:** materials or equipment in stairs, doorways or exit routes; no segregated pedestrian path.
- **Typical false flags:** a route closed briefly during a delivery with a flagman; an exit not yet designated in the
  current construction phase.
- **Evidence for a finding:** photo along the route with a known object for width.
- **Closure evidence:** same viewpoint showing at least 1.2 m clear width, measured with a folding rule, and barriers
  separating pedestrians from vehicles.

## D13 — Lifting operation without exclusion zone (CSSR-2025 Art. 22.5, Critical)

**Requirement:** area beneath and around the suspended load barricaded as an exclusion zone, controlled by a
certified banksman/signaller; nobody under a suspended load.

- **Look for:** load suspended over workers, no barricade around the lift area, no banksman visible.
- **Typical false flags:** load resting on the ground with slings attached; workers guiding with tag lines from
  outside the zone; crane idle with an empty hook; barricade out of frame.
- **Evidence for a finding:** photo showing the suspended load and persons beneath or within the load path.
- **Closure evidence:** photo of a later lift showing the barricaded zone, the banksman in a marked vest and nobody
  under the load; plus the lift plan and the banksman's certificate. Lifting in the affected area stays stopped until
  verified.

## Due-date rules (CSSR-2025 Art. 31)

| Class | Timescale | How it is counted |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | 24 hours + area stop-work until verified (Art. 31.2) | Clock hours from service, including weekends and holidays |
| Major | 3 working days (Art. 31.3) | Working days from the day after service, ending 23:59 |
| Minor | 7 working days (Art. 31.4) | Working days from the day after service, ending 23:59 |

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays; Friday counts as a full working day.

**Worked example — notice served Wednesday 4 February 2026 at 10:40:**

- Critical: due **Thursday 5 February 2026, 10:40**; the affected area is stopped from 10:40 on 4 February until
  verification.
- Major: Thursday 5 (1), Friday 6 (2), Monday 9 (3) → due **Monday 9 February 2026, 23:59**.
- Minor: 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 February → due **Friday 13 February 2026, 23:59**.

**Holidays:** a Major finding served Monday 1 December 2025 skipped the National Day holidays (2–3 December) and was
due Monday 8 December 2025.

**Other rules:**

- Repeat within 90 days (Art. 31.6) escalates the class, and the escalated class sets the timescale.
- A Critical deadline falling on a weekend is verified by the duty inspector on the on-call roster.
- One extension of up to 3 working days may be granted for a Major or Minor finding by the Head of Site Inspection
  Section where specialist material is needed. **Critical findings are never extended.**
- A finding not verified by its due date becomes **Overdue** at 00:00 the next day; the additional penalty in Annex
  B.4 applies and a re-inspection is scheduled within 2 working days.

## Notice of Violation — required content

A Notice of Violation (CSSR-2025 Art. 32) is generated in ePermits only after every finding has been accepted by the
inspector. It is bilingual (Arabic and English, Arabic prevails) and must contain:

| Plot number | MN-DD-SSS-PPPP | MN-04-117-0031 (Al Waha Gardens) |
| Building permit | MNDA-BP-2026-NNNNN | MNDA-BP-2026-00231 |
| Main contractor and trade licence | Name, CN-NNNNNNN | Al Marsa Engineering & Contracting LLC, CN-2207815 |
| Consultant | Name | as on the building permit |

| Signatory | Inspector name and authorisation number; issued under the authority of the Director, Field Operations & Inspection — Eng. Hamad Juma Al Kaabi | Eng. Badr Saleh Al Marri, FOI-AI-0219 |
