# Acceptable Use of IT Resources Policy (IT-POL-02)

Version 2.0 adds rules on public AI tools and the MNDA private AI platform NARVO, and blocks USB storage on all
MNDA devices.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines how MNDA IT resources may and may not be used, so that the Authority's information, systems and
reputation are protected and every user knows what is expected.

**IT resources** include laptops, desktops, corporate smartphones and tablets; the MNDA network, Wi-Fi and VPN;
email, Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365; business systems (Tamkeen CMS, MNDA ePermits, MNDA People, MNDA Finance,
Marsa Atlas, MNDA Records); printers; and AI platforms provided by the Authority.

The policy applies to all employees, secondees, interns, contractors and consultants who are given access to any
MNDA IT resource. It is read together with IS-POL-01 (Information Security & Data Classification) and AI-POL-01
(Responsible AI).

## 2. Devices

1. Use only **MNDA-managed devices** to access MNDA systems and information. Personal computers may not connect to
   the internal network or the VPN; personal phones may access only Outlook and Teams through the managed app.
2. Each laptop carries an asset tag (format MNDA-NB-NNNNN) and is assigned to one named user, who is responsible for
   it. Do not lend your device or share your account.
3. Do not disable or bypass security software, encryption, updates or device management.
4. Lock your device when unattended; carry laptops as hand luggage when travelling.
5. Report loss, theft or damage to the Service Desk (ext. 4357) **immediately**.
6. Return all devices on leaving the Authority or when replaced; devices are wiped and a certificate of data
   destruction is issued.

## 3. Email and collaboration

1. MNDA email and Teams are for official business. Write professionally; messages may be disclosed in audits or
   legal proceedings.
2. Apply the correct sensitivity label (Public, Internal, Confidential). Confidential messages go only to
   @mnda.gov.ae addresses.
3. **Do not auto-forward** MNDA email to personal or external accounts.
4. Do not use personal email, WhatsApp or other consumer messaging for MNDA documents. The official MNDA WhatsApp
   channel (+971 50 663 2000) is operated only by the Customer Happiness Centre through the managed platform.
5. Be alert to phishing: check the sender, do not open unexpected attachments, and report suspicious messages using
   the "Report phishing" button in Outlook.
6. Shared mailboxes (for example housing@mnda.gov.ae, permits@mnda.gov.ae) are used only by their named members.

## 4. Internet use

1. Internet access is provided for business purposes. Web filtering blocks categories that are illegal or
   inappropriate (gambling, adult content, hacking tools, piracy, anonymising proxies).
2. Do not attempt to bypass the web filter, for example through personal VPNs or proxy services.
3. Do not download software, media or files from untrusted sources; software is installed only through the
   MNDA Service Portal and the IT Service Catalogue.
4. Cloud storage and file-sharing services other than MNDA OneDrive, SharePoint and the MNDA external portal are
   blocked for uploads.
5. Streaming video and music for non-work purposes is not permitted during working hours on the MNDA network.

## 5. Social media

1. Only Corporate Communications (Director General's Office) speaks on behalf of the Authority on social media.
2. Employees must not publish Internal or Confidential information, photographs of MNDA offices showing screens or
   documents, or details of customers' cases, applications or permits.
3. Do not comment on behalf of MNDA on complaints or customer posts; refer them to the Customer Happiness Centre.
4. Personal accounts must not use the MNDA logo or suggest that personal views are those of the Authority.
5. Employees remain bound by UAE law, including the laws on cybercrime and the protection of personal data, when
   posting in a personal capacity.

## 6. Personal use

Limited personal use of MNDA email, internet and devices is allowed when it:

- is occasional and brief, and takes place mainly outside core hours (9:00–14:00);
- does not interfere with work or consume significant resources (no large downloads or streaming);
- does not involve commercial activity, private business or political campaigning;
- complies with every other section of this policy.

Personal files must not be stored on MNDA systems beyond what is incidental (for example a personal calendar
entry). The Authority is not responsible for personal data stored on MNDA devices, which may be deleted when the
device is wiped or replaced.

## 7. Prohibited activities

The following are prohibited on any MNDA IT resource:

1. Accessing, storing or sending illegal, offensive, discriminatory or sexually explicit material.
2. Sharing your password or MFA code, or using another person's account.
3. Accessing information you are not authorised to see, including browsing customer or employee records without a
   business reason (all access to Tamkeen CMS and MNDA People records is logged).
4. Installing unlicensed software or circumventing security controls.
5. Connecting unapproved devices (personal routers, USB storage, wireless access points) to the network.
6. Using MNDA resources for cryptocurrency mining, gambling or private commercial activity.
7. Sending MNDA information to personal email, personal cloud storage or **public AI tools**.

## 8. Public AI tools and NARVO

1. It is **forbidden to paste, upload or type Internal or Confidential information into public AI chatbots** or other
   external AI services. This includes customer names and Emirates ID numbers, case and permit files, tender
   documents, draft policies, salary data, screenshots of MNDA systems and meeting notes.
2. Use the **MNDA private AI platform, NARVO**, instead. NARVO runs on MNDA-controlled infrastructure in the UAE,
   respects your classification access and logs every interaction (AI-POL-01).
3. Public AI tools may be used only for **Public** information or general knowledge that contains no MNDA data.
4. AI outputs must be checked by the user before they are relied on or sent. AI never approves or rejects an
   application, permit, payment, hiring or disciplinary decision.
5. Suspected misuse or incidents involving AI are reported to ai.governance@mnda.gov.ae within 24 hours.

## 9. Removable media (USB storage)

1. **USB storage devices are blocked** on all MNDA laptops and desktops. USB keyboards, mice, headsets and approved
   presentation clickers continue to work.
2. To exchange files with external parties, use the MNDA external portal (partners.mnda.gov.ae) or a shared
   OneDrive link with expiry, in line with IS-POL-01.
3. A temporary exception (for example to receive survey data from a consultant's encrypted drive) can be requested
   in the MNDA Service Portal. It requires Information Security approval, is limited to a named encrypted device,
   and lasts no more than 5 working days.
4. Do not plug in USB devices found or received as gifts; hand them to the Service Desk.

## 10. Monitoring

1. MNDA IT resources and the information on them belong to the Authority. To protect security and comply with the
   law, the Digital Transformation Office monitors use of the network, email, internet, devices and systems,
   including access logs for business systems and AI platforms.
2. Monitoring is automated and proportionate. Content of an individual's email or files is reviewed only for a
   specific investigation approved by the Director of Human Capital and the Head of Legal Affairs.
3. Monitoring is never used to track the productivity of individual employees (AI-POL-01).
4. Logs are retained as set out in RM-POL-02.

## 11. Breaches

1. Suspected breaches of this policy are reported to the IT Service Desk (ext. 4357) or to your line manager.
2. The DTO may suspend an account or device immediately to contain a risk.
3. Breaches are investigated with Human Capital and, where needed, Legal Affairs. Consequences range from a
   documented warning to disciplinary action under the Authority's procedures and, for contractors, removal from the
   engagement.
4. Breaches that may constitute a criminal offence are referred by the Head of Legal Affairs to the competent
   authorities.
5. Repeated minor breaches (for example ignoring phishing training) are reported to the employee's Director.
