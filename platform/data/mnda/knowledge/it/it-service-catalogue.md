All requests are raised in the MNDA Service Portal, which routes approvals automatically and shows the status of each
request. Phone and email requests are logged in the Portal by the Service Desk on your behalf.

## 1. Service overview

| Service | Who can request | Approval | Fulfilment SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laptop replacement | All staff with an MNDA laptop | Automatic if older than 4 years; otherwise line manager + DTO | 5 working days |
| Second monitor (27" QHD) | Office-based staff | Line manager | 3 working days |
| VPN access | Staff with an approved HC-POL-07 arrangement, or with manager approval | Automatic with HC-POL-07; otherwise line manager | 1 working day |
| System access request | All staff | Line manager + system owner | 2 working days |
| Password reset | All staff | Self-service with MFA, or identity verification | 30 minutes |
| Corporate smartphone | Field roles, directors, on-call staff | Director | 5 working days |
| Software installation | All staff | Line manager (catalogue); Information Security (non-catalogue) | 2 working days (catalogue) |
| New joiner IT setup | Line managers, via MNDA People onboarding | Human Capital | Ready on day 1 if requested 5 working days ahead |

Fulfilment SLAs run from final approval and exclude weekends and public holidays.

## 2. Laptop replacement

**Standard lifecycle:** laptops are replaced **4 years from the purchase date**. Once a device is older than 4 years,
the employee is **eligible automatically** and the request needs no further approval.

**Earlier replacement** is possible only when:

- the device is **damaged beyond repair**, confirmed by a Service Desk damage report (accidental damage may be
  reviewed with the line manager); or
- the employee's **role changes** and needs a different specification (for example a GIS or design workstation).

**Standard model:** Dell Latitude 7450 (Intel Core Ultra 7, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD), with docking station and charger.

**How to request:** MNDA Service Portal → Hardware → Laptop replacement. Enter the current asset tag (format
MNDA-NB-NNNNN, on the label under the device); the Portal shows the purchase date and eligibility. **Fulfilment: 5
working days.** The old device is returned on collection, wiped to the DTO standard, and a **certificate of data
destruction** is attached to the request. Save personal working files to OneDrive before handover.

## 3. Second monitor

A second **27-inch QHD monitor** is available for **office-based staff** at their assigned MNDA desk.

- **Approval:** line manager.
- **Fulfilment:** 3 working days, including installation at the desk.
- **How to request:** MNDA Service Portal → Hardware → Additional monitor; state your floor and desk number.

Monitors are not provided for home use under the Remote & Flexible Work Policy (HC-POL-07). Hot-desk areas on Floors 3
and 8 of MNDA Tower already have dual monitors and do not need a request. Staff with a medical recommendation for
special equipment (for example a larger screen or ergonomic keyboard) should attach it to the request; these are
reviewed by Human Capital and do not count against the standard allocation.

## 4. VPN access

The MNDA VPN gives secure access to internal systems from outside MNDA premises. **MFA is mandatory** for every VPN
connection.

- **Automatic:** VPN access is granted automatically when an employee's first remote day under **HC-POL-07** is
  approved in MNDA People.
- **Otherwise:** request it in MNDA Service Portal → Access → VPN with **line manager approval** (for example for
  travel or out-of-hours on-call duties).
- **Fulfilment:** 1 working day.
- VPN works only on MNDA-managed laptops and corporate smartphones. Personal devices cannot connect.

VPN accounts that are not used for 90 days are disabled automatically and can be reactivated through the Portal.

## 5. System access request

Access to business systems — for example **Tamkeen CMS** (case management), **MNDA ePermits**, **MNDA Finance** (ERP)
and the GIS **Marsa Atlas** — follows the principle of least privilege (IS-POL-01, section 5).

1. Request in MNDA Service Portal → Access → Business system, selecting the system and the role profile
   (e.g. "Housing Case Officer", "Permit Reviewer — read only").
2. Approval by the **line manager** and then the **system owner** (for example the Director of Housing & Community
   Services for Tamkeen CMS, the Director of Planning & Building Permits for MNDA ePermits).
3. **Fulfilment: 2 working days** after both approvals.

Only the role profile needed for the job is granted. Access is confirmed by system owners in a **quarterly access
review**; access not confirmed is removed. Access for leavers is removed on their last working day.

## 6. Password reset

- **Self-service (recommended):** MNDA Service Portal → "Forgot password", verified with your registered MFA method.
  Available 24/7; takes about 2 minutes.
- **Service Desk:** call ext. 4357. The agent verifies your identity (staff number and a one-time code sent to your
  registered mobile, or in person with your staff card at the Service Desk, Floor 2, MNDA Tower).
- **SLA: 30 minutes.**

The Service Desk will **never** ask for your password or MFA code. New passwords must have at least 14 characters
and must not repeat any of the last 10 passwords (IS-POL-01). If you suspect someone else knows your password, reset
it immediately and report it as a security incident.

## 7. Corporate smartphone

Corporate smartphones are issued to **field roles** (for example inspectors in Field Operations & Inspection),
**directors** and staff on approved **on-call** rosters (IT, Facilities & Assets, Customer Happiness Centre
supervisors).

- **Approval:** the employee's **Director**.
- **Fulfilment:** 5 working days, with the MNDA Smart field apps, Teams, Outlook and MFA pre-installed.
- **How to request:** MNDA Service Portal → Hardware → Mobile device, stating the business justification.

Devices are managed by the DTO; personal apps may be installed from the managed store only. Lost or stolen phones are
reported immediately on ext. 4357 so the device can be locked and wiped. Employees receiving a phone allowance should
check the allowance rules in HC-HB-03.

## 8. Software installation

- **Approved catalogue:** software listed in MNDA Service Portal → Software (for example the CAD drawing viewer, the
  business-intelligence desktop client, the PDF editor and the Marsa Atlas desktop client) is installed remotely after
  line manager approval. **Fulfilment: 2 working days.**
- **Non-catalogue software** requires an **Information Security review** (licensing, data handling, vendor hosting
  location) before approval. Allow 10 working days; the review may decline software that stores MNDA data outside
  the UAE.
- Users do not have local administrator rights; software cannot be installed without a request.

Browser extensions and public AI tools are covered by the Acceptable Use Policy (IT-POL-02).

## 9. New joiner IT setup

When Human Capital confirms a new joiner in MNDA People, the line manager receives an onboarding task to choose the
equipment and system role profiles.

- Requests completed **at least 5 working days** before the start date are ready on day 1: laptop (Dell Latitude 7450),
  MNDA account, mailbox, Teams, MFA enrolment and the requested role profiles.
- The new joiner collects equipment from the Service Desk, Floor 2, MNDA Tower, at 9:00 on the first day and completes
  the 30-minute security induction.
- Business system access follows section 5 (line manager and system owner approval).

## 10. Incident priorities and service levels

Faults and outages (incidents) are prioritised by impact and urgency.

| Priority | Definition | Example | Response | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Critical** | Major outage affecting a whole service, many users or customers | Tamkeen CMS or MNDA ePermits down; CHC phone system down | **15 minutes** | **4 hours** |
| **P2 — High** | Serious degradation or a key user/team unable to work | VPN failure for a department; Customer Happiness Centre counter offline | **30 minutes** | **8 hours** |
| **P3 — Medium** | Single user affected, workaround available | Printer fault, Outlook error on one laptop | **4 hours** | **3 working days** |
| **P4 — Low** | Minor issue or information request | How-to questions, cosmetic issues | **1 working day** | **5 working days** |

P1 incidents are handled 24/7 by the on-call team and communicated through the Teams channel "MNDA Service Status".
