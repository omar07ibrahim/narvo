# Marina Quarter Tower B — Technical Specification

## Section 07 42 13: Podium Façade Cladding and Rooftop Plant Screening

**Plot:** MN-08-214-0007, Marina Quarter · **Permit application:** MNDA-BP-2026-02217
**Licensed consultant:** Qamar Al Khaleej Engineering Consultants · **Contractor:** Gulf Crest Builders LLC
**Revision:** C, 21 September 2026 (supersedes Rev. B, 31 August 2026)

## 1. Scope

Supply and installation of podium façade cladding (levels P1–P4) and roof equipment screening on the podium roof and tower main roof, including louvres, parapet copings, framing and insulation.

## 2. References

1. MNBC-2024 Art. 5-4 and Art. 5-5, as amended by Amendment No. 1/2026 (MNDA/PBP/MNBC/AMD/01/2026).
2. Circular No. 14/2026 (MNDA/PBP/CIR/14/2026) — master-developer NOC in gated communities.
3. Marina Quarter Properties LLC Community Design Guidelines, rev. 3.

## 3. Performance requirements

- Design wind load: 1.6 kPa; deflection ≤ L/175.
- Opaque podium cladding: U-value ≤ 0.57 W/m²K.
- Glazing reflectivity ≤ 15%.
- Screen height ≥ equipment height + 0.3 m; louvre opacity ≥ 50%.
- Reaction-to-fire classification A2-s1,d0 or better.

Give 48 hours' notice of each hold point. Any change to materials, colours or screen heights requires a new drawing revision and a new NOC.
