# Tender MNDA/PRC/2026/057 — Facilities Management Services: Al Ghaf Community Centre & Four Satellite Community Facilities (24 months)

## Section 7 — Service Levels, Key Performance Indicators and Deductions

### 7.1 General

From the Service Commencement Date (1 January 2027) the Service Provider shall meet the service levels in Table 7-1 at all five facilities. Performance shall be measured monthly from CAFM and BMS records and verified by the Authority's Contract Manager.

### 7.2 Key performance indicators

**Table 7-1**

| KPI | Target | Measurement | Service credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| KPI-01 P1 response time | ≤ 30 min for 98% of P1 work orders | CAFM time stamps | 2% of the Monthly Fee per percentage point below target |
| KPI-02 PPM compliance | ≥ 95% of scheduled PPM tasks completed | CAFM monthly report | 1% per percentage point below target |
| KPI-03 HVAC comfort temperature | 22–24 °C ±1 °C in occupied areas, 97% of readings | BMS 15-minute logs | 0.5% per percentage point below target |
| KPI-04 Helpdesk (24/7) | 95% of calls answered within 20 s | Telephony report | 0.5% per percentage point below target |
| KPI-05 Emiratisation plan | Quarterly milestones met | Quarterly HR report | AED 7,500 per missed milestone |
| KPI-06 ISO 41001 / ISO 45001 | Certificates valid throughout the contract | Certificates on file | 5% of the Monthly Fee per month of lapse |

### 7.3 Deductions

Service credits shall be deducted from the monthly invoice. The cap on deductions in any month is 10% of the Monthly Fee. Service credits are a price adjustment, not a penalty, and are without prejudice to the Authority's other rights.

### 7.4 Liquidated damages

If mobilisation is not complete by the Service Commencement Date, the Service Provider shall pay liquidated damages of AED 8,500 per calendar day of delay, capped at 10% of the Contract Value.

### 7.5 Performance bond

Within 14 days of award, the successful Bidder shall submit an unconditional performance bond of 10% of the Contract Value, issued by a bank licensed in the UAE and valid until 90 days after contract expiry.

### 7.6 Termination for repeated failure

If the cap on deductions is reached, or the same KPI is failed, in three consecutive months, the Authority may issue a default notice. If the failure is not remedied within 30 days, the Authority may terminate the contract and call the performance bond.

### 7.7 Dispute resolution

Disputes shall first be referred to the Contract Manager and the Service Provider's Contract Director for amicable settlement within 30 days, then to the Director, Procurement & Contracts, and finally to the competent courts of Marsa Al Noor.
