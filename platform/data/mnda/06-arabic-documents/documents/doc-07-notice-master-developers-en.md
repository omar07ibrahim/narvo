# Notice — Master-developer NOC issuance timelines and digital NOC exchange

**Ref:** MNDA/PBP/NTC/22/2026
**Date:** 7 October 2026
**From:** Eng. Omar Yousef Al Hammadi, Director, Planning & Building Permits
**To:** Lulu Island Developments PJSC · Sidr Communities LLC · Marina Quarter Properties LLC

## 1. Purpose

This notice shares the Authority's Q3 2026 data on NOC issuance under Circular No. 14/2026 (MNDA/PBP/CIR/14/2026) and announces the digital NOC exchange through MNDA ePermits.

## 2. Q3 2026 issuance times

Circular No. 14/2026 §4.4 requests each master developer to issue NOCs within 7 working days of a complete request. Results for 1 July – 30 September 2026:

| Master developer | District | NOC requests | Average time (working days) | Within 7 working days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lulu Island Developments PJSC | MN-03 | 160 | 12.4 | 29% |
| Sidr Communities LLC | MN-06 | 198 | 8.1 | 61% |
| Marina Quarter Properties LLC | MN-08 | 176 | 7.6 | 68% |

Permit applications in gated communities took a median of 27.9 days, against 16.8 days elsewhere; the status "Awaiting master-developer NOC" accounted for 9.2 days on average.

## 3. Digital NOC exchange from 1 November 2026

1. NOCs will be issued as structured data in MNDA ePermits, linked to the permit application.
2. Each NOC will record the plot number (format MN-DD-SSS-PPPP), the drawing revision, the approved scope (façade, roof equipment screening, boundary walls), the issue date and a validity period of 90 days with automatic expiry.
3. Each NOC will carry a QR code that permit officers, licensed consultants and inspectors can verify.
4. NOCs on letterhead will be accepted until 31 December 2026 only.
5. Please nominate two authorised users by 22 October 2026.

## 4. Revision mismatch

ePermits will compare the drawing revision on the NOC with the revision submitted to the Authority and alert the applicant and the master developer immediately. Where a new revision changes only items outside the community design guidelines, we propose a revision confirmation within 3 working days instead of a new NOC. For fit-out works, an NOC remains required only where the façade, roof or external works are affected.
