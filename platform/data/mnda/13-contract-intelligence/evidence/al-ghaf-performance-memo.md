## 1. Purpose

This memorandum is the Contract Owner's performance review under Clause 3.4.2 of contract MNDA/CON/2023/019 with Raseen Facilities Management LLC. It sets out the facts and the available options as input to the renewal decision under Clause 3.3. It does not recommend an option; the decision rests with Procurement & Contracts in consultation with Legal Affairs.

## 2. Contract Position and Notice Deadline

- **Scope:** facilities management at Al Ghaf Community Centre (MN-07), Al Murjan Community Majlis (MN-02), Al Waha Gardens Community Pavilion (MN-04) and Corniche Family Park Amenity Buildings (MN-01).
- **Value:** AED 4,200,000 per year (AED 350,000 per month), excluding VAT.
- **Term:** 1 January 2024 – 31 December 2026, with one (1) optional twelve-month renewal at MNDA's sole discretion (Clause 3.2), at the same rates (Clause 3.4.1). MNDA may make renewal conditional on a performance improvement plan (Clause 3.4.3).
- **Notice:** MNDA must notify its decision to renew or not to renew not less than sixty (60) days before expiry, i.e. by **Sunday 1 November 2026** (Clause 3.3.1). A notice received on a non-working day is deemed received on the next Working Day (Clause 17.2.2), which would be late; it should be served by Friday 30 October before 12:00, and we suggest **Thursday 29 October 2026**.
- **Performance Security:** AED 630,000, valid to 31 March 2027; to be extended if the term is renewed or extended.

## 4. Al Ghaf Community Centre — Cooling Performance

Al Ghaf Community Centre (built 2019, GFA 14,200 m²) is served by two 350 TR air-cooled screw chillers, CH-01 and CH-02, and eight AHUs.

- **Complaints:** 23 comfort complaints in August–September 2026, concentrated in the Multipurpose Hall and Ladies' Fitness (served by AHU-03 and AHU-06).
- **Chiller trips:** CH-02 tripped on high discharge pressure **11 times in September**, all between 13:00 and 16:00 with ambient temperature above 44 °C.
- **Overdue PPM:** the quarterly condenser-coil cleaning (due 15 August under Schedule 1, S1.4) has been **overdue since 15 August 2026** and was not complete at the date of this memo.
- **Energy:** September chiller energy consumption was **17% higher** than in September 2025.
- **Field job:** Raseen raised JOB-RFM-26-04471 "CH-02 high discharge pressure trip". The job remains open and no written root-cause report has been received.

### 4.1 Engineering View and Contractual Notes

Our engineering view is that the pattern (trips only at peak ambient, rising energy, overdue coil cleaning) is consistent with fouled condenser coils reducing heat rejection, but this is not yet confirmed. We note that Clause 15.1 excludes high ambient temperatures from force majeure, and that Clause 7.2.3 treats additional energy costs resulting from a failure to maintain Assets as recoverable direct loss; Procurement and Legal may wish to consider the latter.

## 7. Interaction with Tender MNDA/PRC/2026/057

Tender MNDA/PRC/2026/057, "Facilities Management Services — Al Ghaf Community Centre & Four Satellite Community Facilities (24 months)", covers the same community facilities. It closes on **22 October 2026**, with a planned contract start of 1 January 2027. Evaluation, approvals, award and mobilisation may not be complete by 31 December 2026, so a bridging arrangement may be needed. Raseen, as incumbent, is understood to be a prospective bidder; the renewal decision under MNDA/CON/2023/019 and the evaluation of tender 057 should be kept procedurally separate.
