# Omnichannel Contact Centre Platform — Subscription and Services Agreement

**Nuvola Contact Systems FZ-LLC — Master Subscription and Services Agreement**
Draft v1.2 issued by the Provider on 6 October 2026 for the Customer's review. Subject to contract.
MNDA file reference: MNDA/CON/2026/DRAFT-031

## Parties

This Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of the Effective Date between:

(1) **Nuvola Contact Systems FZ-LLC**, a free zone limited liability company holding Licence No. CN-3290417, whose registered office is at Office 1904, Tech Park Tower C, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (the "**Provider**"); and

(2) **Marsa Al Noor Development Authority**, of MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, United Arab Emirates (the "**Customer**").

The Provider and the Customer are each a "**Party**". The "**Effective Date**" is the date of the last signature of this Agreement or of the first Order Form, whichever is earlier.

## Clause 1 — Definitions

### 1.1 Defined Terms (A to O)

- "**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party.
- "**AI Features**" means features of the Services that use machine learning or generative models, including the Nuvola Assist chatbot, suggested replies and conversation summaries.
- "**Customer Data**" means data submitted to the Services by or on behalf of the Customer or its end customers, including call recordings, transcripts and messages, but excluding Usage Data.
- "**Documentation**" means the Provider's online user guides and technical documentation, as updated from time to time.
- "**Fees**" means the subscription, usage and Professional Services fees set out in an Order Form.
- "**Named Agent Seat**" means a subscription for one named individual user of the agent workspace. Seats may not be shared and may be reassigned no more than once in any thirty (30) day period.
- "**Order Form**" means an ordering document executed by the Parties that references this Agreement, including the Order Form in Exhibit A.

### 1.2 Defined Terms (P to Z)

- "**Platform**" means the Provider's cloud contact centre software, "Nuvola Engage", and related products listed in an Order Form.
- "**Professional Services**" means implementation, configuration and training services described in an Order Form.
- "**Provider Policies**" means the Acceptable Use Policy, Security Overview, Sub-processor List and Support Policy published on the Trust Portal, as updated from time to time.
- "**Services**" means the Platform, support and any Professional Services.
- "**Sub-processor**" means any third party or Provider Affiliate that processes Customer Data for the Provider.
- "**Subscription Term**" means the Initial Subscription Term and all Renewal Terms.
- "**Third-Party Channels**" means messaging platforms, telephony carriers and other third-party services accessed through the Platform, including the WhatsApp channel.
- "**Trust Portal**" means the Provider's web page at trust.nuvolacontact.ae.
- "**Usage Data**" means data relating to the operation, performance and use of the Services, including data derived from Customer Data.

## Clause 2 — Order Forms and Order of Precedence

**2.1 Order Forms.** The Customer may subscribe to the Services by executing Order Forms. Each Order Form forms part of this Agreement.

**2.2 Precedence.** In case of conflict, the following order of precedence applies: (a) the Order Form, solely for the commercial terms set out in it; (b) this Agreement; and (c) the Provider Policies.

**2.3 Exclusion of Customer Terms.** Any terms contained in a Customer purchase order, tender document, letter of award, vendor registration form or other Customer document shall have no effect and are expressly rejected, even if the Provider signs, acknowledges or performs against such document.

## Clause 3 — Provision of the Services

### 3.1 Access Rights

3.1.1 Subject to this Agreement and payment of the Fees, the Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Subscription Term to access and use the Services for its internal business operations, within the quantities in the applicable Order Form.

3.1.2 Seat quantities may be increased at any time at the Provider's then-current prices, co-terminous with the current Subscription Term. Seat quantities may not be decreased during a Subscription Term.

### 3.2 Changes to the Services and Provider Policies

3.2.1 The Provider continuously develops the Services and may add, modify or remove features and functionality from time to time. The Provider will not materially decrease the overall functionality of the subscribed Services during the then-current Subscription Term.

3.2.2 The Provider may update the Provider Policies from time to time by posting the updated version on the Trust Portal. Updated Provider Policies take effect on posting. The Customer is responsible for reviewing the Trust Portal periodically.

3.2.3 The Provider will give at least ninety (90) days' notice before discontinuing a product, except where discontinuation is required by law or by a Third-Party Channel.

### 3.3 Third-Party Channels, Including WhatsApp

3.3.1 Use of the WhatsApp channel and other Third-Party Channels is subject to the terms and policies of the relevant channel operator, which the Customer shall accept directly. The Provider does not control and is not responsible for the availability, security, pricing or data practices of any Third-Party Channel.

3.3.2 Conversation charges, telephony minutes and other fees levied by Third-Party Channel operators are passed through to the Customer as set out in the Order Form. If an operator changes or withdraws its service, the Provider may modify or discontinue the integration without liability.

### 3.4 AI Features

3.4.1 AI Features generate outputs probabilistically and may produce inaccurate, incomplete or inappropriate content. The Customer is solely responsible for reviewing outputs and for any decisions or communications made in reliance on them, including any information provided to end customers.

3.4.2 AI Features may be provided using third-party model providers engaged as Sub-processors.

3.4.3 The Provider does not warrant that any AI Feature will achieve a particular containment, deflection or resolution rate. Bot sessions above the quantity in the Order Form are charged as overage.

## Clause 4 — Customer Responsibilities and Acceptable Use

**4.1 Responsibilities.** The Customer is responsible for: (a) the accuracy, quality and legality of Customer Data; (b) obtaining all notices and consents required for call recording, messaging and processing of end-customer data; (c) its users' compliance with this Agreement and the Acceptable Use Policy; and (d) maintaining the confidentiality of user credentials.

**4.2 Restrictions.** The Customer shall not: resell or sublicense the Services; reverse engineer the Platform; use the Services to send unsolicited communications; or use the Services in breach of applicable law or Third-Party Channel policies.

**4.3 Suspension for Risk.** The Provider may suspend the Customer's access to all or part of the Services immediately, with notice as soon as practicable, if it reasonably believes that the Customer's use poses a security risk to the Services or any third party, may subject the Provider or its Affiliates to liability, or breaches the Acceptable Use Policy.

## Clause 5 — Professional Services

**5.1 Delivery.** The Provider will perform the Professional Services described in the Order Form. Delivery dates are estimates only.

**5.2 Acceptance.** Each deliverable is deemed accepted five (5) business days after delivery unless the Customer provides a written notice identifying a material non-conformity with the agreed specification. The Provider will correct any such non-conformity, which shall be the Customer's sole remedy.

**5.3 Changes.** Work outside the agreed scope, including delays caused by the Customer's failure to provide information, access or decisions on time, will be charged at the Provider's daily rate of AED 6,240 per consultant day.

## Clause 6 — Fees, Invoicing and Payment

### 6.1 Fees Non-cancellable and Non-refundable

The Customer shall pay all Fees set out in each Order Form. Except as expressly provided in Clause 8.3.2, all Fees are non-cancellable and non-refundable, and payment obligations are not subject to any retention, bond, guarantee, set-off or counterclaim.

### 6.2 Invoicing — Annual Subscription in Advance

6.2.1 Subscription Fees are invoiced annually in advance. The first annual invoice is issued on the Effective Date, and subsequent invoices on each anniversary of the Subscription Start Date.

6.2.2 Professional Services Fees are invoiced in full on the Effective Date. Usage, overage and pass-through charges are invoiced monthly in arrears.

### 6.3 Payment Terms — Fifteen Days

The Customer shall pay each invoice within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, in UAE dirhams, by bank transfer to the account specified on the invoice. The Customer's internal certification, approval or purchase-order processes shall not extend the payment period.

### 6.4 Late Payment Interest

Any amount not paid when due shall bear interest at the rate of one and a half per cent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law if lower, from the due date until the date of payment. The Customer shall also reimburse the Provider's reasonable costs of collection, including legal fees.

### 6.5 Suspension for Non-Payment

If any amount owed by the Customer is ten (10) or more days overdue, the Provider may, without limiting its other rights and remedies, suspend the Services until such amounts are paid in full, on giving the Customer at least three (3) days' prior notice by email. Fees continue to accrue during any suspension.

### 6.6 Annual Fee Adjustment — Seven Per Cent

The subscription Fees shall increase by seven per cent (7%) on each anniversary of the Subscription Start Date, including during the Initial Subscription Term and any Renewal Term, applied to the Fees for the immediately preceding twelve (12) months. The Fees for each year of the Initial Subscription Term are set out in Exhibit A, Section A.3.

### 6.7 Taxes

Fees are exclusive of VAT and all other taxes, duties and levies, which shall be paid by the Customer. If any withholding is required by law, the Customer shall gross up its payment so that the Provider receives the full invoiced amount.

## Clause 7 — Term and Renewal

### 7.1 Term of Agreement

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until all Subscription Terms under all Order Forms have expired or been terminated.

### 7.2 Initial Subscription Term — Three Years

The initial subscription term for each Order Form is thirty-six (36) months from the Subscription Start Date stated in the Order Form (the "**Initial Subscription Term**").

### 7.3 Automatic Renewal

Each Order Form shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Term**"), unless the Customer notifies the Provider in writing of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the expiry of the then-current Subscription Term. Notice of non-renewal must be sent to renewals@nuvolacontact.ae and is effective only upon written acknowledgement by the Provider.

### 7.4 Renewal Pricing

The Fees for each Renewal Term shall be the Fees for the final twelve (12) months of the preceding term, increased in accordance with Clause 6.6. Any promotional or first-term discount shall expire at the end of the Initial Subscription Term, and renewal Fees shall then be calculated from the Provider's list price.

## Clause 8 — Termination

### 8.1 Termination for Material Breach

Either Party may terminate this Agreement or an Order Form by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail.

### 8.2 No Termination for Convenience

The Customer acknowledges that the Fees reflect a committed Subscription Term. Except as expressly set out in Clause 8.1 and Clause 18.2, neither Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for convenience before the end of the then-current Subscription Term.

### 8.3 Effect of Termination

8.3.1 If this Agreement is terminated for any reason other than the Provider's uncured material breach, all Fees for the remainder of the then-current Subscription Term become immediately due and payable.

8.3.2 If the Customer terminates for the Provider's uncured material breach, the Provider will refund any prepaid subscription Fees covering the remainder of the Subscription Term after the effective date of termination.

### 8.4 Data Export and Deletion

8.4.1 For thirty (30) days after termination or expiry, the Provider will make Customer Data available for export by the Customer through the Platform's self-service export tools in the Provider's standard formats. Assisted export is available at the rate in Clause 5.3.

8.4.2 After that period, the Provider has no obligation to maintain or provide Customer Data and will delete it in the ordinary course, save for copies retained in backups, which will be overwritten in accordance with the Provider's backup cycle.

## Clause 9 — Service Levels

**9.1 Service Level Agreement.** The Provider will provide the Platform in accordance with the Service Level Agreement in Exhibit B, including a monthly availability commitment of 99.9%.

**9.2 Service Credits as Sole and Exclusive Remedy.** Service credits under Exhibit B are capped at five per cent (5%) of the monthly subscription Fee for the affected product in any month. Service credits are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy, and the Provider's sole and entire liability, for any failure to meet the Service Level Agreement or any unavailability of the Services.

**9.3 Claims.** The Customer must request service credits in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month. Service credits are applied only against future invoices, have no cash value and are forfeited on termination.

## Clause 10 — Customer Data, Privacy and Security

### 10.1 Ownership and Licence of Customer Data

As between the Parties, the Customer owns Customer Data. The Customer grants the Provider and its Affiliates and Sub-processors a worldwide, non-exclusive licence to host, copy, transmit, display and process Customer Data as necessary to provide, secure and support the Services and as otherwise permitted by this Agreement.

### 10.2 Processing Locations

Customer Data may be stored and processed in the Provider's data centres in the European Union and the GCC region and by its sub-processors listed at the Provider's trust portal, which the Provider may update from time to time. The primary hosting region for the Customer's tenant is stated in the Order Form. Support personnel of the Provider and its Affiliates may access Customer Data from the locations from which support is provided under Exhibit C.

### 10.3 Sub-processors

10.3.1 The Customer authorises the Provider to engage the Sub-processors listed on the Trust Portal. The Provider may add or replace Sub-processors by updating the Sub-processor List. The Customer may subscribe to email notifications of updates.

10.3.2 The Customer may object to a new Sub-processor on reasonable data protection grounds within ten (10) days of the update. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection within thirty (30) days, the Customer may terminate the affected Order Form as its sole and exclusive remedy, without refund of prepaid Fees.

### 10.4 Security Measures

The Provider will maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards for the Services as described in the Security Overview, and will maintain ISO/IEC 27001 certification for the Platform. The Provider may update its safeguards from time to time, provided that the overall level of security is not materially reduced.

### 10.5 Security Incident Notification

The Provider will notify the Customer without undue delay after confirming a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of or access to Customer Data. Notification is provided by email to the Customer's designated security contact and does not constitute an acknowledgement of fault or liability.

### 10.6 Usage Data and Product Improvement

The Provider may collect and use Usage Data, and may create aggregated and de-identified Usage Data from Customer Data, to operate, improve and develop the Provider's products and services, including training and tuning the models used in AI Features, and for benchmarking and analytics. The Provider owns all Usage Data and all improvements, enhancements and derivative works arising from it. Aggregated and de-identified Usage Data shall not identify the Customer or any individual.

## Clause 11 — Confidentiality

**11.1 Obligations.** Each Party shall protect the other's confidential information with reasonable care and use it only to perform or receive the Services. Either Party may disclose confidential information to its Affiliates, Sub-processors, auditors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

**11.2 Exceptions.** Confidential information excludes information that is or becomes public without breach, was known to the recipient before disclosure, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without restriction.

**11.3 Duration.** The obligations in this Clause 11 continue for three (3) years from the date of each disclosure.

## Clause 12 — Intellectual Property

**12.1 Provider Ownership.** The Provider and its licensors own all rights in the Services, the Documentation, Usage Data and all improvements, modifications and derivative works of any of them, including any configurations, IVR flows, chatbot intents, templates and integrations created in the course of Professional Services ("**Provider Materials**").

**12.2 Licence to Deliverables.** The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use Provider Materials delivered to it during the Subscription Term, solely in connection with the Services.

**12.3 Feedback.** The Provider may freely use any suggestions or feedback provided by the Customer or its users without obligation.

**13.1 Limited Warranty.** The Provider warrants that the Platform will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation. The Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is for the Provider to correct the non-conformity or, if the Provider cannot do so within a reasonable period, for either Party to terminate the affected Order Form, in which case the Provider will refund prepaid Fees for the remainder of the Subscription Term.

**13.2 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the Services are provided "as is", all implied warranties are disclaimed, and the Provider does not warrant that the Services, AI Features or Third-Party Channels will be uninterrupted or error-free.

## Clause 14 — Indemnification

**14.1 By the Provider.** The Provider will defend the Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Platform, as provided by the Provider, infringes that third party's intellectual property rights, and pay damages finally awarded. This obligation does not apply to claims arising from Customer Data, AI Feature outputs, Third-Party Channels, or combination of the Services with items not supplied by the Provider. The Provider may instead modify the Services, procure a licence, or terminate the affected Services and refund prepaid unused Fees.

**14.2 By the Customer.** The Customer will defend and indemnify the Provider and its Affiliates against any third-party claim arising from Customer Data, the Customer's communications with its end customers, or the Customer's use of the Services in breach of this Agreement or applicable law.

## Clause 15 — Limitation of Liability

### 15.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages

Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for any loss of profits, revenue, goodwill or anticipated savings, loss or corruption of data, business interruption, or regulatory fines or penalties, however caused and whether or not foreseeable.

### 15.2 Aggregate Liability Cap — Fifty Per Cent of Fees Paid

The Provider's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement and all Order Forms, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, shall not exceed fifty per cent (50%) of the Fees actually paid by the Customer under the applicable Order Form in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

### 15.3 Exceptions

Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes either Party's liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence. The limitations in this Clause 15 apply to all other liability, including liability for breach of Clause 10 or Clause 11 and under Clause 14, and do not limit the Customer's obligation to pay Fees.

## Clause 16 — Insurance

During the Subscription Term, the Provider will maintain commercial general liability insurance with a limit of not less than USD 2,000,000 per occurrence, and workers' compensation insurance as required by the laws of the jurisdictions in which its personnel are employed. The Provider will provide a certificate of insurance on the Customer's written request, not more than once in any twelve (12) month period.

## Clause 17 — Subcontracting and Assignment

**17.1 Subcontracting.** The Provider may use its Affiliates and Sub-processors to perform any part of the Services without the Customer's consent. The Provider remains responsible for the performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

**17.2 Assignment by the Provider.** The Provider may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganisation or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, on written notice to the Customer.

**17.3 Assignment by the Customer.** The Customer may not assign this Agreement without the Provider's prior written consent.

## Clause 18 — Force Majeure

**18.1 Excused Performance.** Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, epidemics, war, terrorism, acts of government, labour disputes, failures of the internet, telecommunications networks or third-party hosting providers, denial-of-service and other cyber-attacks, and the acts or omissions of Third-Party Channel operators.

**18.2 Prolonged Events.** If a force majeure event prevents the provision of substantially all of the Services for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, either Party may terminate the affected Order Form on written notice, and the Provider will refund prepaid unused Fees.

## Clause 19 — Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**19.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

**19.2 Arbitration.** Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the LCIA Rules, which are deemed incorporated by reference. The tribunal shall consist of one (1) arbitrator. The seat of arbitration shall be London, England, and the language of the arbitration shall be English.

**19.3 Injunctive Relief.** Nothing in this Clause 19 prevents the Provider from seeking injunctive or interim relief, or from bringing proceedings to recover unpaid Fees, in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## Clause 20 — General Provisions

**20.1 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including the Order Forms and the Provider Policies incorporated by reference, is the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior proposals, tender responses, representations and understandings.

**20.2 Publicity.** The Provider may identify the Customer as a customer, including by use of the Customer's name and logo, on its website and in marketing materials and customer lists.

**20.3 Notices.** Notices to the Provider shall be sent to legal@nuvolacontact.ae. Notices to the Customer shall be sent to the email address in the Order Form and are effective on sending.
