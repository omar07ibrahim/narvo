## Clause 1 — Definitions & Interpretation

### 1.2 Defined Terms — Operational

- "**Assets**" means the building fabric, plant, equipment and systems at the Sites, including those identified in Schedule 1.

- "**PPM**" means planned preventive maintenance under the PPM Schedule.

- "**Work Order**" means an instruction for work logged in the CAFM System.

## Clause 2 — Scope of Facilities Management Services

### 2.1 Services, Sites and Standard of Care

2.1.1 The Contractor shall provide integrated facilities management services at the Sites listed in Schedule 1, comprising the hard services (Clauses 2.2 and 2.3), soft services (Clause 2.4), helpdesk and CAFM services (Clause 2.5) and reactive works (Clause 2.7) (together, the "**Services**").

2.1.2 The Contractor shall perform the Services with the skill, care and diligence of a competent and experienced facilities management contractor, in accordance with good industry practice, applicable law, manufacturers' recommendations and MNDA policies notified to it in writing.

2.1.3 No additional payment is due for any task reasonably inferable as necessary for the proper performance of the Services.

### 2.2 Hard Services — Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing

The hard services comprise planned, reactive and statutory maintenance of: (a) low-voltage distribution, lighting, emergency lighting, standby generators and UPS units; (b) plumbing, drainage, water tanks and booster pumps, including six-monthly tank disinfection and monthly water sampling; (c) fire detection, alarm and fire-fighting systems, through a contractor holding the civil-defence approvals required by law; (d) passenger lifts, through an approved subcontractor under Clause 13.2; (e) BMS field devices and controllers; (f) building fabric, doors, ceilings, flooring, sanitary fixtures and minor civil repairs; and (g) access control, CCTV hardware and public-address systems, excluding live monitoring.

### 2.3 HVAC and Chiller Plant Maintenance

2.3.1 The Contractor shall maintain all HVAC equipment at the Sites, including the two (2) air-cooled screw chillers CH-01 and CH-02 (350 TR each) and the eight (8) air-handling units at Al Ghaf Community Centre, chilled-water pumps, fan-coil units, split and packaged units and ventilation fans.

2.3.2 The condenser coils of all air-cooled chillers shall be cleaned quarterly in accordance with the PPM Schedule and Section S1.4 of Schedule 1.

2.3.3 The Contractor shall log chiller operating parameters (suction and discharge pressures, chilled-water temperatures, compressor current) daily from 1 May to 31 October and weekly otherwise, and shall report any trip, alarm or abnormal trend to the Contract Owner within one (1) Working Day.

2.3.4 Refrigerant shall be handled only by certified technicians, with all quantities charged or recovered logged in the CAFM System.

### 2.4 Soft Services — Cleaning, Pest Control, Waste and Landscaping

The soft services comprise: (a) daily cleaning of internal areas during operating hours and monthly deep cleaning of the Multipurpose Hall, fitness areas and kitchens; (b) washroom consumables, excluding paper products supplied by MNDA; (c) monthly and on-call pest control by a licensed operator; (d) segregated waste collection to the municipal collection point; (e) façade and glazing cleaning twice in each Contract Year; and (f) soft landscaping and irrigation within the plot of the Corniche Family Park Amenity Buildings. Cleaning chemicals require the Contract Owner's prior approval.

### 2.5 Helpdesk, CAFM System and Asset Register

2.5.1 The Contractor shall operate a 24/7 helpdesk (telephone, email and CAFM portal) logging every request as a Work Order with a unique reference, priority and timestamp.

2.5.2 Until MNDA designates an MNDA-owned CAFM System by written notice, the Contractor shall use its own system (RFM Connect), give MNDA read-only access for at least five (5) named users and provide a full monthly data export in CSV format. Within sixty (60) days after such designation, the Contractor shall log all Work Orders and PPM tasks directly in MNDA's CAFM System.

2.5.3 The Contractor shall complete a condition survey of all Assets and populate the Asset Register by 29 February 2024, and keep it current throughout the Term.

2.5.4 All CAFM records are MNDA Data (Clause 11) and Deliverables (Clause 12).

### 2.6 Staffing and Resident Team

2.6.1 The Contractor shall maintain as a minimum: (a) at Al Ghaf Community Centre, on each operating day, one (1) HVAC technician, one (1) electrician, one (1) plumber and one (1) multi-skilled technician, plus nine (9) cleaners; (b) a mobile team of three (3) technicians for the other Sites; and (c) a dedicated contract manager and helpdesk coordinator.

2.6.2 The contract manager shall not be replaced without the Contract Owner's prior written approval, save on resignation, serious illness or death.

2.6.3 MNDA may require the removal of any individual for reasonable cause.

### 2.7 Reactive Works and Inclusive Repair Threshold

2.7.1 Reactive repairs, including labour and materials, up to AED 2,500 per Work Order (the "**Inclusive Repair Threshold**") are included in the Contract Price.

2.7.2 Above that threshold, the Contractor shall Make Safe and submit a quotation priced from the schedule of rates in its proposal; the work proceeds only on the Contract Owner's written instruction under a separate purchase order.

2.7.3 The Contractor shall not split a repair into several Work Orders to stay below the threshold. End-of-life replacement of whole Assets is excluded unless instructed under Clause 2.7.2.

## Clause 3 — Term & Renewal

### 3.1 Initial Term

3.1.1 This Agreement takes effect on signature. The Services shall be provided for an initial term commencing on the Commencement Date, 1 January 2024, and expiring on 31 December 2026 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Clause 9.

3.1.2 The period between signature and the Commencement Date is the mobilisation period (Clause 6.4.1).

### 3.2 Renewal Option — One Optional Twelve-Month Renewal

3.2.1 MNDA may, at its sole discretion, renew this Agreement for one (1) optional renewal term of twelve (12) months, from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2027 (the "**Renewal Term**").

3.2.2 The option may be exercised only once, only by MNDA and only by written notice of renewal served under Clause 3.3 (a "**Renewal Notice**"). The Contractor has no right to require renewal, and no renewal shall arise by implication, conduct, continued performance or acceptance of payment.

3.2.3 Nothing in this Clause 3.2 limits MNDA's rights under Clause 9 during the Renewal Term, including termination for convenience under Clause 9.1.

### 3.3 Notice of Renewal or Non-Renewal — Sixty Days Before Expiry

3.3.1 MNDA shall notify the Contractor in writing of its decision to renew or not to renew not less than sixty (60) days before the Expiry Date.

3.3.2 A notice under this Clause 3.3 shall be signed by MNDA's Director, Procurement & Contracts, or his authorised delegate, and served under Clause 17. The date of service is determined by the deemed-receipt rules in Clause 17.2.

3.3.3 If MNDA does not serve a Renewal Notice within the period in Clause 3.3.1, the renewal option lapses and this Agreement expires on the Expiry Date without liability of MNDA to the Contractor.

### 3.4 Renewal Terms and Performance Review

3.4.1 Any Renewal Term shall be on the same terms and at the same rates as the preceding Contract Year, without indexation or price adjustment.

3.4.2 Renewal is subject to a performance review by the Contract Owner covering at least the preceding twelve (12) months, taking into account KPI results, Service Credits applied, customer satisfaction, HSE record and compliance with Clauses 8 and 14.

3.4.3 MNDA may make renewal conditional on the Contractor's written acceptance of a performance improvement plan with measurable milestones. Failure to meet those milestones during the Renewal Term is a breach for the purposes of Clause 9.2.

3.4.4 Before the Renewal Term commences, the Contractor shall extend the Performance Security (Clause 4.6.2) and deliver renewed insurance certificates (Clause 8.3).

### 3.5 Extension Other Than by Renewal

Save for the Renewal Term under Clause 3.2, the Term may be extended only by a written variation agreement signed by the authorised signatories of both Parties and approved under MNDA's procurement regulations. Unless that variation provides otherwise, any extension shall be at the Monthly Fee pro rata and on the terms of this Agreement, and the Contractor shall extend the Performance Security and its insurances to cover the extension period plus ninety (90) days.

## Clause 5 — Performance Standards & KPIs

### 5.1 Performance Standards, PPM Schedule and Certifications

5.1.1 The Contractor shall perform the Services to the standards in Schedule 1 and meet or exceed each KPI in Schedule 2.

5.1.2 The Contractor shall submit the PPM Schedule for each Contract Year by 15 November of the preceding year (for 2024, by 22 December 2023) for the Contract Owner's approval, with task frequencies no less onerous than manufacturers' recommendations and Section S1.4 of Schedule 1.

5.1.3 A PPM task may be rescheduled only with the Contract Owner's written approval obtained before its window closes; otherwise it counts as not completed.

5.1.4 The Contractor shall maintain ISO 41001, ISO 45001 and ISO 9001 certification throughout the Term and notify MNDA within five (5) Working Days of any suspension or withdrawal.

### 5.2 KPI Measurement and Verification

5.2.1 KPIs are measured monthly, or quarterly where Schedule 2 so provides, from CAFM data under the rules in Section S2.3 of Schedule 2. Timestamps shall not be altered after closure of a Work Order except by audited correction.

5.2.2 MNDA may verify any KPI by inspection, sample re-performance or interrogation of the CAFM System. Where MNDA's verified figure differs from the reported figure by more than one (1) percentage point, MNDA's figure applies.

5.2.3 Customer satisfaction is measured quarterly by an MNDA-administered survey of facility users and centre management on a five-point scale.

### 5.3 Monthly Performance Report — Fifth Working Day

By the fifth (5th) Working Day of each month, the Contractor shall submit a Monthly Performance Report for the preceding month containing at least: (a) KPI results by Site; (b) PPM tasks scheduled, completed, rescheduled and overdue, with reasons; (c) reactive Work Orders by priority and Site, including every P1 Fault; (d) chiller and major plant trips, alarms and trends under Clause 2.3.3; (e) energy and water consumption by Site against the same month of the previous year; (f) HSE statistics; (g) staffing against Clause 2.6; (h) the self-assessed Service Credit calculation; and (i) open risks and recommended capital replacements.

### 5.4 Monthly Performance Review Meeting

5.4.1 A monthly performance review meeting shall be held within ten (10) Working Days after receipt of the Monthly Performance Report, attended by the Contractor's contract manager and the Contract Owner's nominated facilities engineer.

5.4.2 The meeting shall review the report, agree the month's Service Credits, track open actions and agree any remediation plan under Clause 5.6. Minutes circulated by the Contractor and not challenged within five (5) Working Days are deemed accepted.

### 5.5 Quarterly Contract Review Chaired by Facilities & Assets

A quarterly contract review shall be chaired by the Contract Owner on behalf of the Facilities & Assets Department and attended by a Procurement & Contracts representative and a Contractor director with authority to commit resources. It shall consider performance trends, customer satisfaction, Service Credits, risks, lifecycle replacements and, in the final Contract Year of the Initial Term, the performance review under Clause 3.4.2.

### 5.6 Remediation Plan

5.6.1 If any KPI is missed in two (2) consecutive measurement periods, or the Contract Owner otherwise reasonably considers performance unsatisfactory, the Contract Owner may require a remediation plan within ten (10) Working Days setting out root causes, corrective actions, additional resources and dates.

5.6.2 The Contractor shall implement the approved plan at its own cost. Approval does not waive any Service Credit or other right of MNDA.

### 5.7 Records and Audit Access — MNDA Internal Audit

The Contractor shall keep complete records relating to the Services, the Contract Price and its subcontractors for five (5) years after the end of the Term. MNDA, MNDA Internal Audit and any auditor appointed by MNDA may, on five (5) Working Days' notice, inspect and copy those records, interview personnel and access CAFM data during the Term and for five (5) years after its end, with reasonable assistance from the Contractor at its own cost.

## Clause 6 — Service Credits & Liquidated Damages

### 6.1 Service Credits for PPM Compliance

6.1.1 Where PPM Compliance in any month falls below ninety-five per cent (95%), the Contractor shall credit MNDA one per cent (1%) of the Monthly Fee for each full percentage point by which PPM Compliance falls below 95%.

6.1.2 By way of example, PPM Compliance of 92.4% gives a Service Credit of 2% of the Monthly Fee (AED 7,000).

6.1.3 Service Credits under this Clause 6.1 and Clause 6.2 together shall not exceed ten per cent (10%) of the Monthly Fee, being AED 35,000, in any month.

### 6.2 Service Credits for Other KPIs

For each other KPI with a Service Credit shown in Section S2.1 of Schedule 2 that is not met in its measurement period, the Contractor shall credit MNDA the percentage of the Monthly Fee shown against it. Credits for quarterly KPIs accrue in the last month of the quarter. The cap in Clause 6.1.3 applies to all Service Credits accruing in a month.

### 6.3 Persistent Under-Performance — Material Breach

6.3.1 If PPM Compliance is below ninety per cent (90%) in three (3) consecutive months, that shall constitute a material breach entitling MNDA to terminate under Clause 9.3(a) without further cure period.

6.3.2 If the cap in Clause 6.1.3 is reached in any three (3) months within a rolling six (6) months, MNDA may also require additional resources at the Contractor's cost until two (2) consecutive months of full KPI compliance are achieved.

### 6.4 Liquidated Damages for Delay in Mobilisation

6.4.1 The Contractor shall achieve full mobilisation by the Commencement Date (personnel under Clause 2.6 deployed, helpdesk operational, insurances and Performance Security in place, PPM Schedule submitted) and complete the Asset Register under Clause 2.5.3 by 29 February 2024.

6.4.2 For each week or part week of delay in either milestone, the Contractor shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the Contract Price (AED 21,000), up to a maximum of ten per cent (10%) of the Contract Price (AED 420,000), being a genuine pre-estimate of MNDA's loss.

### 6.5 Nature of Service Credits and Liquidated Damages

Service Credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services and are not a penalty. They are without prejudice to MNDA's other rights, including termination under Clause 9 and recovery of losses exceeding the Service Credits. Payment or deduction of Service Credits or liquidated damages does not relieve the Contractor of its obligation to complete overdue PPM tasks or rectify faults.

## Clause 7 — Liability & Indemnity

### 7.1 Contractor Indemnity

The Contractor shall indemnify MNDA, its employees and agents against all losses, claims, costs and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from: (a) death or personal injury caused by the Contractor, its personnel or subcontractors; (b) loss of or damage to MNDA or third-party property; (c) any breach of this Agreement or negligent act or omission of the Contractor, its personnel or subcontractors; (d) claims by the Contractor's personnel, including for wages, end-of-service benefits or visas; and (e) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights by the Services or Deliverables.

7.2.2 Service Credits and liquidated damages do not count towards that cap.

7.2.3 Neither Party is liable for loss of profit or indirect loss, save that MNDA may recover as direct loss the cost of replacement services, additional energy costs resulting from the Contractor's failure to maintain Assets, and the cost of repairing or replacing damaged Assets.

### 7.3 Exclusions from the Liability Cap

The limitation in Clause 7.2.1 does not apply to the Contractor's liability for: (a) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (b) wilful misconduct or abandonment of the Services; (c) death or personal injury; (d) breach of Clause 10 (Confidentiality) or Clause 11 (Data Protection & Data Residency); (e) infringement of intellectual property rights under Clause 7.1(e) or Clause 12; or (f) any liability which cannot be limited under UAE law. Liability under these heads is unlimited.

MNDA's total liability to the Contractor is limited to sums properly due and unpaid for Services performed in accordance with this Agreement, together with, on termination under Clause 9.1, the sums payable under Clause 9.5.2. MNDA has no liability for the Contractor's loss of profit, loss of opportunity or indirect loss.

## Clause 9 — Termination

### 9.1 Termination for Convenience by MNDA — Thirty Days

MNDA may terminate this Agreement, in whole or for one or more Sites, for convenience at any time, including during any Renewal Term or extension, on not less than thirty (30) days' written notice. A partial termination reduces the Monthly Fee by the amount allocated to the omitted Site in Clause 4.2.

### 9.2 Termination for Cause — Fourteen-Day Cure Period

9.2.1 If the Contractor commits a breach, MNDA may give written notice specifying the breach and requiring it to be remedied within fourteen (14) days.

9.2.2 If the breach is not remedied to MNDA's reasonable satisfaction within that period, MNDA may terminate this Agreement, in whole or for one or more Sites, with immediate effect by further written notice.

9.2.3 In addition or as an alternative, MNDA may engage a third party to perform the affected Services at the Contractor's cost to the extent exceeding the corresponding portion of the Contract Price.

### 9.3 Immediate Termination Events

MNDA may terminate with immediate effect by written notice if: (a) a material breach occurs under Clause 6.3.1; (b) the Contractor abandons the Services or any Site for more than forty-eight (48) consecutive hours; (c) the Contractor or its personnel offer any bribe, gift or inducement to an MNDA employee; (d) the Contractor commits fraud; (e) the Contractor becomes insolvent or its trade licence is cancelled or not renewed; or (f) liquidated damages reach the maximum in Clause 6.4.2.

### 9.4 Termination by the Contractor

The Contractor may terminate only if MNDA fails to pay an undisputed, certified sum for more than ninety (90) days after its due date and does not pay within a further thirty (30) days after written notice of the Contractor's intention to terminate. The Contractor has no other right to terminate or to suspend the Services.

### 9.5 Consequences of Termination or Expiry

9.5.1 Termination or expiry does not affect accrued rights or the continuation of Clauses 5.7, 7, 9.6, 10, 11, 12 and 16.

9.5.2 On termination under Clause 9.1, MNDA shall pay the Monthly Fee pro rata to the termination date and reasonable, documented demobilisation costs approved by the Contract Owner, up to AED 75,000. No compensation is payable for loss of anticipated profit.

9.5.3 On termination under Clause 9.2 or 9.3, MNDA may withhold payment until the excess cost under Clause 9.2.3 is determined, and may call the Performance Security.

### 9.6 Exit Management and Handover to a Successor Contractor

9.6.1 Not less than ninety (90) days before the Expiry Date, or within seven (7) days after a notice of termination, the Contractor shall submit an exit plan for the Contract Owner's approval.

9.6.2 During the last sixty (60) days of the Term or notice period, the Contractor shall cooperate in good faith with MNDA and any successor contractor, allow joint site surveys and hand over the Asset Register, PPM records, open Work Orders, O&M manuals, as-built drawings, keys, access cards, statutory certificates and a full CAFM data export.

9.6.3 The Contractor shall not withdraw personnel or reduce service levels during handover. The final Monthly Fee is payable only after the Contract Owner certifies handover complete.

**10.2 Publicity.** The Contractor shall not make public statements about this Agreement or use MNDA's name or logo without MNDA's prior written consent.

**10.3 Exceptions and Survival.** Clause 10.1 does not apply to information that becomes public other than by breach. These obligations survive expiry or termination for five (5) years; obligations concerning security arrangements, access codes and personal data continue without limit of time.

## Clause 11 — Data Protection & Data Residency

### 11.1 MNDA Data and Personal Data
