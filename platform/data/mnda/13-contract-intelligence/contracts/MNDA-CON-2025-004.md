## Parties and Recitals

THIS AGREEMENT is made in Marsa Al Noor on 13 March 2025 BETWEEN:

(1) **Marsa Al Noor Development Authority**, a government authority established under Law No. 4 of 2011, of MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, United Arab Emirates ("**MNDA**"); and

(2) **Hisn Security Services LLC**, a limited liability company holding Trade Licence No. CN-2318640, of Office 1103, Al Noor Business Centre, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, United Arab Emirates (the "**Contractor**").

WHEREAS, following Tender No. MNDA/PRC/2024/087, MNDA awarded the Contractor the provision of security guarding services at MNDA Tower and the three Customer Happiness Centres by Letter of Award No. MNDA/PRC/LOA/087/2024.

NOW IT IS AGREED as follows.

## Clause 1 — Definitions & Interpretation

### 1.1 Defined Terms

- "**Contract Owner**" means MNDA's Director, Facilities & Assets, currently Eng. Tariq Mahmoud Saleh.
- "**Contract Price**" means AED 2,946,000 per Contract Year, exclusive of VAT.
- "**Contract Year**" means each twelve-month period from 1 April.
- "**Monthly Fee**" means AED 245,500, being one-twelfth of the Contract Price.
- "**Post**" means a security position listed in Section S1.2 of Schedule 1, with its location, hours and duties.
- "**Post-Shift**" means one Post for one rostered shift.
- "**Unmanned Post-Shift**" means a Post-Shift in which the Post is not occupied by a Security Officer meeting Clause 2.3 for more than thirty (30) cumulative minutes, excluding relieved breaks.
- "**Post Coverage**" means manned Post-hours divided by rostered Post-hours in a month, as a percentage.
- "**Security Officer**" means any guard, supervisor or control-room operator deployed by the Contractor.
- "**Sites**" means MNDA Tower and the Customer Happiness Centres listed in Section S1.1 of Schedule 1.
- "**Working Day**" means Monday to Friday, excluding UAE government-sector public holidays.

### 1.2 Interpretation and Order of Precedence

The Agreement comprises, in descending order of precedence: (a) these Clauses; (b) Schedule 2 (KPIs); (c) Schedule 1 (Sites and Posts); (d) the Letter of Award; (e) the tender documents; and (f) the Contractor's proposal dated 21 November 2024 as clarified. "Including" means "including without limitation". This Agreement is executed in Arabic and English; the Arabic text prevails before UAE courts.

## Clause 2 — Scope of Security Guarding Services

### 2.1 Services

2.1.1 The Contractor shall provide manned security guarding at the Sites in accordance with Schedule 1, including: access control and visitor management; screening of mail and deliveries at MNDA Tower; operation of the Security Control Room (CCTV and access control); internal and perimeter patrols; queue and crowd management at the Customer Happiness Centres; first response to incidents, alarms and medical emergencies; key control; and support to evacuation drills (the "**Services**").

2.1.2 The Contractor shall perform the Services in accordance with the post orders approved by the Contract Owner, applicable law, and MNDA's security, HSE and customer-service policies.

### 2.2 Post Orders and Customer Conduct

2.2.1 The Contractor shall prepare written post orders for each Post, in Arabic and English, within fourteen (14) days of the Commencement Date for the Contract Owner's approval, and review them annually.

2.2.2 Security Officers at the Customer Happiness Centres shall treat customers courteously, apply MNDA's customer-happiness standards, and escalate disputes to the Centre manager on duty rather than intervene physically, save in emergency.

2.2.3 A female Security Officer shall be on duty in the ladies' section of each Customer Happiness Centre at all opening hours.

### 2.3 Licensing and Competence of Security Officers

2.3.1 Every Security Officer shall, before deployment and at all times while deployed, hold a valid security guard licence issued by the competent licensing authority in the UAE for the category of duty performed. Control-room operators shall also hold the relevant CCTV operator certification.

2.3.2 Each Security Officer shall be at least twenty-one (21) years old, medically fit, and able to communicate in Arabic or English (supervisors in both), and shall complete MNDA site induction, first aid and fire-warden training before deployment.

2.3.3 No Security Officer whose licence has expired or been suspended shall be deployed. A Post-Shift covered by such an officer is an Unmanned Post-Shift.

### 2.4 Vetting and Annual Re-vetting by 31 March

2.4.1 Before first deployment, the Contractor shall vet every Security Officer, including identity and right-to-work checks, a certificate of good conduct, employment history for the previous five (5) years and medical and drug screening, and submit the vetting file to the Contract Owner.

2.4.2 The Contractor shall re-vet every deployed Security Officer annually, completing the re-vetting by 31 March of each year, covering licence validity, a renewed certificate of good conduct, medical fitness and sixteen (16) hours of refresher training, and shall submit a re-vetting certificate signed by its general manager to the Contract Owner by 7 April.

2.4.3 MNDA may refuse or require the removal of any Security Officer at any time for reasonable cause, without giving reasons where security so requires.

### 2.5 Labour Law, Welfare and Summer Working Conditions

2.5.1 The Contractor shall comply with UAE labour law, pay all wages through the Wage Protection System, provide compliant accommodation and transport, and observe maximum working hours, rest days and overtime limits.

2.5.2 During the summer midday work ban (15 June to 15 September, 12:30 to 15:00), Security Officers at outdoor Posts shall work only from shaded, air-conditioned booths or be rotated to indoor duties, without reducing Post Coverage.

2.5.3 MNDA may audit the Contractor's payroll, accommodation and rosters for compliance.

## Clause 3 — Term & No Renewal

### 3.1 Term

This Agreement takes effect on signature. The Services shall be provided from 1 April 2025 (the "**Commencement Date**") until 31 March 2028 (the "**Expiry Date**") (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Clause 9.

### 3.2 No Renewal — Re-tender of the Services

3.2.1 This Agreement contains no renewal option and shall expire on the Expiry Date. MNDA intends to re-tender the Services before the Expiry Date, and the Contractor may participate in that tender on equal terms.

3.2.2 Any extension of the Term, including a short extension pending award of a successor contract, may be made only by a written variation agreement signed by the authorised signatories of both Parties and approved under MNDA's procurement regulations, at the Monthly Fee pro rata unless otherwise agreed.

3.2.3 Continued performance after the Expiry Date without such variation shall not extend or renew this Agreement.

## Clause 4 — Contract Price & Payment

### 4.1 Contract Price and Breakdown by Site

4.1.1 MNDA shall pay the Contract Price of AED 2,946,000 per Contract Year, exclusive of VAT, in twelve (12) monthly instalments of AED 245,500. The Total Contract Value is AED 8,838,000, exclusive of VAT.

| Site | Monthly (AED) | Annual (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| MNDA Tower (MN-01) | 168,940 | 2,027,280 |
| Customer Happiness Centre — Downtown (MN-01) | 31,260 | 375,120 |
| Customer Happiness Centre — Al Murjan (MN-02) | 22,650 | 271,800 |
| Customer Happiness Centre — Al Waha (MN-04) | 22,650 | 271,800 |
| **Total** | **245,500** | **2,946,000** |

4.1.2 The Contract Price includes all salaries, allowances, overtime, relief cover, uniforms, equipment, radios, guard-tour system, vehicles, accommodation, transport, visas, licensing, training, insurances, overheads and profit.

### 4.2 Additional Guarding and Fixed Rates

4.2.1 MNDA may order additional Security Officers for events or temporary requirements by written work order at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance, at the following rates, exclusive of VAT: Security Officer (male or female) AED 46.50 per hour; supervisor AED 58.00 per hour. Additional guarding is invoiced monthly with the signed timesheets.

4.2.2 All rates and prices are fixed for the Term and are not subject to adjustment for any reason other than a change in the VAT rate.

### 4.3 Invoicing and Payment Within Thirty Days

4.3.1 The Contractor shall invoice the Monthly Fee monthly in arrears by a valid tax invoice, accompanied by the Monthly Attendance Report under Clause 5.2 and the calculation of Service Credits, liquidated damages and abatement under Clause 6.

4.3.2 MNDA shall pay each valid tax invoice certified by the Contract Owner within thirty (30) days of certification. The Contract Owner shall certify or dispute an invoice within ten (10) Working Days of receipt. MNDA may set off any sum due from the Contractor.

### 4.4 Performance Security

The Contractor shall deliver within fourteen (14) days of signature an unconditional, irrevocable on-demand bank guarantee for AED 441,900, being five per cent (5%) of the Total Contract Value, issued by a bank licensed in the UAE and valid until 29 June 2028 (ninety (90) days after the Expiry Date). MNDA may call the Performance Security for any sum unpaid fourteen (14) days after written demand, or in full if it is not extended when required.

## Clause 5 — Performance Standards & KPIs

### 5.1 KPIs and Measurement

5.1.1 The Contractor shall meet the KPIs in Schedule 2, including Post Coverage of at least 99.5% in each month.

5.1.2 Attendance shall be recorded by biometric clock-in at each Site and by the electronic guard-tour system, which MNDA may access in real time. Manual attendance records are accepted only where the system has failed and the failure is reported to the Security Control Room at the time.

### 5.2 Monthly Attendance Report — Fifth Working Day

By the fifth (5th) Working Day of each month, the Contractor shall submit a Monthly Attendance Report for the preceding month, showing: rostered and actual Post-hours by Post and Site; Post Coverage; each Unmanned Post-Shift with times and reasons; late reliefs; overtime by Security Officer; licence and vetting expiry dates in the following ninety (90) days; patrol checkpoint compliance; incidents; and the calculation of Service Credits, liquidated damages and abatement.

### 5.3 Incident Reporting

Every security incident shall be reported verbally to the Security Control Room and the Site's duty manager within fifteen (15) minutes, and in writing to the Contract Owner within twenty-four (24) hours. Incidents involving injury, violence, theft of MNDA property or a breach of the Security Control Room shall also be reported immediately to the Contract Owner by telephone.

### 5.4 Review Meetings and Audit Access

5.4.1 A monthly operations meeting shall be held between the Contractor's contract manager, the Contract Owner's representative and the Customer Happiness Centre focal point. A quarterly contract review shall be chaired by the Contract Owner with a Procurement & Contracts representative.

5.4.2 MNDA, MNDA Internal Audit and auditors appointed by MNDA may inspect Posts unannounced, and may audit the Contractor's rosters, payroll, vetting files and records during the Term and for five (5) years after it.

## Clause 6 — Service Credits & Liquidated Damages

### 6.1 Abatement and Liquidated Damages for Unmanned Posts — AED 750 per Post-Shift

6.1.1 For each Unmanned Post-Shift, the Contractor shall pay MNDA liquidated damages of AED 750 (seven hundred and fifty UAE dirhams).

6.1.2 In addition, MNDA shall not pay for unmanned Post-hours, which shall be abated at the hourly rate in Clause 4.2.1.

6.1.3 The Parties agree that the amount in Clause 6.1.1 is a genuine pre-estimate of the loss and security risk to MNDA and is not a penalty.

### 6.2 Service Credits for Other KPIs and Aggregate Cap

6.2.1 For each other KPI not met, the Contractor shall credit MNDA the percentage of the Monthly Fee shown in Schedule 2.

6.2.2 Service Credits under Clause 6.2.1 and liquidated damages under Clause 6.1.1 shall not exceed in aggregate ten per cent (10%) of the Monthly Fee (AED 24,550) in any month. Abatement under Clause 6.1.2 is not subject to that cap.

6.2.3 If the cap in Clause 6.2.2 is reached in two (2) consecutive months, or Post Coverage falls below 98% in any month, that shall be a material breach entitling MNDA to terminate under Clause 9.2.

### 6.3 Liquidated Damages for Delay in Mobilisation

If the Contractor fails to deploy all Posts with vetted and licensed Security Officers by the Commencement Date, it shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the Contract Price (AED 14,730) for each week or part week of delay, up to ten per cent (10%) of the Contract Price (AED 294,600), and MNDA may procure interim guarding at the Contractor's cost.

## Clause 7 — Liability & Indemnity

### 7.1 Contractor Indemnity

The Contractor shall indemnify MNDA, its employees and visitors against all losses, claims, costs and expenses arising from: (a) death or personal injury, or loss of or damage to property, caused by the Contractor or its Security Officers; (b) any theft, dishonesty or unlawful use of force by Security Officers; (c) any breach of this Agreement or negligence of the Contractor; and (d) any claim by Security Officers, including for wages, end-of-service benefits, visas or labour law violations.

### 7.2 Limitation of the Contractor's Liability

7.2.1 The Contractor's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred per cent (100%) of the Total Contract Value (AED 8,838,000).

7.2.2 The cap does not apply to liability for: (a) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (b) wilful misconduct; (c) death or personal injury; (d) breach of Clause 10 or Clause 11; (e) infringement of intellectual property rights; or (f) any liability which cannot be limited under UAE law. Liquidated damages and Service Credits do not count towards the cap.

### 7.3 Limitation of MNDA's Liability

MNDA's total liability to the Contractor is limited to sums properly due and unpaid for Services performed in accordance with this Agreement. MNDA has no liability for loss of profit or indirect loss.

## Clause 8 — Insurance

### 8.1 Required Policies

The Contractor shall maintain with insurers licensed in the UAE throughout the Term: (a) public liability insurance of not less than AED 10,000,000 per occurrence, naming MNDA as additional insured; (b) workmen's compensation insurance as required by UAE labour law; (c) employer's liability insurance of not less than AED 5,000,000 per occurrence; (d) fidelity guarantee insurance of not less than AED 1,000,000 covering dishonesty of Security Officers; and (e) motor insurance for patrol vehicles.

### 8.2 Evidence and Renewal of Insurance

The Contractor shall deliver certificates before the Commencement Date and renewed certificates no later than the expiry of each policy. If it fails to do so, MNDA may withhold payment until compliance is evidenced or effect the insurance itself at the Contractor's cost. Insurance does not limit the Contractor's liability.

## Clause 9 — Termination

### 9.1 Termination for Convenience by MNDA

MNDA may terminate this Agreement, in whole or for one or more Sites, for convenience at any time on not less than thirty (30) days' written notice. MNDA shall pay the Monthly Fee pro rata to the termination date; no compensation for loss of profit is payable.

### 9.2 Termination for Cause

9.2.1 If the Contractor commits a breach, MNDA may require it to be remedied within fourteen (14) days of written notice and, if not so remedied, may terminate with immediate effect.

9.2.2 MNDA may terminate immediately on written notice for a material breach under Clause 6.2.3, abandonment of any Post for more than four (4) consecutive hours without notice, fraud, bribery, a serious security breach caused by the Contractor, the cancellation or suspension of the Contractor's licence to provide security services, or insolvency.

9.2.3 On termination for cause, MNDA may engage another contractor at the Contractor's cost to the extent exceeding the Contract Price, and call the Performance Security.

### 9.3 Exit and Handover

During the last thirty (30) days of the Term, or the notice period, the Contractor shall cooperate with any successor contractor, hand over keys, access cards, post orders, incident logs and equipment belonging to MNDA, and maintain full Post Coverage until the last day. The final Monthly Fee is payable on the Contract Owner's certification that handover is complete.

## Clause 10 — Confidentiality

**10.1 Obligations.** The Contractor shall keep confidential all information obtained in performing the Services, including building layouts, security procedures, access codes, CCTV footage, visitor information and the terms of this Agreement, and shall ensure every Security Officer signs a confidentiality undertaking before deployment.

**10.2 Survival.** These obligations survive expiry or termination for five (5) years; obligations concerning security procedures, access codes and personal data continue without limit of time.

## Clause 11 — Data Protection & Data Residency

**11.1 Personal Data.** CCTV footage, access-control records, visitor logs and identity details are MNDA Data and personal data. The Contractor shall process them only on MNDA systems, on MNDA's instructions and for the Services, in accordance with applicable UAE data protection legislation.

**11.2 Residency and Copies.** MNDA Data shall be stored and processed only in the UAE and only on MNDA-owned systems. Security Officers shall not copy, photograph or transmit footage or records by personal devices or messaging applications. Footage shall be released only on the Contract Owner's written authorisation or a competent authority's lawful request.

**11.3 Breach Notification.** The Contractor shall notify the Contract Owner in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorised disclosure of MNDA Data. No sub-processor may be used without MNDA's prior written approval.

## Clause 12 — Intellectual Property

All post orders, risk assessments, reports, logs and other documents prepared for MNDA under this Agreement, and all MNDA Data, are owned by MNDA upon creation, and the Contractor assigns to MNDA all rights in them. The Contractor retains ownership of its pre-existing training materials and grants MNDA a royalty-free licence to use any of them incorporated in the deliverables.

## Clause 13 — Subcontracting & Assignment

**13.1 Subcontracting.** The Contractor shall not subcontract any part of the Services, nor deploy personnel supplied by a labour-supply agency, without MNDA's prior written consent. All Security Officers shall be employed directly by the Contractor and sponsored under its trade licence.

**13.2 Assignment.** The Contractor shall not assign or novate this Agreement without MNDA's prior written consent and shall notify MNDA of any change of control within ten (10) Working Days.

## Clause 14 — Emiratisation & In-Country Value

### 14.1 UAE National Supervisors — Minimum Fifteen Per Cent

14.1.1 Not less than fifteen per cent (15%) of the supervisory positions under this Agreement (contract manager, shift supervisors, Customer Happiness Centre area supervisor and control-room supervisor), rounded up to the nearest whole person, shall be held by UAE nationals throughout the Term. With seven (7) supervisory positions at the Commencement Date, the minimum is two (2).

14.1.2 A vacancy in a position held by a UAE national shall be filled by a UAE national within sixty (60) days.

14.1.3 The Contractor shall report compliance quarterly in the Monthly Attendance Report and comply with all other Emiratisation obligations applicable to it under UAE law.

### 14.2 In-Country Value

The Contractor's ICV certificate score at tender was 42.8%. The Contractor shall maintain a valid ICV certificate with a score of not less than thirty-five per cent (35%) throughout the Term and provide each renewed certificate within ten (10) Working Days of issue.

## Clause 15 — Force Majeure

Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control that could not reasonably have been foreseen or avoided. Labour shortages, absenteeism, visa or licensing delays, extreme heat and strikes by the Contractor's personnel are not force majeure events. The Monthly Fee abates for Services not performed. If a force majeure event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either Party may terminate on thirty (30) days' notice.

## Clause 16 — Governing Law & Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates applicable in Marsa Al Noor. Disputes shall first be referred to the Contract Owner and the Contractor's general manager for amicable negotiation; if not resolved within thirty (30) days of a written notice of dispute, either Party may refer the dispute to the competent courts of the United Arab Emirates. The Contractor shall continue to perform pending resolution.

## Clause 17 — Notices

Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, or email with a signed PDF attached, to: **MNDA** — Director, Procurement & Contracts, MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, UAE, procurement.contracts@mnda.gov.ae, copy to tariq.saleh@mnda.gov.ae; **Contractor** — General Manager, Hisn Security Services LLC, Office 1103, Al Noor Business Centre, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, UAE, contracts@hisnsecurity.ae.
