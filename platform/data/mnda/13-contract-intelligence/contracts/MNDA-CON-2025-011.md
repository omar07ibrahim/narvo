THIS AGREEMENT is made in Marsa Al Noor on 16 January 2025 BETWEEN:

(1) **Marsa Al Noor Development Authority**, a government authority established under Law No. 4 of 2011, of MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, United Arab Emirates ("**MNDA**"); and

NOW IT IS AGREED as follows.

## Clause 1 — Definitions & Interpretation

### 1.1 Defined Terms

- "**Contract Owner**" means MNDA's Director, Digital Transformation Office, currently Hessa Abdulla Al Suwaidi.
- "**MNDA Service Manager**" means MNDA's IT Service Desk Lead, currently Yousef Ibrahim Al Hosani, who manages the Services day to day.
- "**Contract Price**" means AED 2,184,000 per Contract Year, exclusive of VAT; "**Monthly Fee**" means AED 182,000.
- "**Contract Year**" means each twelve-month period from 1 February.
- "**First-Contact Resolution**" or "**FCR**" means the percentage of Incidents and service requests received by telephone, chat or walk-in that are resolved by the Level 1 agent during the first contact without escalation or call-back.
- "**Incident**" means an unplanned interruption or reduction in quality of an IT service, classified P1 to P4 under Section S2.2 of Schedule 2.
- "**ITSM Tool**" means MNDA's IT service management platform.
- "**Key Personnel**" means the individuals named in Section S1.4 of Schedule 1.
- "**Users**" means MNDA employees (approximately 1,240) and contractors with MNDA accounts.
- "**Working Day**" means Monday to Friday, excluding UAE government-sector public holidays.

### 1.2 Interpretation and Order of Precedence

The Agreement comprises, in descending order of precedence: (a) these Clauses; (b) Schedule 2 (Service Levels and KPIs); (c) Schedule 1 (Services, Coverage and Staffing); (d) the Letter of Award; (e) the tender documents; and (f) the Contractor's proposal dated 30 October 2024 as clarified. "Including" means "including without limitation". The Arabic text of this Agreement prevails before UAE courts.

## Clause 2 — Scope of Service Desk & End-User Support Services

### 2.1 Level 1 Service Desk

2.1.1 The Contractor shall operate a bilingual (Arabic and English) Level 1 service desk for all Users through telephone (internal extension 7400), self-service portal, email, chat and a walk-in Tech Bar at MNDA Tower, during the hours in Section S1.2 of Schedule 1.

2.1.2 Level 1 covers: logging, categorising and prioritising every contact in the ITSM Tool; password and multi-factor authentication resets; account unlocks; remote-access support under MNDA policy HC-POL-07; standard software requests; first-line troubleshooting of endpoints, printers and collaboration tools; and escalation to Level 2 or MNDA's Level 3 teams.

2.1.3 The Contractor shall maintain the knowledge base in the ITSM Tool and publish at least eight (8) new or revised articles per month based on recurring contacts.

### 2.2 Level 2 Desk-side and Field Support

2.2.1 The Contractor shall provide desk-side support at MNDA Tower and the three Customer Happiness Centres (Downtown, Al Murjan and Al Waha), including hardware and software troubleshooting, peripheral replacement, meeting-room audio-visual support for thirty-eight (38) rooms and first-line support for sixty-four (64) multifunction printers.

2.2.2 The Contractor shall provide priority support to the Office of the Director General and to Users designated as VIP by the Contract Owner, including on-call support outside service hours.

2.2.3 Customer Happiness Centre counters are business-critical: any fault preventing a counter from serving customers shall be treated as at least P2.

### 2.3 Endpoint Lifecycle, Onboarding and Asset Records

2.3.1 The Contractor shall perform installation, move, addition and change (IMAC) activities for approximately 1,318 end-user devices, including imaging with MNDA's standard build, deployment, laptop replacement and secure recovery of returned devices.

2.3.2 New joiners shall have an account, device and standard access ready by 08:00 on their first working day, provided the request is received complete at least three (3) Working Days in advance. Leavers' access shall be revoked on the day notified by Human Capital.

2.3.3 The Contractor shall keep the end-user asset records in the ITSM Tool accurate and complete, and shall reconcile them with a physical count every six (6) months.

## Clause 3 — Term & Renewal

### 3.1 Initial Term

This Agreement takes effect on signature. The Services shall be provided for an initial term from 1 February 2025 (the "**Commencement Date**") to 31 January 2027 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Clause 9.

### 3.2 Renewal Option — One Optional Twelve-Month Renewal

3.2.1 MNDA may, at its sole discretion, renew this Agreement once for a renewal term of twelve (12) months, from 1 February 2027 to 31 January 2028 (the "**Renewal Term**").

3.2.2 The Renewal Term shall be on the same terms and at the same rates as the Initial Term, subject to a satisfactory performance review by the Contract Owner covering KPI results, Service Credits applied, User satisfaction and Key Personnel stability.

3.2.3 The Contractor has no right to require renewal, and no renewal arises by conduct or continued performance.

### 3.3 Renewal Notice — Ninety Days Before Expiry

3.3.1 MNDA shall notify the Contractor in writing of its decision to renew or not to renew not less than ninety (90) days before the expiry of the Initial Term, that is, no later than 2 November 2026.

3.3.2 The notice shall be signed by MNDA's Director, Procurement & Contracts, and served under Clause 17. If no notice of renewal is served in time, the renewal option lapses and this Agreement expires on 31 January 2027.

3.3.3 Before the Renewal Term commences, the Contractor shall extend the Performance Security and its insurances.

## Clause 4 — Contract Price & Payment

### 4.1 Contract Price and Breakdown

4.1.1 MNDA shall pay the Contract Price of AED 2,184,000 per Contract Year, exclusive of VAT, in twelve (12) monthly instalments of AED 182,000. The Total Contract Value for the Initial Term is AED 4,368,000.

| Service component | Monthly (AED) |
|---|---|
| Level 1 service desk and Tech Bar | 71,400 |
| Level 2 desk-side and field support | 84,700 |
| Service management, reporting and knowledge management | 25,900 |
| **Total Monthly Fee** | **182,000** |

4.1.2 The Contract Price includes all personnel, tools, travel between Sites, training, insurances, overheads and profit. All prices and rates are fixed for the Term.

### 4.2 Rate Card for Additional Resources

MNDA may order additional resources by written work order at the following fixed rates, exclusive of VAT: Level 1 analyst AED 16,850 per month; desk-side engineer AED 21,400 per month; after-hours call-out (non-VIP) AED 385 per call-out, including the first two (2) hours.

### 4.3 Invoicing and Payment Within Thirty Days

4.3.1 The Contractor shall invoice the Monthly Fee monthly in arrears by a valid tax invoice, accompanied by the Monthly Service Report and the Service Credit calculation.

4.3.2 MNDA shall pay each valid tax invoice certified by the Contract Owner within thirty (30) days of certification. MNDA may deduct Service Credits and set off any other sum due from the Contractor. No interest accrues on late payment.

### 4.4 Performance Security

The Contractor shall deliver within fourteen (14) days of signature an unconditional, irrevocable on-demand bank guarantee for AED 218,400, being five per cent (5%) of the Total Contract Value, issued by a bank licensed in the UAE and valid until 1 May 2027 (ninety (90) days after the end of the Initial Term), to be extended for any Renewal Term.

## Clause 5 — Performance Standards & KPIs

### 5.1 Service Levels and Measurement

5.1.1 The Contractor shall meet the service levels and KPIs in Schedule 2, including First-Contact Resolution of at least 70%, restoration of P1 Incidents within four (4) hours and User satisfaction of at least 4.2 out of 5.

5.1.2 All KPIs are measured from the ITSM Tool and MNDA's telephony system. Tickets shall not be closed without User confirmation or a documented three-attempt contact procedure, and shall not be re-categorised to avoid a KPI breach.

### 5.2 Monthly Service Report — Seventh Working Day

By the seventh (7th) Working Day of each month, the Contractor shall submit a Monthly Service Report to the MNDA Service Manager containing: contact volumes by channel; KPI results against Schedule 2; P1 and P2 Incidents with root causes; top ten recurring issues and problem-management actions; knowledge articles published; onboarding and leaver performance; asset-record changes; User satisfaction scores and verbatim comments; staffing and Key Personnel status; and the Service Credit calculation.

### 5.3 Quarterly Service Review

A quarterly service review shall be chaired by the Contract Owner, attended by the MNDA Service Manager, a Procurement & Contracts representative and the Contractor's account director, to review KPI trends, User satisfaction, continual-improvement initiatives, risks and, in the final quarter of the Initial Term, the performance review for renewal under Clause 3.2.2. The MNDA Service Manager and the Contractor's service delivery manager shall also meet weekly.

### 5.4 Audit Access

MNDA, MNDA Internal Audit and auditors appointed by MNDA may audit the Contractor's records, ticket handling, timesheets and security compliance on five (5) Working Days' notice during the Term and for five (5) years after it.

## Clause 6 — Service Credits & Liquidated Damages

### 6.1 Service Credits

6.1.1 For each KPI in Section S2.1 of Schedule 2 that is not met in a month, the Contractor shall credit MNDA the percentage of the Monthly Fee shown against it.

6.1.2 Service Credits in any month shall not exceed ten per cent (10%) of the Monthly Fee (AED 18,200). They are a price adjustment and without prejudice to MNDA's other rights.

6.1.3 If the same KPI is missed in three (3) consecutive months, the Contractor shall submit a remediation plan within ten (10) Working Days. If FCR or P1 restoration is missed in four (4) consecutive months, that shall be a material breach for the purposes of Clause 9.2.

### 6.2 Liquidated Damages for Delay in Transition-In

The Contractor shall complete transition-in (knowledge transfer from the outgoing provider, staffing to Section S1.3, ITSM Tool access and acceptance of the service-readiness checklist) by the Commencement Date. For each week or part week of delay, the Contractor shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the Contract Price (AED 10,920), up to ten per cent (10%) of the Contract Price (AED 218,400).

## Clause 7 — Liability & Indemnity

### 7.1 Contractor Indemnity

The Contractor shall indemnify MNDA against all losses, claims, costs and expenses arising from: (a) death, personal injury or property damage caused by the Contractor or its personnel; (b) loss, corruption or unauthorised disclosure of MNDA Data caused by the Contractor; (c) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights by tools or materials supplied by the Contractor; and (d) any breach of this Agreement or negligence of the Contractor or its personnel.

### 7.2 Limitation of Liability

7.2.1 The Contractor's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred per cent (100%) of the Total Contract Value (AED 4,368,000), increased by the Contract Price for any Renewal Term.

7.2.2 The cap does not apply to liability for: (a) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (b) wilful misconduct; (c) death or personal injury; (d) breach of Clause 10 or Clause 11; (e) infringement of intellectual property rights; or (f) any liability which cannot be limited under UAE law.

7.2.3 MNDA's total liability to the Contractor is limited to sums properly due and unpaid. Neither Party is liable for loss of profit or indirect loss, save that the cost of restoring MNDA Data is direct loss.

## Clause 8 — Insurance

The Contractor shall maintain with insurers licensed in the UAE throughout the Term: (a) professional indemnity insurance of not less than AED 5,000,000 per claim; (b) public liability insurance of not less than AED 10,000,000 per occurrence; (c) workmen's compensation insurance as required by UAE law; and (d) employer's liability insurance of not less than AED 2,000,000. The Contractor shall deliver certificates before the Commencement Date and renewed certificates no later than the expiry of each policy.

## Clause 9 — Termination

### 9.1 Termination for Convenience by MNDA

MNDA may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on not less than thirty (30) days' written notice, paying the Monthly Fee pro rata to the termination date. No compensation for loss of profit is payable.

### 9.2 Termination for Cause

9.2.1 If the Contractor commits a breach, MNDA may require it to be remedied within fourteen (14) days of written notice and, if it is not remedied, terminate with immediate effect.

9.2.2 MNDA may terminate immediately on written notice for a material breach under Clause 6.1.3, a serious security breach caused by the Contractor, fraud, bribery or insolvency.

### 9.3 Exit and Transition-Out

For up to sixty (60) days before expiry or after a notice of termination, the Contractor shall deliver an approved exit plan, provide knowledge transfer to MNDA or a successor provider (including the knowledge base, open tickets, runbooks and asset records), return all MNDA equipment and credentials, and maintain all service levels until the last day.

## Clause 10 — Confidentiality

**10.1 Obligations.** The Contractor shall keep confidential all MNDA information to which it has access, including User data, system configurations, credentials, security information and the terms of this Agreement, and shall ensure each of its personnel signs MNDA's confidentiality and acceptable-use undertaking before receiving system access.

**10.2 Survival.** These obligations survive expiry or termination for five (5) years; obligations concerning personal data, credentials and security information continue without limit of time.

## Clause 11 — Data Protection & Data Residency

### 11.1 Data Residency and Access from the UAE Only

11.1.1 MNDA Data, including the personal data of Users and of customers stored on endpoints, shall be stored, processed and accessed only within the United Arab Emirates. The Contractor shall deliver the Services only from MNDA premises or, for on-call support, from MNDA-managed devices located in the UAE. No offshore or remote delivery centre may be used.

11.1.2 Remote-control tools may be used only if approved and provided by MNDA, with User consent recorded in the ticket.

### 11.2 Breach Notification and Sub-processors

11.2.1 The Contractor shall notify the Contract Owner and MNDA's information security function within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of any actual or suspected security incident involving MNDA Data, credentials or devices, including loss of a device in its custody.

11.2.2 The Contractor shall not engage any sub-processor or subcontractor with access to MNDA Data without MNDA's prior written approval, and shall process personal data only on MNDA's instructions in accordance with applicable UAE data protection legislation.

## Clause 12 — Intellectual Property

All knowledge articles, runbooks, reports, scripts and other materials created for MNDA under this Agreement, and all MNDA Data, are owned by MNDA on creation, and the Contractor assigns to MNDA all rights in them. The Contractor's pre-existing tools and methodologies remain its property, and it grants MNDA a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any of them incorporated in the deliverables.

## Clause 13 — Subcontracting, Key Personnel & Assignment

### 13.1 Key Personnel — Replacement Requires MNDA Approval

13.1.1 The Contractor shall assign the Key Personnel named in Section S1.4 of Schedule 1 for the Term and shall not remove or replace any of them without the Contract Owner's prior written approval, save in the case of resignation, serious illness or death.

13.1.2 Any proposed replacement shall have equal or better qualifications and experience, shall be presented to MNDA for interview at least thirty (30) days before the change, and shall work alongside the outgoing person for at least ten (10) Working Days, at no cost to MNDA.

13.1.3 MNDA may require the replacement of any member of the Contractor's personnel for reasonable cause.

### 13.2 Subcontracting and Assignment

The Contractor shall not subcontract any part of the Services, or assign or novate this Agreement, without MNDA's prior written consent, and shall notify MNDA of any change of control within ten (10) Working Days. Consent does not relieve the Contractor of any obligation.

## Clause 14 — Emiratisation & In-Country Value

**14.1 Emiratisation.** The Contractor shall comply with the Emiratisation obligations applicable to it and shall deploy at least one (1) UAE national among the personnel in Section S1.3 of Schedule 1 from the Commencement Date, and at least two (2) from 1 February 2026, reporting compliance in the Monthly Service Report.

**14.2 In-Country Value.** The Contractor's ICV certificate score at tender was 27.3%. The Contractor shall maintain a valid ICV certificate with a score of not less than twenty-five per cent (25%) throughout the Term.

## Clause 15 — Force Majeure

Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control that could not reasonably have been foreseen or avoided. Staff absence, resignations, visa delays and failures of the Contractor's own systems are not force majeure events. The Monthly Fee abates for Services not performed, and either Party may terminate on thirty (30) days' notice if the event continues for more than sixty (60) days.

## Clause 16 — Governing Law & Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates applicable in Marsa Al Noor. Disputes shall first be referred to the Contract Owner and the Contractor's general manager for amicable negotiation; if not resolved within thirty (30) days of a written notice of dispute, either Party may refer the dispute to the competent courts of the United Arab Emirates. The Contractor shall continue to perform pending resolution.

## Clause 17 — Notices

Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, or email with a signed PDF attached, to: **MNDA** — Director, Procurement & Contracts, MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, UAE, procurement.contracts@mnda.gov.ae, copy to hessa.alsuwaidi@mnda.gov.ae and yousef.alhosani@mnda.gov.ae; **Contractor** — General Manager, Thabit IT Services LLC, Office 712, Block B, Al Quoz Industrial 1, Dubai, UAE, contracts@thabit-it.ae.
