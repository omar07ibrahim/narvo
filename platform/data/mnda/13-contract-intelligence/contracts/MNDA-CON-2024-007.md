## Parties and Recitals

THIS AGREEMENT is made in Marsa Al Noor on 22 February 2024 BETWEEN:

(1) **Marsa Al Noor Development Authority**, a government authority established under Law No. 4 of 2011, of MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, United Arab Emirates ("**MNDA**"); and

(2) **Mizan Asset Systems FZ-LLC**, a free zone limited liability company holding Licence No. CN-3051794, of Office 1416, Building 3, Technology Park Free Zone, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (the "**Supplier**").

WHEREAS MNDA wishes to implement a computer-aided facilities management platform for its headquarters, Customer Happiness Centres and community facilities, and the Supplier was awarded the contract under Letter of Award No. MNDA/PRC/LOA/129/2023 following Tender No. MNDA/PRC/2023/129.

NOW IT IS AGREED as follows.

## Clause 1 — Definitions & Interpretation

### 1.1 Defined Terms

- "**Annual Fees**" means AED 386,500 per Contract Year, exclusive of VAT, comprising the subscription, hosting and support fees in Clause 4.1.
- "**Availability**" has the meaning in Section S2.1 of Schedule 2.
- "**Contract Owner**" means MNDA's Director, Facilities & Assets, currently Eng. Tariq Mahmoud Saleh.
- "**Contract Year**" means each twelve-month period from 1 March.
- "**Go-Live**" means acceptance by MNDA of a Phase under Clause 2.4.
- "**Incident**" means any unplanned interruption or degradation of the Platform, classified by priority in Section S2.2 of Schedule 2.
- "**MNDA Data**" means all data entered into, generated by or stored in the Platform by or for MNDA, including asset, work order, user and personal data.
- "**Monthly Fee**" means one-twelfth of the Annual Fees (AED 32,208.33).
- "**Platform**" means the Supplier's "Mizan FM" software (version 8.3 and later releases), with the modules in Schedule 1, hosted and supported under this Agreement.
- "**Working Day**" means Monday to Friday, excluding UAE government-sector public holidays.

### 1.2 Interpretation and Order of Precedence

1.2.1 The Agreement comprises, in descending order of precedence: (a) these Clauses; (b) Schedule 2 (Service Levels); (c) Schedule 1 (Modules, Users and Hosting); (d) the Letter of Award; (e) the tender documents; and (f) the Supplier's proposal dated 14 December 2023 as clarified.

1.2.2 No shrink-wrap, click-through or online terms of the Supplier or of any third party shall apply to MNDA, even if accepted by an MNDA user in the course of using the Platform.

## Clause 2 — Scope of Licence, Hosting and Support Services

### 2.1 Licence Grant

2.1.1 The Supplier grants MNDA a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence for the Term to access and use the Platform for MNDA's internal purposes, for the numbers of users set out in Schedule 1.

2.1.2 MNDA may permit its facilities management contractors and their personnel to use the Platform on MNDA's behalf within the licensed user numbers. MNDA is responsible for their compliance with this licence.

2.1.3 MNDA may reallocate named-user licences between individuals at any time without charge.

### 2.2 Hosting Services

The Supplier shall host the Platform in a dedicated single-tenant environment in a Tier III-certified data centre in the Abu Dhabi region, with a disaster-recovery environment in a second data centre in the UAE (Schedule 1, Section S1.3). The Supplier shall provide daily backups retained for thirty-five (35) days and monthly backups retained for twelve (12) months, a recovery point objective of one (1) hour and a recovery time objective of four (4) hours, and shall test disaster recovery at least once in each Contract Year in the presence of MNDA's Digital Transformation Office.

### 2.3 Support and Maintenance Services

2.3.1 The Supplier shall provide: (a) a support desk available 24/7 for P1 Incidents and from 08:00 to 18:00 on Working Days for other Incidents; (b) Incident resolution in accordance with Schedule 2; (c) corrective patches, security updates and all new releases of the Platform at no additional charge; and (d) a named customer success manager.

2.3.2 Security patches rated "critical" shall be applied within seventy-two (72) hours of release, and "high" within fourteen (14) days.

2.3.3 The Supplier shall maintain the interfaces listed in Schedule 1, including after any Platform upgrade, at no additional charge.

### 2.4 Implementation Services and Go-Live

2.4.1 The Supplier shall implement the Platform in two Phases: Phase 1 (MNDA Tower and the three Customer Happiness Centres) with Go-Live by 1 June 2024; and Phase 2 (community facilities, including migration of historical records from the incumbent FM contractor's system) with Go-Live by 1 September 2024.

2.4.2 Implementation includes configuration, data migration, interface build, user acceptance testing, training of up to sixty (60) users and four (4) weeks of hypercare after each Go-Live.

2.4.3 Go-Live of a Phase occurs only on MNDA's written acceptance following successful user acceptance testing with no open P1 or P2 defects.

## Clause 3 — Term & Renewal

### 3.1 Initial Term

This Agreement takes effect on signature. The Services shall be provided for an initial term from 1 March 2024 to 28 February 2027 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Clause 9.

### 3.2 Automatic Renewal — Ninety Days' Notice of Non-Renewal

3.2.1 On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "**Renewal Period**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Period.

3.2.2 For the Initial Term, notice of non-renewal must therefore be served no later than 30 November 2026. If no such notice is served, the Agreement renews for the period 1 March 2027 to 29 February 2028.

3.2.3 Any Renewal Period shall be at the Annual Fees then applicable, without increase, unless otherwise agreed in a written variation signed by both Parties.

3.2.4 Automatic renewal does not affect MNDA's right to terminate for convenience under Clause 9.1 at any time, including during any Renewal Period.

## Clause 4 — Contract Price & Payment

### 4.1 Annual Fees and Implementation Fee

4.1.1 MNDA shall pay the following, exclusive of VAT:

| Component | Amount (AED) |
|---|---|
| Platform subscription licence (per Contract Year) | 241,800 |
| Hosting services (per Contract Year) | 88,200 |
| Support and maintenance (per Contract Year) | 56,500 |
| **Annual Fees** | **386,500** |
| Implementation Fee (one-time) | 212,400 |

4.1.2 The Total Contract Value for the Initial Term is AED 1,371,900. The Annual Fees and all rates are fixed for the Initial Term.

### 4.2 Invoicing and Payment Terms

4.2.1 The Annual Fees shall be invoiced quarterly in advance in four (4) equal instalments of AED 96,625. The Implementation Fee shall be invoiced 30% on MNDA's approval of the solution design, 50% on Phase 2 Go-Live and 20% sixty (60) days after Phase 2 Go-Live.

4.2.2 MNDA shall pay each valid tax invoice certified by the Contract Owner within thirty (30) days of certification. Service Credits shall be deducted from the next quarterly invoice.

4.2.3 MNDA may set off any sum owed by the Supplier against any sum due to it. No interest accrues on late payment.

### 4.3 Performance Security

The Supplier shall deliver within fourteen (14) days of signature an unconditional on-demand bank guarantee for AED 68,595, being five per cent (5%) of the Total Contract Value, issued by a bank licensed in the UAE, valid until 29 May 2027 (ninety (90) days after the end of the Initial Term) and extended for each Renewal Period. MNDA may call it for any sum unpaid fourteen (14) days after written demand.

## Clause 5 — Performance Standards & Service Levels

### 5.1 Service Levels and Reporting

5.1.1 The Supplier shall meet the service levels in Schedule 2, including Platform Availability of at least 99.5% in each calendar month and restoration of service for P1 Incidents within four (4) hours.

5.1.2 By the fifth (5th) Working Day of each month, the Supplier shall submit a service report showing Availability, Incidents by priority with response and restoration times, security patches applied, backup and restore test results, capacity trends and Service Credits due.

5.1.3 A quarterly service review shall be chaired by the Contract Owner with the Digital Transformation Office.

### 5.2 Audit and Security Testing

5.2.1 MNDA, MNDA Internal Audit and auditors appointed by MNDA may audit the Supplier's performance, invoicing and security controls on ten (10) Working Days' notice during the Term and for five (5) years after it.

5.2.2 The Supplier shall commission an independent penetration test of the Platform at least annually, share the executive summary with MNDA, and remediate critical and high findings within thirty (30) days. MNDA may conduct its own security testing on agreed dates.

## Clause 6 — Service Credits & Liquidated Damages

### 6.1 Service Credits

6.1.1 If a service level in Schedule 2 is not met, the Supplier shall credit MNDA the percentage of the Monthly Fee specified in Section S2.3 of Schedule 2.

6.1.2 Service Credits in any month shall not exceed ten per cent (10%) of the Monthly Fee. Service Credits are a price adjustment and are without prejudice to MNDA's other rights.

6.1.3 If Availability falls below 98.0% in any month, or below 99.5% in three (3) months within any rolling six (6) months, that shall constitute a material breach entitling MNDA to terminate under Clause 9.2 without further cure period.

### 6.2 Liquidated Damages for Delay in Go-Live

If Go-Live of a Phase is not achieved by the date in Clause 2.4.1 for reasons attributable to the Supplier, the Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the Implementation Fee for each week or part week of delay, up to a maximum of ten per cent (10%) of the Implementation Fee, without prejudice to MNDA's right to terminate if the delay exceeds twelve (12) weeks.

## Clause 7 — Liability & Indemnity

### 7.1 Supplier Indemnity

The Supplier shall indemnify MNDA against all losses, claims, costs and expenses arising from: (a) any claim that the Platform or its use in accordance with this Agreement infringes third-party intellectual property rights; (b) any breach of Clause 10 or Clause 11; (c) death, personal injury or property damage caused by the Supplier or its personnel; and (d) any breach of this Agreement or negligent act or omission of the Supplier, its personnel or subcontractors.

### 7.2 Limitation of the Supplier's Liability — Fees Paid in the Preceding Twelve Months

7.2.1 Subject to Clause 7.3, the Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed an amount equal to one hundred per cent (100%) of the fees paid or payable by MNDA under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7.2.2 Service Credits and liquidated damages do not count towards the cap in Clause 7.2.1.

7.2.3 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit, save that costs of restoring or re-creating MNDA Data, of procuring replacement services and of regulatory notification shall be recoverable as direct loss.

### 7.3 Exclusions from the Liability Cap

The limitation in Clause 7.2.1 does not apply to the Supplier's liability for: (a) fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation; (b) wilful misconduct, including wilful suspension of the Platform; (c) death or personal injury; (d) breach of Clause 10 (Confidentiality) or Clause 11 (Data Protection & Data Residency); (e) the indemnity for intellectual property infringement under Clause 7.1(a); or (f) any liability which cannot be limited under UAE law.

### 7.4 Limitation of MNDA's Liability

MNDA's total liability to the Supplier is limited to fees properly due and unpaid under this Agreement. MNDA has no liability for the Supplier's loss of profit, revenue or opportunity.

## Clause 8 — Insurance

The Supplier shall maintain, with reputable insurers, for the Term and for two (2) years after it: (a) professional indemnity insurance of not less than AED 5,000,000 per claim; (b) public liability insurance of not less than AED 10,000,000 per occurrence; (c) cyber and data liability insurance of not less than AED 3,500,000 per claim; and (d) workmen's compensation insurance as required by UAE law. The Supplier shall deliver certificates before signature and renewed certificates no later than the expiry of each policy.

## Clause 9 — Termination

### 9.1 Termination for Convenience by MNDA — Thirty Days

9.1.1 MNDA may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time, during the Initial Term or any Renewal Period, by giving the Supplier not less than thirty (30) days' written notice.

9.1.2 On termination under Clause 9.1.1, the Supplier shall refund the portion of any Annual Fees paid in advance relating to the period after the termination date, within thirty (30) days. No other compensation, early-termination fee or loss of profit is payable.

### 9.2 Termination for Cause and Insolvency

9.2.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within fourteen (14) days of written notice specifying the breach.

9.2.2 MNDA may terminate with immediate effect if the Supplier becomes insolvent, ceases to support the Platform, commits fraud or bribery, or undergoes a change of control to which MNDA has not consented.

9.2.3 The Supplier shall not suspend the Platform for any reason, including a payment dispute, other than in accordance with a court order.

### 9.3 Exit Assistance and Data Return

9.3.1 On expiry or termination for any reason, the Supplier shall provide, for up to ninety (90) days at no additional charge: (a) continued read-only access to the Platform; (b) a complete export of MNDA Data, including attachments and audit trails, in CSV and a documented database format; and (c) reasonable assistance to MNDA and any successor supplier with migration.

9.3.2 After MNDA confirms successful migration, the Supplier shall securely delete MNDA Data from all environments and backups and certify deletion in writing within thirty (30) days.

## Clause 10 — Confidentiality

**10.1 Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential the other's confidential information, including MNDA Data, system configurations, security information and the terms of this Agreement, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to personnel and approved subcontractors bound by equivalent obligations or as required by law.

**10.2 Survival.** These obligations survive expiry or termination for five (5) years; obligations relating to personal data and security information continue without limit of time.

## Clause 11 — Data Protection & Data Residency

### 11.1 Data Residency — Abu Dhabi Region Data Centre

11.1.1 All MNDA Data, including backups, logs and disaster-recovery copies, shall be stored and processed only in the UAE, in the data centres identified in Section S1.3 of Schedule 1.

11.1.2 MNDA Data shall not be accessed from outside the UAE. Exceptionally, named Supplier engineers may access the production environment from outside the UAE only with MNDA's prior written approval for each occasion, through an MNDA-controlled privileged-access session that is recorded.

11.1.3 The Supplier shall process personal data only on MNDA's documented instructions and in accordance with applicable UAE data protection legislation and MNDA's information security policies.

### 11.2 Breach Notification and Sub-processors

11.2.1 The Supplier shall notify MNDA's Digital Transformation Office and the Contract Owner within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of any actual or suspected security incident affecting MNDA Data, and shall provide a written root-cause report within five (5) Working Days.

11.2.2 The Supplier shall not engage any sub-processor without MNDA's prior written approval. The approved sub-processors at signature are listed in Section S1.3 of Schedule 1. The Supplier remains fully liable for its sub-processors.

## Clause 12 — Intellectual Property

**12.1 Supplier IP.** The Platform and all intellectual property rights in it remain the property of the Supplier or its licensors.

**12.2 MNDA Data and Deliverables.** MNDA owns all MNDA Data and all MNDA-specific configurations, reports, dashboards, workflows, interface specifications and training materials produced under this Agreement ("**Deliverables**"), and the Supplier assigns to MNDA all rights in the Deliverables. The Supplier shall not use MNDA Data for any purpose other than performing this Agreement, including product development or benchmarking.

**12.3 Escrow.** The Supplier shall deposit the Platform source code with an escrow agent approved by MNDA, updated with each major release, to be released to MNDA if the Supplier becomes insolvent or ceases to support the Platform.

## Clause 13 — Subcontracting & Assignment

**13.1 Subcontracting.** The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the services without MNDA's prior written consent. Consent does not relieve the Supplier of any obligation, and the Supplier is responsible for its subcontractors as for itself.

**13.2 Assignment.** The Supplier shall not assign or novate this Agreement without MNDA's prior written consent and shall notify MNDA within ten (10) Working Days of any change of control. MNDA may novate this Agreement to any government entity succeeding to its functions.

## Clause 14 — Emiratisation & In-Country Value

The Supplier shall comply with the Emiratisation requirements applicable to it, maintain a valid ICV certificate throughout the Term (score at tender: 19.4%), and provide each renewed certificate within ten (10) Working Days of issue. The Supplier shall deliver implementation and first-line support from personnel based in the UAE.

## Clause 15 — Force Majeure

Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by an event beyond its reasonable control that could not reasonably have been foreseen or avoided. Failures of the Supplier's hosting providers, subcontractors or utilities, and cyber-attacks that appropriate security measures would have prevented, are not force majeure events. The disaster-recovery obligations in Clause 2.2 continue to apply. If a force majeure event continues for more than thirty (30) days, MNDA may terminate on written notice.

## Clause 16 — Governing Law & Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates applicable in Marsa Al Noor. Any dispute shall first be referred to the Contract Owner and the Supplier's managing director for amicable negotiation. If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days of a written notice of dispute, either Party may refer it to the competent courts of the United Arab Emirates, which shall have exclusive jurisdiction. The Supplier shall continue to perform pending resolution.

## Clause 17 — Notices

Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, or email with a signed PDF attached, to: **MNDA** — Director, Procurement & Contracts, MNDA Tower, Corniche Road, MN-01, Marsa Al Noor, UAE, procurement.contracts@mnda.gov.ae, copy to tariq.saleh@mnda.gov.ae; **Supplier** — Managing Director, Mizan Asset Systems FZ-LLC, Office 1416, Building 3, Technology Park Free Zone, Dubai, UAE, legal@mizanassets.ae. A notice received after 14:00 on a Working Day (12:00 on a Friday), or on a non-Working Day, is deemed received at 09:00 on the next Working Day.

## Schedule 2 — Service Levels and Service Credits

### S2.1 Availability

Availability = (total minutes in the month − unplanned downtime minutes) ÷ total minutes in the month, measured by the Supplier's external monitoring and verifiable by MNDA. Scheduled maintenance is excluded only if notified five (5) Working Days in advance and performed between 22:00 Saturday and 04:00 Sunday, up to eight (8) hours per month. Target: **99.5% per calendar month**.

### S2.2 Incident Priorities

| Priority | Definition | Response | Restore |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Platform unavailable, or a critical function (work orders, helpdesk, mobile app) unusable for all users | 30 minutes, 24/7 | 4 hours |
| P2 | Critical function degraded or unavailable for a group of users | 2 hours | 1 Working Day |
| P3 | Non-critical function impaired; workaround available | 1 Working Day | 5 Working Days |
| P4 | Cosmetic issue or enhancement request | 3 Working Days | Next release |

### S2.3 Service Credits

| Failure | Service Credit (% of Monthly Fee) |
|---|---|
| Availability 99.0% to below 99.5% | 5% |
| Availability below 99.0% | 10% |
| Each P1 Incident not restored within 4 hours | 2% |
| Each P2 Incident not restored within 1 Working Day | 1% |
| Monthly service report later than 5th Working Day | 0.5% |

Aggregate cap: 10% of the Monthly Fee per month (Clause 6.1.2).
