# Raseen ThermaShield heat-reflective roof coating (PNT-TS-20)

Launch date: **1 November 2026**. Manufactured for RBM by Coastal Coatings Industries LLC.

## Description

Water-based acrylic, heat-reflective coating for concrete roofs, metal roofs and bitumen membranes. Bright white
finish that reflects solar energy and lowers roof-surface temperature.

## Performance (laboratory report RBM-LAB-26-112)

| Property | Value | Method / basis |
|---|---|---|
| Solar reflectance | **0.88** | ASTM C1549 |
| Solar Reflectance Index (SRI) | **110** | ASTM E1980 |
| Roof surface temperature | **up to 18 °C cooler** than uncoated grey concrete | Lab test at 46 °C ambient |

## Application

- 2 coats; coverage **1.2–1.5 m² per litre per coat** depending on substrate porosity.
- Surface clean, dry and sound; prime porous concrete per Raseen application guide.
- Do not apply below 10 °C or above 45 °C surface temperature, or when rain is expected within 6 hours.

## Warranty

**10-year warranty when applied by a Raseen-certified applicator** and registered within 30 days of completion.

## Packaging and price

20 L pail. Trade price **AED 385 per pail, excluding VAT** (PL-2026-Q4).

## Not claimed

ThermaShield does not replace air conditioning, is not a waterproofing membrane on its own and holds no government
certification.
