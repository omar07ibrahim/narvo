# Refrigerant Handling & Record-Keeping

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reference | RFM-HSE-PRO-011 Rev 2, 30 June 2025 (supersedes Rev 1, February 2022) |
| Owner | Raseen FM — HSE & Environment |
| Applies to | All work on refrigerant circuits — chillers, packaged units, splits and VRF — by Raseen FM staff and subcontractors |
| Linked documents | RFM-HSE-PRO-007 (LOTO) · ALD-ACX350-SM-07 §7.7 · form RF-01 (refrigerant log) |

Rev 2 changes: high-head-pressure charging rule added (section 6); van cylinder limits tightened (section 7).

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure makes sure refrigerants are handled safely, never released to atmosphere, and fully accounted for. It
applies to every refrigerant in use on Raseen FM sites, mainly R-134a (screw chillers such as the Aldara ACX-350) and
R-410A (scroll chillers, splits and VRF).

## 2. Certification — Raseen refrigerant handling certificate

- Only holders of a **valid Raseen internal refrigerant handling certificate** may connect gauges, recover, charge,
  leak-test or braze on a refrigerant circuit. Certificate numbers follow the technician ID, for example
  **RFM-RH-0417** (Arun Pillai, Senior HVAC Technician).
- Certificates are issued after a 2-day course and a practical assessment by a Raseen refrigerant handling assessor
  (for HVAC: Mohammed Rafiq). They are valid for 3 years and listed in the RFM Connect competency register.
- Helpers and trainees may assist under direct supervision but may not operate valves or the recovery unit.

## 3. No venting

- **Deliberate release of any refrigerant to atmosphere is prohibited** — including purging non-condensables,
  "blowing through" lines with refrigerant and venting charging hoses.
- Use low-loss hose fittings and recover hose contents after charging.
- Any accidental release above 1 kg is reported to HSE & Environment within 24 hours and recorded as an environmental
  incident.

## 4. Recovery

- Recover the refrigerant **before opening any circuit**, using the recovery unit and 22 kg recovery cylinder set
  **RFM-TL-0480**.
- Recover until the circuit holds at −30 kPa(g) or lower for 5 minutes.
- **Fill a recovery cylinder to no more than 80% of the maximum fill weight** marked on it; weigh it on a scale
  throughout.
- **One refrigerant type per recovery cylinder — never mix.** Label each cylinder with refrigerant type, asset tag,
  date and net kilograms.
- Clean refrigerant recovered from a system may be returned to the same system. Otherwise it goes back to the Central
  Store for reclamation or destruction by a licensed contractor.

## 5. Leak testing

- **Frequency:** systems with 50 kg or more of refrigerant every 3 months (the ACX-350, with 196 kg, is checked at
  each quarterly PPM); 10–50 kg every 6 months; below 10 kg annually.
- **Method:** electronic leak detector with sensitivity of 5 g/year or better; bubble solution to pinpoint.
- **After any repair:** pressure test with oxygen-free nitrogen (ACX-350 high side: 1,600 kPa(g), held 30 minutes
  with no drop after temperature correction). **Never use oxygen or compressed air.** Then evacuate to 500 microns or
  below and hold for 30 minutes with a rise of no more than 250 microns.
- Leaks are repaired within 14 days. A unit that has lost more than 10% of its charge stays off until repaired unless
  the HVAC Supervisor approves continued operation for a service-critical area.

## 6. Charging rules

- Charge **by weight** on a calibrated scale (±10 g). R-410A is always charged as liquid. On the ACX-350, charge
  liquid into the liquid-line charging port in steps of no more than 2 kg.
- **A system that needs refrigerant has a leak.** Find it and record it before or alongside any addition.
- **Never add refrigerant to cure high head pressure without first checking subcooling and coil condition.** High
  discharge pressure with subcooling above 10 K means overcharge — adding refrigerant makes it worse. In summer the
  usual causes are a fouled condenser coil or a failed condenser fan (ALD-ACX350-SM-07 §7.3).
- Any single addition above **5% of the circuit's nameplate charge** (ACX-350: 4.9 kg per circuit) needs HVAC
  Supervisor approval in RFM Connect before charging.
- Where total additions to one asset exceed **10% of its charge in 12 months** (ACX-350: 19.6 kg), a leak
  investigation job is raised automatically.

## 7. Cylinder storage and transport

- **Store** cylinders upright and secured in the shaded, ventilated refrigerant cage at the RFM Central Store, Al Quoz
  Industrial 3. Cylinder temperature must not exceed 50 °C. Keep at least 3 m from oxygen and acetylene.
- Stock cylinders: **RFM-REF-0134** (R-134a, 13.6 kg) and **RFM-REF-0410** (R-410A, 11.3 kg).
- Valve caps on at all times; no damaged or out-of-test cylinders. Disposable cylinders are never refilled.
- Each cylinder carries a Central Store cylinder number on its collar label (for example C134-2611); this number is
  recorded on form RF-01 for every issue, charge and return.
- **Vans:** no more than 2 refrigerant cylinders and 1 recovery cylinder per van, upright in a ventilated rack. Never
  leave cylinders in a parked van in the sun for more than 2 hours — van interiors exceed 60 °C in summer. Cylinders
  return to a shaded store at the end of the day.

## 8. PPE, frostbite and other hazards

- Wear chemical splash goggles and nitrile gauntlets **RFM-PPE-0215** when connecting or disconnecting hoses, and
  long sleeves.
- **Frostbite:** liquid R-134a boils at −26 °C and R-410A at −51 °C at atmospheric pressure, freezing skin on contact.
  First aid: warm the area with lukewarm (not hot) water, do not rub, flush eyes for 15 minutes, and get medical
  attention.
- **Asphyxiation:** refrigerant vapour is heavier than air and collects in basement plant rooms. Ventilate, wear a
  personal gas monitor, and leave immediately if it alarms.
- **Decomposition:** never braze or weld on a charged system — heated refrigerant forms toxic hydrogen fluoride. No
  smoking near refrigerant work.

## 9. Refrigerant log — form RF-01 in RFM Connect

Every addition, recovery, transfer and leak test is recorded on **form RF-01 in RFM Connect on the same day**.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Asset tag and circuit | AMCC-CH-01, circuit 2 |
| Job number | JOB-RFM-25-03894 |
| Date, technician and certificate number | 12 June 2025, Ramesh Kumar, RFM-RH-0522 |
| Refrigerant type | R-410A |
| Activity | charge / recovery / transfer / leak test |
| kg added (0.1 kg) | 2.2 |
| kg recovered (0.1 kg) | 0.0 |
| Cylinder number, net contents before and after | C410-1873, 9.6 kg → 7.4 kg |
| Reason, leak location and repair | flare nut at liquid-line sight glass, re-made |
| Leak test method and result | electronic detector, no leak after repair |
| Supervisor approval | required above 5% of circuit charge |

Central Store reconciles cylinder weights against RF-01 entries every month; any difference above 0.5 kg per
cylinder is investigated. Annual refrigerant additions per asset are reported to the client in the December monthly
FM report.

## 10. Applicable UAE environmental regulations

Raseen FM complies with the applicable UAE federal and local environmental regulations on ozone-depleting
substances and fluorinated refrigerants, including requirements for certified handling, the prohibition of venting,
recovery and reclamation, and record-keeping. Where a client contract or a regulation sets a stricter requirement than
this procedure, the stricter requirement applies. HSE & Environment maintains the legal register and briefs HVAC
supervisors on changes.
