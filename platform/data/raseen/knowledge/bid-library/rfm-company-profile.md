# Raseen Facilities Management LLC — Company Profile 2026

## 1. Company overview

Raseen Facilities Management LLC ("RFM") is the facilities management company of Raseen Group, the family-owned UAE group founded in 1996 by Saeed Abdulrahman Al Falasi. RFM began in 2004 as the FM division of Raseen Group, maintaining the Group's own buildings and warehouses, and was incorporated as a separate limited liability company in 2009 to serve external clients. Today RFM delivers integrated hard and soft FM services to government, education, residential community, commercial and industrial clients across the UAE.

At 30 September 2026 RFM employed **1,096 staff** and managed **46 sites** with a combined area of **1,874,000 m²**.

## 5. Hard services

RFM's own technical workforce delivers planned preventive and reactive maintenance of:

- **HVAC:** air-cooled and water-cooled chillers, chilled-water pumps, AHUs, FCUs, DX, VRF and packaged units, ventilation and extract systems;
- **Electrical:** LV switchboards and distribution, lighting, emergency lighting, standby generators and ATS, earthing and lightning protection;
- **Plumbing and drainage:** water tanks, booster sets, water heaters, drainage, grease traps and lift stations, with water-hygiene and Legionella monitoring;
- **BMS and controls:** operation, alarm monitoring, scheduling and optimisation;
- **Building fabric:** doors, ceilings, partitions, finishes, roofing inspections and minor works.

Fire and life safety systems are maintained, tested and certified through RFM's Civil Defence-approved specialist subcontractor, Safwan Fire Protection Systems LLC, under RFM's supervision.

## 6. Soft services

- **Cleaning:** daily, periodic and deep cleaning, washroom hygiene and consumables, event cleaning, carpet care and glazing cleaning. RFM employs its own cleaning workforce at all sites.
- **Pest control:** integrated pest management delivered through **Gulf Shield Pest Control LLC**, a municipality-approved pest control subcontractor engaged by RFM since 2018, with monthly routine treatments and on-call attendance.
- **Waste management:** collection, segregation at source into general, recyclable and organic streams, and removal by licensed waste carriers, with monthly waste reporting.

## 7. Helpdesk and CAFM

All RFM contracts are supported by the **RFM Care Centre**, a 24/7 bilingual Arabic/English helpdesk reached on a single toll-free number, and by **RFM Connect**, RFM's in-house CAFM platform hosted in a UAE data centre in Dubai, with a technician mobile app and read-only client portal. See RFM-LIB-012 and RFM-LIB-015.

## 8. Quality management (ISO 9001)

RFM operates a quality management system certified to ISO 9001:2015 (certificate QMS-9001-0954, valid to 18 June 2027). Key elements:

- a contract quality plan for every contract, issued within 30 days of service start;
- an annual internal audit programme covering every contract at least once a year, conducted by trained internal auditors from the Quality & Compliance team;
- monthly site quality inspections by contract supervisors using RFM Connect checklists;
- a non-conformance and corrective action procedure with root-cause analysis, tracked to closure in RFM Connect;
- an annual management review chaired by the RFM General Manager.

## 9. Management system certifications

RFM holds ISO 41001:2018, ISO 45001:2018, ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certificates issued by Gulf Assurance Certification Services. Certificate numbers, scopes and expiry dates are maintained in the certificate register (RFM-LIB-002).
