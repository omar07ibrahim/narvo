## 5. Al Ghaf Community Centre — condenser coil cleaning overdue

- The quarterly condenser coil cleaning PPM for chillers CH-01 and CH-02 at Al Ghaf Community Centre fell due on 15 August 2026 and has been **overdue since 15 August 2026**.
- Causes: (1) **access constraints during the events season** — roof access to the chiller area was restricted on event days and the summer midday ban limited the available morning and late-afternoon windows; (2) a **stock-out of coil-cleaning chemical** at the MN-05 depot from mid-August, with the replacement order delayed.
- The overdue coil cleaning is the main contributor to the August and September PPM compliance results.

## 6. Al Ghaf Community Centre — comfort complaints and CH-02 trips

- **23 comfort complaints** were received in August and September 2026 (9 in August, 14 in September), concentrated in the **Multipurpose Hall** (15) and the **Ladies' Fitness** centre (8).
- Chiller **CH-02 recorded 11 high-discharge-pressure trips in September 2026**, all between 13:00 and 16:00 with ambient temperatures above 44 °C. Each trip left CH-01 alone to carry the load until reset.
- Chiller energy consumption in September 2026 was 17% higher than in September 2025.
- Field investigation is recorded under job **JOB-RFM-26-04471** ("CH-02 high discharge pressure trip — Al Ghaf Community Centre"), technician Arun Pillai, supervisor Mohammed Rafiq. Fouled condenser coils are the most likely cause; condenser fan and pressure-switch checks are in progress.
