# Schedule of Insurance Policies — Raseen Facilities Management LLC

## 1. Summary of current policies

All policies are placed with **Al Manara Insurance PJSC** through the Group's broker, **Crestline Insurance Brokers LLC**. Status is as at 6 October 2026.

| Cover | Policy no. | Limit | Period of insurance | Status |

| Public liability | PL-RFM-2025-0447 | AED 10,000,000 per occurrence | 16 Oct 2025 – **15 Oct 2026** | In force — **expires 15 Oct 2026**; renewal under review |

## 2. Public liability insurance

- **Insured:** Raseen Facilities Management LLC
- **Policy:** PL-RFM-2025-0447, Al Manara Insurance PJSC
- **Limit of indemnity:** AED 10,000,000 any one occurrence; products liability AED 10,000,000 in the aggregate
- **Deductible:** AED 25,000 each and every claim (third-party property damage only)
- **Territorial limits:** United Arab Emirates
- **Extensions:** cross liability; contractual liability; principal's indemnity; additional insured endorsements issued per client contract (MNDA is named as additional insured for contract MNDA/CON/2023/019)
- **Period:** 16 October 2025 to 15 October 2026, both days inclusive

## 3. Workmen's compensation and employer's liability

- **Policy:** WC-RFM-2026-0112, Al Manara Insurance PJSC
- **Workmen's compensation:** statutory benefits for all RFM employees as required by UAE labour law, including occupational disease
- **Employer's liability:** AED 5,000,000 any one occurrence
- **Insured headcount declared:** 1,096 employees (declaration updated 30 September 2026)
- **Period:** 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027

## 4. Professional indemnity

- **Policy:** PI-RFM-2026-0093, Al Manara Insurance PJSC
- **Limit:** AED 5,000,000 any one claim and in the aggregate
- **Covers:** advice and design-related services provided by RFM, including condition surveys, lifecycle planning and minor design for works
- **Period:** 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027

## 5. Motor fleet

- **Policy:** MF-RFM-2026-0031, Al Manara Insurance PJSC
- **Cover:** comprehensive, including third-party liability, for 187 vehicles (pick-ups, vans, staff buses and two mobile elevating work platforms registered for road use)
- **Period:** 1 February 2026 to 31 January 2027

## 6. Public liability renewal status (October 2026)

- Policy PL-RFM-2025-0447 expires on **15 October 2026**.
- Crestline Insurance Brokers LLC submitted Al Manara's renewal quotation on 24 September 2026 for a further twelve months at the same AED 10,000,000 limit. The quotation includes a revised premium and a higher deductible proposal for property damage.
- The renewal quotation is **under review by Group Finance**. Renewal has not yet been confirmed and **no renewed certificate has been issued**.
- Until a renewal is bound and Al Manara issues a new certificate, the only public liability certificate available for bids is the current certificate for PL-RFM-2025-0447, which is valid only up to 15 October 2026.
- Action owner: Group Finance (Insurance), with Crestline as broker. Bid teams must check the status with Group Finance before attaching any public liability certificate.
