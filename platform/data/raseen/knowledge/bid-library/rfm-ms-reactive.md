# Method Statement — Reactive Maintenance and Fault Response

Document RFM-OPS-MS-011, revision 5.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This method statement sets out how Raseen FM receives, prioritises, attends, makes safe, rectifies and closes reactive maintenance requests on all contracts. It applies 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, to RFM's own staff and to subcontractors.

## 2. Fault reporting channels

Faults are reported to the **RFM Care Centre** by:

- the single toll-free number, 24/7, in Arabic or English;
- email or the client's read-only portal in RFM Connect (portal users can raise requests);
- RFM technicians and supervisors, who log faults found during PPM or patrols directly on the mobile app;
- BMS alarms, which are reviewed by the resident team or the Care Centre out of hours.

Every request receives a job number (format JOB-RFM-26-NNNNN) and an SMS or email acknowledgement to the requester.

## 3. Priority matrix

| Priority | Definition | Examples | Attend | Make safe | Rectify |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Emergency | Risk to life, health, safety or the environment; loss of a critical service; risk of serious property damage | Total loss of cooling to an occupied hall, flooding, smoke or burning smell, power failure | 30 minutes | 2 hours | 24 hours |
| P2 Urgent | Fault affecting operation or comfort, or likely to escalate | One chiller tripped, AHU failure, blocked public toilet, failed emergency light | 2 hours | — | 24 hours |
| P3 Routine | Limited impact on operations | Dripping tap, single light out, door closer fault | 8 hours | — | 72 hours |
| P4 Planned | Non-urgent, scheduled by agreement | Touch-up painting, signage, furniture adjustment | Planned | — | 10 working days |

The Care Centre agent assigns the priority using the RFM Connect fault-code library; the supervisor may upgrade the priority. Downgrading a P1 requires the Operations Manager's approval.

## 4. Dispatch and attendance

- P1 and P2 jobs are pushed to the nearest available qualified technician on the mobile app and confirmed by phone call from the Care Centre.
- At sites with a resident team, the resident technician attends first; the mobile team from the Ras Al Marsa Industrial depot (MN-05) supports satellite sites in Marsa Al Noor.
- Attendance is recorded when the technician scans the asset or site QR tag on arrival.

## 5. Make safe and rectification

- For P1 faults the first priority is to make the situation safe: isolate, contain, barricade and inform the client's representative on site.
- Temporary measures (for example, portable spot coolers, temporary lighting, redirection of users) are deployed where permanent repair will take longer than the make-safe time.
- Permanent rectification follows within the target time; where parts are not available, the job is placed on hold only with the client's agreement and the reason is recorded.

## 6. Escalation matrix

| Trigger | Escalated to | Action |
|---|---|---|
| P1 not attended within 20 minutes | Shift Supervisor | Re-dispatch, phone follow-up |
| P1 not attended within 30 minutes | Operations Manager | Client informed by phone |
| P1 not made safe within 2 hours | Contract Manager | Additional resources, client update every hour |
| P1 not rectified within 12 hours | Head of Operations | Specialist or OEM support engaged |
| P2 not attended within 2 hours | Shift Supervisor | Re-dispatch |
| Any job on hold more than 5 days | Operations Manager | Weekly review with client |

## 7. Spares and out-of-hours cover

- Critical spares (contactors, fan motors, belts, filters, refrigerant, pump seals, common sensors) are held at the MN-05 depot and in resident site stores.
- An on-call technician for each trade and an on-call supervisor are rostered every night and weekend.
- Specialist subcontractors are contracted with call-out response times consistent with RFM's own priorities.

## 8. Close-out and first-time fix

- Jobs are closed on the mobile app with a description of the work, parts used, time on site and photographs.
- The requester receives a completion notification and may reopen the job within 48 hours.
- **First-time fix** is measured as jobs completed at the first visit without a return visit ÷ all reactive jobs.

## 9. Repeat faults and root-cause analysis

Any asset with three or more reactive faults of the same type within 30 days is flagged in RFM Connect as a repeat fault. The supervisor carries out a root-cause analysis within 5 working days and records corrective actions, which may include changes to the PPM frequency, replacement of components or a recommendation to the client for capital replacement.
