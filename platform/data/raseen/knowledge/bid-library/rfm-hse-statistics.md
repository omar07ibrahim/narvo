## 2. Definitions and basis of the statistics

- Man-hours cover all RFM employees on all contracts and offices; subcontractor hours are recorded separately and excluded.
- **LTI:** a work-related injury resulting in at least one full shift lost after the day of the injury.
- **LTIFR** = number of LTIs × 200,000 ÷ man-hours worked.
- **TRIR** = number of recordable injuries (LTIs, restricted work cases and medical treatment cases) × 200,000 ÷ man-hours worked. RFM introduced recordable-injury classification in RFM Connect's HSE module in January 2025; TRIR is therefore available from 2025 onwards only.

## 3. Lost time injuries 2023 and 2024

- **2023 (1 LTI):** a technician suffered a hand laceration while replacing an AHU fan belt; 9 days lost. Root cause: isolation not verified before work. Action: LOTO "try" step made mandatory and added to the mobile app checklist.
- **2024 (1 LTI):** a cleaner slipped on a wet floor in a washroom and fractured a wrist; 17 days lost. Root cause: wet-floor signage not placed. Action: signage check added to cleaning supervisors' inspection and anti-slip footwear issued to all cleaners.

## 4. HSE management system

RFM's HSE management system is certified to **ISO 45001:2018** by Gulf Assurance Certification Services, certificate **OHS-45001-1183, valid to 30 November 2026**. The recertification audit is booked for 10–12 November 2026. The system includes the HSE policy signed by the Group COO, hazard identification and risk assessment for every contract, method statements for all routine and high-risk activities, monthly site HSE inspections, an annual internal HSE audit of every contract, and HSE induction and refresher training for all staff.

## 5. HSE organisation

The HSE Department is led by **Ravi Shankar, HSE Manager** (NEBOSH IGC, IOSH Managing Safely), who currently covers three contracts. He is supported by HSE officers deployed across the portfolio and by site supervisors trained as HSE champions. HSE performance is reported monthly to the RFM General Manager and quarterly to the Group COO.

## 6. Heat-stress management

- Full compliance with the **summer midday work ban (15 June – 15 September, 12:30–15:00)** for all outdoor and exposed work, including roof-top plant. No breaches were recorded in RFM's internal audits in 2025 or 2026.
- Heat index monitored on every site with outdoor work; work–rest cycles increased when the heat index exceeds set thresholds.
- Cool drinking water, oral rehydration salts and shaded or air-conditioned rest areas provided at all sites.
- Acclimatisation of new arrivals over the first 7 to 14 days, with reduced outdoor exposure.
- "Buddy system" for roof-top work and toolbox talks on heat illness symptoms at the start of each summer.

## 7. Permit-to-work, LOTO and work at height

- **Permit-to-work:** required for hot work, electrical isolation, work at height above 2 m, confined spaces, roof access and work on live chilled-water or fire systems. Permits are issued by authorised supervisors, displayed at the work location and closed out with a site check.
- **Lock-out/tag-out:** personal padlocks and danger tags for every technician, isolation registers, and a "try" step to verify zero energy before work starts.
- **Work at height:** ladders limited to short-duration light work; mobile scaffolds and mobile elevating work platforms operated by certified operators; harnesses and rescue plans for roof edges without permanent protection.

## 8. Emergency response and incident notification

- Every site has an emergency response plan covering fire, power failure, flooding, medical emergency and evacuation, coordinated with the client's own plan. Drills are held at least twice a year.
- All incidents are reported internally to the supervisor immediately and to the HSE Manager within 1 hour.
- **Notification to the client:** RFM notifies the client of incidents involving injury, fire, property damage or service disruption **as soon as practicable, and in any case within 24 hours** of the incident.
- Investigations are completed within 5 working days, with corrective actions tracked in RFM Connect.
