# Method Statement — Energy Monitoring and Management

Document RFM-TS-MS-013, revision 2.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This method statement describes how Raseen FM monitors and reports energy and water consumption at client sites and identifies operational energy savings through sub-metering and BMS optimisation. It applies to all contracts where energy reporting is part of the scope. Utility bills are normally paid by the client; RFM's role is monitoring, reporting and optimising the operation of the plant it maintains.

## 2. Data sources

- **Utility bills:** monthly electricity and water bills provided by the client, entered into RFM Connect by the 10th of the following month.
- **Sub-meters:** where installed, sub-meters on chillers, AHU groups, lighting and small power are read monthly (manually or through the BMS). RFM recommends additional sub-meters where major loads are not separately metered.
- **BMS trend data:** chiller run hours, chilled-water temperatures, AHU schedules and space temperatures, exported monthly.
- **Site information:** operating hours and event calendars from the client.

## 3. Monthly energy reporting

RFM issues a monthly energy and water report for each site as part of the monthly performance report. The report contains:

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Consumption summary | Electricity (kWh) and water (m³) for the month |
| Comparison | Consumption **compared with the same month of the previous year**, in absolute terms and as a percentage change |
| Breakdown | Sub-metered loads where available (cooling, lighting, small power) |
| Intensity | kWh per m² GFA for the month and the rolling 12 months |
| Exceptions | Months with an increase above 5% compared with the same month of the previous year, with likely causes |
| Actions | Optimisation actions taken in the month and proposed for the next month |

Reports are prepared by the MEP/BMS Engineer, Karthik Subramanian, from data in RFM Connect, and reviewed by the Operations Manager before issue.

## 4. Energy conservation measures

RFM focuses on no-cost and low-cost operational measures within the existing plant:

- **BMS time schedules:** aligning AHU, FCU and lighting schedules with actual operating hours and the client's event calendar, and removing overrides left in "hand" mode.
- **Setpoint management:** maintaining agreed space temperature setpoints and preventing over-cooling of low-occupancy areas.
- **Chilled-water optimisation:** chilled-water supply temperature reset during low-load periods and staging of chillers to match load.
- **Lighting:** occupancy controls in toilets and stores, and recommendations for LED retrofits where fittings are at end of life.
- **Maintenance-related savings:** clean filters and coils, correct refrigerant charge and tight building envelope (door closers, seals).

Measures that require capital investment are submitted to the client with an estimated cost and simple payback for approval.

## 5. Results achieved

BMS optimisation and scheduling measures have delivered typical savings of **4–6%** in electricity consumption at two RFM sites:

| Site | Measures | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial office building, Dubai (26,400 m²) | AHU schedule alignment, chilled-water reset, car park lighting controls | 5.7% lower electricity consumption over 12 months compared with the previous 12 months |
| School campus, Sharjah (Khaleej Scholars Education Group) | Holiday and weekend BMS schedules, classroom setpoint control | 4.2% lower electricity consumption over the academic year compared with the previous academic year |

Savings are calculated by comparing metered consumption with the equivalent previous period.

## 6. Water monitoring

Water consumption is reported monthly from utility bills and sub-meters. Sudden increases are investigated as possible leaks; supervisors check toilets, urinal flush valves and tank overflows during routine rounds. Recommendations such as flow restrictors and sensor taps are submitted to clients as proposals.

## 7. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| MEP/BMS Engineer | Data collection, BMS optimisation, monthly energy report |
| Site HVAC Supervisor | Implementation of schedule and setpoint changes, plant logs |
| Operations Manager | Review of reports, client communication, approval of proposals |
| Client facility manager | Provision of bills and event calendars, approval of changes |

## 8. Environmental management link

Energy and water monitoring forms part of RFM's environmental management system certified to ISO 14001:2015 (certificate EMS-14001-0611). Energy use is recorded as a significant environmental aspect in the site environmental aspects registers.
