## 2. Text of the letter

> To Whom It May Concern,
>
> This is to certify that Raseen Facilities Management LLC (trade licence CN-2207815) has been providing facilities management services to the Marsa Al Noor Development Authority under contract MNDA/CON/2023/019, "Facilities Management Services — Community Centres", since 1 January 2024. The contract covers Al Ghaf Community Centre and four satellite community facilities in Marsa Al Noor and includes hard services, cleaning, pest control, waste management and a 24-hour helpdesk. The annual contract value is AED 4,200,000 and the contract term runs until 31 December 2026.
>
> During the 2024 contract year, the performance of Raseen Facilities Management LLC was assessed as **satisfactory**. Planned preventive maintenance compliance for the year was **96.9%**, above the contractual target of 95%. Statutory inspections and certificates for the facilities were kept current, and the contractor supported a full programme of community events, including during the Holy Month of Ramadan and the National Day celebrations, without significant disruption to services.
>
> This letter is issued at the request of Raseen Facilities Management LLC for submission with tenders and does not constitute any commitment or liability on the part of the Marsa Al Noor Development Authority.
>
> Eng. Tariq Mahmoud Saleh
> Director, Facilities & Assets
> Marsa Al Noor Development Authority
