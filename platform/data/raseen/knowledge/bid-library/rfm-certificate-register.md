# Management System Certificate Register

## 1. Purpose and control of this register

This register lists the management system certificates, licences and client registrations held by Raseen Facilities Management LLC (RFM). It is maintained by the Quality & Compliance team, reviewed at the end of every month and is the only approved source for certificate details quoted in bids. Copies of the certificates themselves are held in the bid library folder "02-Certificates" in PDF. Status in this register is as at 30 September 2026.

## 3. Certificate scope statements

- **ISO 41001:2018 (FMS-41001-2207):** "Provision of integrated facilities management services, including hard services, soft services, helpdesk and CAFM-based asset management, to government, education, residential, commercial and industrial clients in the United Arab Emirates." Sites covered: head office (Al Quoz Industrial 3, Dubai), regional depot (Ras Al Marsa Industrial, Marsa Al Noor) and client sites.
- **ISO 45001:2018 (OHS-45001-1183):** "Provision of hard and soft facilities management services, including mechanical, electrical and plumbing maintenance, cleaning, pest control management and helpdesk operations." Same sites as above.
- **ISO 9001:2015 (QMS-9001-0954):** "Provision of facilities management, MEP maintenance and cleaning services."
- **ISO 14001:2015 (EMS-14001-0611):** "Provision of facilities management and cleaning services, including waste segregation and environmental aspects management at client sites."

## 6. Rules for using certificates in bids

1. Attach the current PDF copy of each certificate requested; never attach an expired or superseded certificate.
2. Check each certificate's expiry date against the tender closing date and, where the RFP requires, against the end of the bid validity period.
3. Where a certificate expires before the end of the bid validity period, the Bid Manager must raise it with the Quality & Compliance Manager before submission and record the agreed approach in the bid risk register.
4. Quote certificate numbers, scopes and dates exactly as shown in this register.
5. Do not state or imply certification to any standard not listed in Section 2.
