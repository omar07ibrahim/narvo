# In-Country Value (ICV) Certificate 2026 — Raseen Facilities Management LLC

## 1. Certificate particulars

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Certified entity | Raseen Facilities Management LLC |
| Trade licence | CN-2207815 |
| Certificate number | ICV-2026-0418772 |
| ICV score | **31.4%** |
| Date of issue | 3 February 2026 |
| Valid until | 2 February 2027 |
| Certifying body | Emirates Value Audit Partners |
| Basis | Audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2024 |

The certificate is issued under the national ICV certification methodology for service providers and is accepted by government entities using the national ICV programme, including MNDA.

## 2. ICV score breakdown

| Component | Contribution to ICV score |
|---|---|
| Third-party spend with UAE-based suppliers and subcontractors | 17.9% |
| Workforce cost of employees based in the UAE | 8.6% |
| Investment in UAE fixed assets (depots, vehicles, equipment) | 3.1% |
| Emiratisation | 1.8% |
| **Total ICV score** | **31.4%** |

The largest contributor is local spend: more than 80% of RFM's third-party spend in 2024 was with UAE-based suppliers and subcontractors, including spare parts distributors, cleaning chemical suppliers, the Group's own building materials company and specialist subcontractors such as Gulf Shield Pest Control LLC.

## 3. Validity for tenders

- The certificate was issued on 3 February 2026 and remains valid until 2 February 2027.
- For tenders that require an ICV certificate "issued within the last 12 months", the certificate qualifies for any closing date up to and including 2 February 2027.
- The 2027 certificate will be based on the audited 2025 financial statements. Group Finance plans to submit the 2025 data to Emirates Value Audit Partners in December 2026 so that the renewed certificate is available before 2 February 2027.

## 4. Score history

| Certificate year | Basis (audited FY) | ICV score |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | FY 2022 | 27.9% |
| 2025 | FY 2023 | 29.6% |
| 2026 | FY 2024 | 31.4% |

The improvement since 2024 reflects increased local sourcing of spare parts and consumables and the purchase of new service vehicles and equipment for the Ras Al Marsa Industrial depot (MN-05).

## 5. ICV improvement initiatives 2026–2027

The following company-level initiatives are approved by Raseen Group Finance and are expected to support the ICV score in future certificates:

1. **Local sourcing of HVAC spares:** framework agreements with UAE-based distributors for compressor parts, refrigerants, filters and belts, replacing two overseas suppliers by Q1 2027.
2. **Cleaning chemicals and consumables:** consolidation of cleaning chemical purchases with UAE-based manufacturers and distributors.
3. **Depot investment:** expansion of the Ras Al Marsa Industrial depot stores and workshop (completed June 2026).
4. **UAE-based subcontractors:** preference for UAE-based specialist subcontractors in pest control, fire and life safety, water treatment and façade access work.
5. **Emiratisation:** continued graduate intake and helpdesk recruitment of UAE nationals (see RFM-LIB-005).

## 6. Use in bids

- Attach the certificate PDF issued by Emirates Value Audit Partners, not this summary.
- Quote the score as **31.4%** and the certificate number exactly as shown.
- Do not quote future or target ICV scores as achieved values.
