# Financial Summary 2023–2025 — Raseen Facilities Management LLC

## 1. Basis of preparation and audit

This summary is extracted from the audited financial statements of Raseen Facilities Management LLC for the years ended 31 December 2023, 2024 and 2025. The statements were audited by **Baker Lane & Co. Chartered Accountants**, who issued an unqualified (clean) audit opinion for each of the three years. The 2025 statements were approved by the shareholders on 14 April 2026. Figures are in UAE dirhams (AED); amounts in millions are rounded to one decimal place. Full signed statements are held in the bid library folder "06-Financials" and must be attached to bids in full, not replaced by this summary.

## 2. Revenue and profitability 2023–2025

| Year ended 31 December | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue (AED million) | 212.4 | 238.9 | 261.7 |
| Revenue growth | — | +12.5% | +9.5% |
| Gross profit (AED million) | 30.9 | 35.6 | 40.3 |
| Gross margin | 14.5% | 14.9% | 15.4% |
| Net profit (AED million) | 13.2 | 15.5 | 17.8 |
| Net margin | 6.2% | 6.5% | **6.8%** |

Revenue growth in 2024 and 2025 came mainly from new integrated FM contracts in the education and community sectors, including the MNDA community-centres contract MNDA/CON/2023/019 which started on 1 January 2024.

## 4. Liquidity and financial position

| Indicator | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current ratio | 1.31 | 1.37 | **1.42** |
| Trade receivables days | 84 | 79 | 76 |

RFM has no long-term borrowings. Working capital is supported by Raseen Group treasury, which centrally manages bank facilities for all Group companies, including bank guarantee lines used for bid securities and performance bonds.

## 5. Litigation and contingent liabilities

There is **no pending or threatened litigation** against Raseen Facilities Management LLC, and no contract has been terminated for default in the last five years. Contingent liabilities at 31 December 2025 consisted only of bank guarantees issued in the ordinary course of business (bid securities, performance bonds and advance payment guarantees), as disclosed in note 24 of the 2025 audited financial statements.

## 6. Compliance with typical tender financial thresholds

- **Minimum annual turnover:** RFM's audited revenue exceeded AED 200 million in each of 2023, 2024 and 2025, so any tender threshold of up to AED 200 million per year is met on audited figures.
- **Three years of audited statements:** available for 2023, 2024 and 2025, each with an unqualified opinion from Baker Lane & Co. Chartered Accountants.
- **Profitability and liquidity:** positive net profit in all three years; current ratio above 1.3 in all three years.

## 7. Bid guarantees and bonds

Bid securities and performance bonds for RFM bids are issued by the Group's relationship banks in the UAE through Raseen Group treasury. Requests must reach Group Finance at least five working days before the required issue date, with the beneficiary's guarantee format attached. Group Finance confirms the guarantee wording against the tender form before issue.
