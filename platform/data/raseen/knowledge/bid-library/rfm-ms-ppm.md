# Method Statement — Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM)

Document RFM-OPS-MS-010, revision 4.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This method statement describes how Raseen FM plans, executes, records and verifies planned preventive maintenance (PPM) for building services and fabric on all RFM contracts. It applies to RFM's own technicians and to specialist subcontractors working under RFM supervision.

## 2. PPM standard and task library

RFM's PPM task library is **aligned with SFG20**, the recognised maintenance specification for building engineering services. Each SFG20-aligned schedule is held in RFM Connect with:

- the task steps and checklist;
- the frequency and the scheduling window;
- the skill set required and estimated duration;
- whether the task is statutory (legal or insurance requirement) or non-statutory.

Where the manufacturer's recommendations are more demanding than the SFG20 frequency, the manufacturer's frequency is adopted. Frequencies are reviewed annually against asset condition and fault history.

## 3. Planning and scheduling

- The annual PPM plan for each site is generated in RFM Connect from the verified asset register during mobilisation and issued to the client for information within 30 days of service start.
- Work orders are released automatically each week and assigned to technicians by trade and site.
- Tasks are scheduled outside operating hours or event times where they would disturb users, in agreement with the client's facility manager.
- During the summer midday work ban (15 June – 15 September, 12:30–15:00), roof-top and external PPM is scheduled in the morning or after 15:00.

## 5. Chiller maintenance regime

For air-cooled chillers, RFM's standard regime is:

- **daily logs** of suction and discharge pressures, chilled-water supply and return temperatures and compressor amps, recorded in RFM Connect on resident sites;
- **quarterly condenser coil cleaning** using approved coil-cleaning chemicals and low-pressure water rinsing, with before-and-after photographs;
- a **pre-summer readiness check each May**, covering coil condition, condenser fan operation, refrigerant charge, high-pressure cut-out settings and control calibration, so that chillers enter the summer in good condition;
- an **annual major service** with oil analysis and compressor checks, carried out in the cooler months (November–March).

Chiller readings outside normal ranges raise an alert to the HVAC Supervisor for review.

## 6. Execution and evidence

- Technicians open each PPM work order on the RFM Connect mobile app by scanning the QR tag on the asset, which confirms that the technician is at the correct asset.
- Checklists are completed on the app; readings outside limits automatically create a follow-up reactive work order.
- Photographs are mandatory for coil cleaning, filter replacement, water tank cleaning and any defect found.
- Permit-to-work and LOTO apply to all PPM on electrical and rotating plant.

## 7. PPM compliance measurement and deferral

- **PPM compliance** = PPM work orders completed within their scheduling window ÷ PPM work orders due in the month × 100.
- Scheduling windows: weekly tasks ±1 day; monthly tasks ±5 days; quarterly and longer tasks ±10 days.
- A PPM task may be deferred only with the approval of the client's facility manager, recorded in RFM Connect with the reason and the new date. Deferred tasks still count as non-compliant in the month they fall due.
- Overdue PPM is reviewed weekly by the Operations Manager.

## 8. Quality verification

- Supervisors re-inspect at least 10% of completed PPM tasks each month, selected at random by RFM Connect.
- Results of supervisor checks are part of the monthly site quality inspection under the ISO 9001 quality management system.
- PPM compliance and overdue tasks are reported to the client in the monthly performance report.
