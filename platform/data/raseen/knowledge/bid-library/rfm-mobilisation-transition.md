# Standard Mobilisation and Transition Plan (30 Days)

Document RFM-OPS-PL-016, revision 3.

## 1. Objectives of mobilisation

RFM's standard mobilisation plan brings a new contract to full service delivery within **30 calendar days** of the mobilisation start (normally the letter of award). Its objectives are:

- safe and uninterrupted continuity of services from the first day;
- verified assets, records and spares taken over from the outgoing contractor or the client;
- staff recruited, inducted and trained for the specific sites;
- RFM Connect configured, with the client's portal access live;
- agreed baselines for KPIs, reporting and communication.

The plan is tailored for each contract and issued to the client within 7 days of award.

## 2. Mobilisation team

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Mobilisation Lead (normally the proposed Contract Manager) | Overall plan, client meetings, weekly progress report |
| Operations Manager | Staffing, rosters, subcontractors, site set-up |
| HVAC Supervisor and trade supervisors | Asset verification, condition of critical plant, spares |
| CAFM Coordinator (Digital & CAFM team) | Data migration, QR tagging, PPM plans, portal set-up |
| HSE Manager | Site risk assessments, emergency plans, inductions |
| HR Business Partner | Recruitment, transfers, visas and onboarding |

## 3. Thirty-day mobilisation programme

| Period | Key activities |
|---|---|
| Days 1–7 | Kick-off meeting with client; mobilisation plan issued; receipt of asset register, drawings, O&M manuals and statutory certificates; site risk assessments; recruitment and transfer of staff started; subcontractors confirmed |
| Days 8–14 | Joint site walk-downs with the client and outgoing contractor; asset verification of critical plant (chillers, generators, fire systems, LV boards, pumps); QR tagging started; inventory of spares and tools; site offices and stores set up |
| Days 15–21 | Asset data loaded into RFM Connect; PPM plans generated from the SFG20-aligned library; helpdesk scripts and fault codes configured; staff inductions and site-specific training; emergency plans issued |
| Days 22–30 | Shadowing of outgoing contractor where possible; client portal access issued; KPI baseline and reporting format agreed; remaining asset verification and tagging completed; mobilisation completion review with client |

## 4. Asset verification

- The client's asset register is imported into RFM Connect, or created from site surveys where no register exists. Registers in COBie spreadsheet format can be imported directly.
- Supervisors verify each asset on site: existence, location, identification details, and a visual condition grade on a 1–5 scale for critical assets.
- Discrepancies (missing, additional or mis-described assets) are recorded in a verification log and agreed with the client.
- QR tags are fixed to all maintainable assets. Critical assets are tagged first.
- Defects found during verification are reported to the client in a "Day-30 condition report", separating defects existing at handover from new defects.

## 5. Staff transfer and recruitment

- Where appropriate and agreed, suitable staff of the outgoing contractor are offered employment, subject to RFM vetting and competency checks.
- Remaining roles are filled from RFM's existing workforce and recruitment pipeline.
- All staff complete RFM's general HSE induction and a site-specific induction before working on site.

## 6. CAFM set-up and data migration

- Site hierarchy, assets, PPM plans, SLA rules and escalation contacts configured in RFM Connect.
- Historical work orders from the outgoing contractor imported where provided in a usable format.
- Client users trained on the portal; dashboards configured.

## 7. Handover from the outgoing contractor

RFM requests the following at the start of mobilisation: asset register, PPM history for the last 12 months, open work orders, statutory certificates and test records, O&M manuals and as-built drawings, spares inventory, keys and access cards, and a list of known defects. Items not received are logged as client dependencies and reported weekly.

## 8. Mobilisation risks and mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Incomplete handover information | Early request, joint walk-downs, dependency log reported weekly |
| Staff shortages at start | Temporary deployment from other RFM contracts and the MN-05 depot mobile team |
| Critical plant in poor condition | Day-30 condition report; temporary measures agreed with client |
| Summer start (high cooling load) | Priority verification of chillers and AHUs in the first week |

## 9. Mobilisation completion criteria

Mobilisation is complete when: all staff are deployed and inducted; RFM Connect is live with verified critical assets and PPM plans; the helpdesk is taking calls for all sites; the client portal is active; and the client has signed the mobilisation completion checklist.
