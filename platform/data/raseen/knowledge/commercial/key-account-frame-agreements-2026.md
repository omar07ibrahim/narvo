# Key-Account Frame Agreements 2026 — contract price lists (Q4 2026 rates)

RBM supplies its six key contractor accounts under annual frame agreements. Contract rates are in AED per unit,
**excluding 5% VAT**. Rebar rates are **indexed quarterly** to the Sahara Rebar Mills base price; Q4 2026 rates apply to
deliveries from **1 October to 31 December 2026**. All other rates are fixed for the agreement year.

General rules common to all frame agreements:

1. The frame rate applies to every order and project quotation for the account during the validity period.
2. A project-specific quotation **must not exceed** the frame rate unless the customer accepts it in writing.
3. Prices below the frame rate require written approval of the Sales Director (Fadi Khoury); below standard cost +4%
   requires the General Manager, RBM (Tariq Nasser).
4. Payment terms beyond the account's credit tier require Group CFO approval (see RBM-FIN-POL-04).
5. Retention, liquidated damages or fixed-price-for-project clauses are **not** part of any frame agreement and need
   a separate contract amendment signed by the Sales Director.
