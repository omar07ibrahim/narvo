# Credit & Collections Policy — trade accounts (RBM-FIN-POL-04 rev. 6)

Effective 1 March 2026. Applies to all Raseen Building Materials trade accounts (~1,850 active accounts).

## Credit tiers and payment terms

| Tier | Typical credit limit | Maximum standard payment terms |
|---|---|---|
| A — key accounts | AED 1,500,000 and above | 60 days from invoice date |
| B | AED 500,000 – 1,499,999 | 45 days from invoice date |
| C | below AED 500,000 | 30 days from invoice date |

Payment terms longer than the tier maximum (for example **90 days for a Tier A account**) may only be granted by the
**Group CFO (Priya Raghavan)** and only with credit-insurance cover or an on-demand bank guarantee for the
incremental exposure. KAMs must not offer or imply extended terms before that approval.

## Exposure and automatic credit hold

**Credit exposure = AR outstanding + open sales orders not yet invoiced (including 5% VAT).**
The ERP places a sales order on **automatic credit hold** when accepting it would take exposure above the approved
credit limit. Accounts with any invoice more than 60 days overdue are blocked for new orders.

## Temporary credit release

- A temporary release above the approved limit requires **Group CFO approval** (no delegation).
- Maximum release: **15% above the approved limit**, valid for **30 days**.
- Conditions: no invoice more than 60 days overdue; a dated payment commitment from the customer for overdue amounts;
  the Credit Controller (Nadia Rahimi) records the release reference on the order.
- Partial release is preferred: release only the portion of the order needed to keep the site working.

## Limit reviews

Limits are reviewed annually and whenever a single opportunity would exceed 50% of the current limit. A request
for a permanent increase needs: 2 years of audited financials, aged AR, a trade reference, and Sales Director
endorsement. Tier A increases above AED 3,000,000 go to the Group CEO.

## Collections

Statements are sent on the 1st working day of each month. Reminders at +7 and +21 days overdue; KAM call at +30;
order block at +60; legal referral at +120 days.

## ملخص بالعربية

يُعمل بهذه السياسة اعتباراً من 1 مارس 2026. الحد الأقصى لشروط السداد: 60 يوماً لحسابات الفئة (أ)، و45 يوماً للفئة (ب)،
و30 يوماً للفئة (ج) من تاريخ الفاتورة. لا يجوز منح شروط سداد أطول من الحد الأقصى للفئة (مثل 90 يوماً) إلا بموافقة
المدير المالي للمجموعة، مع تغطية تأمين ائتماني أو ضمان بنكي. يُحتسب التعرض الائتماني بجمع الذمم المدينة القائمة
وأوامر البيع المفتوحة غير المفوترة شاملة ضريبة القيمة المضافة 5%، ويُوقف أمر البيع تلقائياً إذا تجاوز التعرض الحد
المعتمد. الإفراج المؤقت عن الائتمان يتطلب موافقة المدير المالي للمجموعة، بحد أقصى 15% فوق الحد المعتمد ولمدة 30 يوماً.
