# Order Desk Pricing & Order-Exception Procedure (RBM-COM-PRC-07 rev. 2)

Effective 1 September 2026. Applies to every sales order entered for Raseen Building Materials Trading LLC.
Read together with the frame agreements (RBM-COM-FA-2026), the Credit & Collections Policy (RBM-FIN-POL-04) and
the Delivery & Site-Access Policy (RBM-OPS-POL-11).

## Reference price for an order line

- **Frame-agreement accounts** (Bin Hilal, Gulf Crest, APCC, Al Marsa Engineering, Emerald Line, Saqr Al Noor): the
  frame rate for the delivery quarter. Rebar is indexed quarterly — Q4 2026 rates apply to all deliveries from
  1 October 2026, including orders placed earlier. Other frame items are fixed for the agreement year.
- **All other accounts:** price list PL-2026-Q4 (effective 1 October 2026). The order desk may give a discount off
  list without further approval of up to **5.0% for Tier A, 3.5% for Tier B and 2.0% for Tier C** accounts.
- A deeper discount needs the written approval of the Sales Director; a price below **standard cost + 4%** needs the
  General Manager, RBM.
- The customer's PO price is never the reference. An order entered at a PO price that differs from the reference
  price is a **price exception** and must be corrected — or formally approved — before the delivery note and
  invoice are issued. A price above the frame rate needs the customer's written acceptance (frame agreement rule 2).

## Exception types, owners and approvers

| Exception | Detected when | Works it | Approves the resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock shortfall | Available-to-promise at the ship-from warehouse is below the open quantity, allocating by promise date | Supply planner | Supply Chain & Planning Manager (Joseph Abraham) |
| Price mismatch | Entered price differs from the reference price above | Key Account Manager | Sales Director if the final price is below reference |
| Delivery-window conflict | Requested day/time is outside the site window, or the gate permit lead time cannot be met | Dispatch (Marwan Qasim) | Supply Chain & Planning Manager |
| Credit exposure over limit | AR + open orders incl. 5% VAT exceed the approved limit (automatic credit hold) | Credit Controller (Nadia Rahimi) | Group CFO (Priya Raghavan) for any temporary release |

During the Sahara Rebar Mills shutdown (5–19 October 2026) rebar is allocated **order by order in promise-date
sequence**; no planner may re-allocate stock already promised to an earlier order without the Supply Chain &
Planning Manager's approval.

## Resolution options

Every resolution put forward for approval must show, per option:

1. the delivery promise **per order line** (split lines get their own promise date);
2. extra cost (transfer at AED 38/t, alternative-source premium) and the **margin impact against standard cost**;
3. the customer's **credit exposure after** the change and whether a temporary release is needed;
4. the approvals required.

Preferred order of remedies for a rebar shortfall: (a) split delivery from available stock with the balance from
confirmed inbound purchase orders; (b) stock transfer Jebel Ali → MN-05; (c) alternative approved source. A split
needs the customer's written agreement (e-mail or WhatsApp from an authorised site or procurement contact is
accepted).

## Handover to billing and customer notice

- After approval the planner sends a **billing handover note** to Group Finance (billing): corrected unit prices,
  split lines with their promise dates, invoicing per delivery, and any credit-release reference.
- The customer is informed **before the original promise date** with the revised date per line and the reason
  (stock, site access, price, credit), in the customer's preferred language and channel.
