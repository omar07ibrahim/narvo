## Supply notices

### Sahara Rebar Mills — planned maintenance 5–19 October 2026

Notice SRM/COM/2026/114 dated 15 September 2026: the rolling mill and reheating furnace stop for planned maintenance
from **5 to 19 October 2026**. No dispatches during the shutdown; dispatches resume **20 October 2026** with priority
for confirmed purchase orders. RBM pre-bought 1,150 t in September to cover the gap; planners must allocate remaining
16 mm stock order by order.

### Northern Emirates Cement — energy surcharge proposal

Letter NEC/SALES/2026/208 (28 Aug 2026) proposed an "energy & fuel surcharge" of AED 1.20 per bag from 1 September 2026.
**RBM has not accepted the surcharge**; the supply contract price remains AED 14.20 per bag (OPC) and AED 16.35 (SRC)
until 31 December 2026. Negotiation owner: Joseph Abraham.

### Andalus Ceramics — price revision

Andalus quotation AQ-26-0412 (porcelain 60×120 at AED 46.80/m²) expired 31 August 2026. A new quotation at AED 49.60/m²
was received on 10 September and is **under review — not accepted**. Open purchase orders placed before acceptance stay
at the PO price.

### Alternative steel source

Al Dhafra Steel Trading LLC (spot importer, TRN 100583027400003) can supply B500B 16 mm ex-stock Mussafah at roughly
**AED 165/t above Sahara's price**, available within 3 working days. Use only with the Supply Chain & Planning
Manager's approval.
