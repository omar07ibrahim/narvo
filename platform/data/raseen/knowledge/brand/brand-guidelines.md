# Raseen Brand & Content Guidelines v3.1

Applies to every external text produced for Raseen Group companies — web, social, WhatsApp broadcasts, brochures,
emails to customers and tender documents. Owner: Hana Yousef, Marketing & Brand Manager. Every launch asset is
released only after her approval.

## Voice

- **Clear, technical, confident — never hype.** Write like an experienced site engineer explaining to a peer.
- Lead with the customer's problem (heat load on roofs, site downtime, cost certainty), then the evidence.
- Every performance number carries its **test basis** the first time it appears in an asset
  (e.g. "up to 18 °C cooler roof surface — lab report RBM-LAB-26-112, 46 °C ambient").
- Prefer short sentences. No exclamation marks in web, brochure or email copy; at most one in social posts.
- No superlatives we cannot prove: avoid "best", "No. 1", "unbeatable", "revolutionary", "guaranteed savings".
- Never name or disparage competitors.

## Arabic style

- Modern Standard Arabic for web, brochures and emails; a lighter, Gulf-friendly register is acceptable for WhatsApp
  and Instagram (e.g. "لسطح أبرد في الصيف"), but never slang in technical claims.
- Write Arabic natively — do not translate English word for word. Keep sentence rhythm natural.
- Use Latin digits for numbers and units (0.88، 18 °C، 20 لتر) so specifications read identically in both languages.
- Product names: **رصين ثيرماشيلد** (Raseen ThermaShield). On first mention in Arabic: "رصين ثيرماشيلد (Raseen
  ThermaShield)".

## Forbidden and restricted claims

Forbidden in every channel and language:

1. "Eliminates the need for air conditioning" / "no more AC" / "بدون مكيف" — or any implication that cooling is no
   longer required.
2. "100% leak-proof", "waterproof forever", "مقاوم للتسرب 100%".
3. Any government certification, approval or endorsement ("approved by the municipality", "government certified",
   "معتمد من الجهات الحكومية") — ThermaShield holds **no** government certification.
4. Energy or bill savings expressed as a fixed percentage or amount ("cuts your bill by 40%") — no approved savings
   figure exists.
5. Health claims of any kind.

Restricted (allowed only with the qualifier):

- Temperature reduction → "**up to** 18 °C cooler roof surface" + test basis. Never "18 °C cooler rooms/indoors".
- Warranty → "10-year warranty **when applied by a Raseen-certified applicator**".
- Price → "AED 385 per 20 L pail (excl. VAT)" — never "from AED …" or discounts not approved by Sales.

## Channel rules

| Channel | Length | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Web product page | 120–220 words | Headline ≤ 60 characters; specification list; test basis; CTA "Request a technical visit" |
| LinkedIn post | 80–160 words | Professional; max 3 hashtags; no emoji |
| Instagram caption | 40–90 words | Max 2 emoji; max 5 hashtags incl. #RaseenThermaShield |
| WhatsApp broadcast | 30–70 words | Opt-in lists only; one CTA; no links other than raseen.ae |
| Trade brochure blurb | 60–110 words | Third person; specification-led |
| Contractor email | 120–200 words | Subject ≤ 70 characters; personal greeting; one CTA; sign-off from the KAM |

Mandatory hashtags where hashtags are used: **#RaseenThermaShield** and **#رصين**.
