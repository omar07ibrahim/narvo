# Delivery & Site-Access Policy (RBM-OPS-POL-11 rev. 3)

Effective 20 July 2026, updated after Circular 14/2026 tightened controls in gated communities of Marsa Al Noor.

## Delivery promise

- Stock items: delivery within **48 hours** of a credit-cleared order, inside the site's delivery window.
- A delivery promise is given per order line and recorded in the ERP. **Promise accuracy** = share of order lines
  delivered complete on or before the promised date. Group target: 96%.
- If a line cannot be delivered as promised, the customer is informed **before** the promise date with a revised
  date and the reason (stock, site access, credit).

## Site windows and gate permits

| Zone | Days | Window | Gate / permit | Vehicle limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MN-08 Marina Quarter | Mon–Sat | **09:00–16:00** | Marina Quarter Properties LLC gate permit, booked **48 h** ahead | 12 m flatbed |
| MN-03 Al Lulu Island | Mon–Fri | 08:00–17:00 | Lulu Island Developments PJSC gate pass, 24 h ahead | 12 m flatbed |
| MN-06 Al Sidr Residential | Mon–Sat | 08:00–18:00 | Sidr Communities LLC contractor pass | Any |
| Other Marsa Al Noor districts | Mon–Sat | 07:00–18:00 | — | Any |
| Dubai sites | Mon–Sat | 07:00–18:00 | Per main contractor | City-centre truck restrictions 06:30–09:00 and 17:00–20:00 |

No deliveries into gated communities on Sundays or public holidays. Requests outside a window are re-scheduled to
the next available slot; the planner must not confirm an out-of-window time to the customer.

Summer midday work ban (15 June – 15 September, 12:30–15:00): no manual off-loading in the open.

## Loads, transfers and splits

- Minimum rebar drop **10 t**; trailer capacity **25 t**. Cement: 600 bags per 30 t truck.
- Stock transfer Jebel Ali → MN-05: **1 working day**, internal cost **AED 38 per tonne**.
- Split deliveries are allowed when the customer agrees in writing; each split line gets its own promise date.
- Alternative-source steel may be bought only from approved suppliers and needs the Supply Chain & Planning
  Manager's approval; the price difference is recorded against the order margin.

## ملخص بالعربية

يُعمل بهذه السياسة اعتباراً من 20 يوليو 2026. التسليم إلى حي المارينا (MN-08) من الاثنين إلى السبت بين الساعة
09:00 و16:00 فقط، مع تصريح دخول من شركة مارينا كوارتر العقارية يُحجز قبل 48 ساعة، ولا تسليم أيام الأحد أو العطل
الرسمية داخل المجتمعات المسوّرة. الحد الأدنى لتسليم حديد التسليح 10 أطنان، وسعة الشاحنة 25 طناً. نقل المخزون من
جبل علي إلى رأس المرسى الصناعية يستغرق يوم عمل واحداً بتكلفة 38 درهماً للطن. يجب إبلاغ المتعامل بأي تغيير في
موعد التسليم قبل حلول الموعد المتفق عليه مع ذكر السبب.
